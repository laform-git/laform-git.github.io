Lesson 1: What is accessibility?

Story

Hi! Today you are going to learn a new word: accessibility.

It is a long word, but it means something easy: everyone can use things, each in their own way.

You get to know the world with your ears and your hands. That is a superpower!

Your iPhone uses your superpower. When you touch the screen, it tells you out loud what is there.

And if you talk to it, it listens. You can ask it to call Mom or to tell you the time.

Game

I tell you about a thing. You decide: does it help everyone use it?

Your challenge for today

Look around your home for something with a special sound or texture. It could be a raised button, an alarm or a toy.

Tell Mom or Dad what you found!
