# Key Names & Sources Cited

| Person | Role in the argument |
|---|---|
| Charles Darwin | Proposed natural selection explains apparent design away |
| Francis Crick | Co-discovered DNA structure; later proposed "directed panspermia" |
| Claude Shannon | Originated mathematical (probabilistic) information theory |
| Henry Quastler | "Creation of information is habitually associated with conscious activity" |
| William Dembski | Formalized the complexity + specification design-detection criteria (*The Design Inference*) |
| Paul Davies / Fred Hoyle | Physicists on the "impression of design" in fine-tuning |
| Lawrence Krauss / Leonard Susskind | Propose multiverse instead of a fine-tuner |
| Elliott Sober | Naturalist critic — but argues *if* design is accepted, transcendent > extraterrestrial designer |

**Meyer's related books:** *Signature in the Cell* (2009) · *Darwin's Doubt* (2013) · *Return of the God Hypothesis* (2021)
