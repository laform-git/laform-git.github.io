# One-Paragraph Takeaway

Two independent scientific discoveries — the digital, functionally-specified information encoded in DNA, and the fine-tuning of the physical constants of the universe — each exhibit the joint signature (high complexity + specification) that in every other context licenses an inference to intelligent design. Materialist alternatives (natural selection for biology, multiverse for cosmology) either presuppose what they need to explain or push the explanatory problem back a step without resolving it. Taken together, and combined with the universe's own beginning in time, the evidence points beyond an intelligence *within* the cosmos toward a transcendent designing intelligence — the classical conception of God.
