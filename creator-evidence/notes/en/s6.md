# One-Paragraph Takeaway

Two independent scientific discoveries stand at the center of Meyer's case: the digital, functionally specified information written in DNA, and the fine-tuning of the physical constants of the universe. Each shows the same double signature (high complexity + specification) that in every other setting leads us to infer a mind at work. The materialist alternatives (natural selection for biology, the multiverse for cosmology) either assume what they need to explain or push the problem back one step without solving it. Taken together, and added to the fact that the universe itself began in time, the evidence points past any intelligence *within* the cosmos toward a transcendent designing intelligence: the classical conception of God.

**Meyer's conclusion: two clues, one in the cell and one in the stars, point the same way, to a mind that came before matter.**
