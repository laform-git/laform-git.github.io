Phase 2 block (to paste in the MASTER CHAT)
ELLE BAKEHOUSE · PHASE 2

Save this block to your Notion page, in HOW IT WORKS, under the heading PHASE 2. From now on, when I say "Turn on upgrade N":
1. Explain in two lines what will change and what I have to do.
2. Create whatever is missing in Notion, Google Drive or Google Calendar.
3. Update the HOW IT WORKS section of the pages of the chats that change and show me the change.

Turn on one upgrade at a time and in order. If I ask for one without having done the previous one, tell me.

UPGRADES
1. CATALOG: Catalog database in Notion (Product, Size, Price, Photo, Recipe). Ask me for the photos from my album, one per product. From then on, ORDERS uses the catalog photo and price on each Trello card and warns if an order doesn't match the catalog.
2. RECIPES: in RECIPES, take my recipes from Apple Notes (I paste them or send you the exported file) and save them in the Recipes database with category, yield, ingredients and allergens. Link each recipe to its product in the Catalog.
3. COSTS: with Recipes and Ingredients, work out the cost and profit of each Catalog product. When PANTRY changes a price, RECIPES updates the costs. If a product earns little, tell me.
4. WEEKLY REPORT: when I type "Weekly report" in the MASTER CHAT, create a Google Doc in the Weekly reports folder with: orders, sales, best-selling product, estimated profit and shopping list. End with a question to improve.
5. MESSAGES: in ORDERS, after recording an order, give me the confirmation message ready to copy. When the order moves to Delivery, give me the "your order is ready" one. Use my tone: ask me how I say hello and goodbye.
6. CALENDAR: in ORDERS, put each delivery on my Google Calendar with the customer, the product and a reminder the day before. If the date changes, update the event.
7. CUSTOMERS: Customers database in Notion (Name, Instagram, Orders, Favorite product, Special date). ORDERS fills it with each order. SHOWCASE uses it to suggest promotions and special-date reminders.
Before you start
  • Message limit: 🟡 Claude Pro has a usage cap per period. Phase 2 does more work per order, so if you sell a lot you could hit it. If that happens, wait for it to reset or consider a bigger plan.
  • Google Calendar: upgrade 6 needs it connected in Settings → Connectors.
  • Recipes in Apple Notes: there's no direct connection. If there are few, copy and paste them. If there are many, export them all from a Mac and send the file.
  • Costs: they are estimates based on the prices you give. Check them the first time.
