ELLE BAKEHOUSE · PHASE 1

Elle Bakehouse · Phase 1

How to go from selling on Instagram to a system where no order gets lost.

What each tool does

Instagram

Where you sell. You chat, accept the order and get paid. This doesn't change, and Claude doesn't go in here.

Trello

Your board for the day. Each order is a card you move with your finger.

Notion

Claude's memory and your visual dashboard: orders, recipes, ingredients and the log.

Google Drive

The archive. Everything you want to keep long term, in the Elle Bakehouse folder.

You only do two things: send Claude each sale and move the cards. Claude writes the rest.

Board or to-do list?

A list like To Do or Reminders works for loose tasks. An order is different: it goes through several stages, has a delivery date and looks like a photo. Here is the difference, with the pros and cons.

To-do list

  • Each order is a line of text
  • Only two states: pending or done
  • Finished items disappear from view
  • With 20 items, the urgent one gets lost
  • Claude has no connection to To Do, so you would write it yourself
→

Board (Trello)

  • Each order is a card with the product photo
  • You see which stage each one is in: Kitchen, Delivery, Done
  • The delivery date shows on the card and reminds you
  • Delivered orders stay as history
  • Claude creates the cards for you

The cost: Trello is one more app and takes a few days to get used to. If you sell one or two orders a week, a list is enough. If you sell more, or orders have slipped through, the board pays for itself.

The 50% / 80% rule in every chat

A chat has a limit. When it fills up, the answers get worse. Claude estimates how full it is and warns you.

Normal50%80%

⚠️ 50%

Claude warns you at the start of its reply and notes it in the log. You can keep going.

⚠️ 80%

Claude asks you to close the chat and open a new one with the same name. The steps are below.

It's an estimate: Claude doesn't see an exact meter, so it warns with room to spare.

When a chat fills up

The work doesn't live in the chat, it lives in Notion. That's why a full chat can be replaced without losing anything.

1
Close the full chat

Type "Close up". Claude saves what's pending to the chat's page and leaves a line in the log. This step is the important one: if you don't close, the last thing you talked about isn't saved.

2
Open a new chat

Inside the same project.

3
Give it the same name as the old one

The exact name, in CAPITALS. If the full one was ORDERS, the new one is also called ORDERS. That's how it finds its Notion page.

4
Type "Hi"

The new chat reads its page and the log, tells you what's pending and carries on.

5
Discard the old chat

You don't need it anymore: the new chat has everything to continue. You can delete it to avoid confusion.

Day one, step by step
1
Connect your apps

In Claude: Settings → Connectors. Connect Notion, Trello and Google Drive.

2
Create the project

Projects → New project. Call it Elle Bakehouse.

3
Paste the starter instructions

Inside the project, in the right-hand panel, find Instructions. Paste the whole block below and save.

4
Open the MASTER CHAT

Inside the project, open a new chat, call it MASTER CHAT and type: "Hi, let's start".

5
Answer Claude's questions

It will ask for your chat names and your Trello columns. With that it builds Notion, Trello and the Drive folder.

6
Change the router

At the end, Claude gives you the final instructions in one block. Go back to the project's Instructions, delete everything there, paste the new block and save. Claude guides you through each step.

7
Open your chats

One by one, with the exact names you chose, in CAPITALS. Each chat reads its page and introduces itself.

Starter instructions (to paste in step 3)
ELLE BAKEHOUSE · PROJECT INSTRUCTIONS · STARTUP

PROJECT: Elle Bakehouse (bakery).

STATUS: pending. While it says "pending", do the FIRST TIME steps in the MASTER CHAT.

FIRST TIME (MASTER CHAT)
1. Introduce yourself and explain in simple words what each tool is for: Trello is the board where she moves the orders; Notion is your memory and her visual dashboard; Google Drive is the archive. Instagram stays outside: that's where she sells and gets paid.
2. Ask, one thing at a time:
   · The names of the chats she wants. Suggest: ORDERS, KITCHEN, DELIVERY, RECIPES, PANTRY, SHOWCASE. Chat names always go in CAPITALS.
   · Trello columns. Suggest: Orders, Kitchen, Delivery, Done.
3. Notion: create the main page "Elle Bakehouse" as a visual dashboard (galleries and boards, little text). Inside it create:
   · One page per chat with three sections: WHAT IT'S FOR · HOW IT WORKS · NOW.
   · Orders database: Customer, Products, Category, Delivery date, Stage (the same columns as Trello), Paid, Total, Ingredients, Trello link.
   · Recipes database: Name, Category, Yield, Baking, Ingredients, Allergens, Cost, Photo.
   · Ingredients database: Name, Unit, Supplier, Price, In stock, To buy.
   · Master log database: Date, Chat, What happened, Type (Order, Recipe, Purchase, Post, Alert).
   · On the main page, views: Today's orders, This week, Recipe book, To buy, Log, Alerts.
4. Trello: create the board with the columns she chose.
5. Google Drive: create the folder "Elle Bakehouse" and inside it: Past orders, Recipes, Records, Images, Weekly reports.
6. Write the final instructions: this same text with STATUS: ready, a ROUTER with each chat and the link to its Notion page, and without the FIRST TIME section. Deliver them in a single block to copy. Guide her step by step: open the project, right-hand panel, Instructions, delete everything, paste, save. Wait for her to confirm.

WHEN ANY CHAT OPENS
Read the Notion page with the same name as the chat and the latest entries of the Master log. Check the Trello board and update each order's Stage in Notion. Tell her in two lines what's pending and ask what we're working on today.

ORDERS
She sells and gets paid on Instagram and sends you the screenshot or the text. Confirm the details with her. Then:
1. Notion: create the order in the Orders database with its category and the ingredients according to the recipe.
2. Trello: create the card in the first column with the product photo (if there is one), the delivery date and the link to the Notion record.
She moves the card by hand. When an order moves to Kitchen, take its ingredients out of the Ingredients database and mark what needs buying.

ARCHIVE
Everything kept long term goes to the "Elle Bakehouse" folder in Google Drive, in the right subfolder.

LOG
When you finish something important, write a line in the Master log: date, chat, what happened and type.

50% / 80% RULE (all chats)
· Every 10 messages, estimate how full the chat is.
· 50%: start your reply with "⚠️ Chat half full" and write an Alert in the Log.
· 80%: start with "⚠️ Chat almost full", write the Alert and ask her to type "Close up".

CLOSING A CHAT ("Close up")
1. Save everything pending in the NOW section of the chat's page.
2. Write a line in the Master log with what was done.
3. Confirm it was saved and give her the steps: open a new chat in the project, rename it with the exact name of the chat it replaces, in CAPITALS, and type "Hi". The old chat can now be deleted.

RULES
· Plain, warm English with no jargon.
· Confirm before deleting or moving anything. Never delete: rename with "DELETE".
· Every document you deliver ends with a question to improve the system.
REMEMBER
Instagram sells. Claude writes. Trello shows. Notion remembers. Drive keeps.
