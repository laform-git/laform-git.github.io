# MarianAntiphons

The four seasonal hymns sung to Our Lady at the close of Compline — Alma Redemptoris Mater, Ave Regina Caelorum, Regina Caeli, and Salve Regina.

## About the Antiphons

The Marian antiphons are four ancient hymns of praise and petition to Mary sung (or recited) at the conclusion of Compline, the Church's final prayer of the day, and often after other Hours as well. Each belongs to a season of the liturgical year: *Alma Redemptoris Mater* (Advent through the Presentation) hails Mary as the mother of the Redeemer who help a fallen people rise; *Ave Regina Caelorum* (Presentation to Holy Thursday) greets her as Queen of Heaven; *Regina Caeli* (Easter season) replaces the others with the joyful command to rejoice because "He whom you merited to bear has risen"; and *Salve Regina* (Trinity Sunday through Advent) is the plaintive cry of exiles for their "life, sweetness, and hope."

Their placement at day's end is deliberate: the Church entrusts the close of each day, and implicitly each life, to Mary's maternal intercession before Christ. Praying them across the liturgical year traces the whole arc of salvation history through Mary's own experience of it — waiting, queenship, resurrection joy, and this valley of tears.

## Plain Latin Text

### 1. Salve Regina

Salve, Regina, Mater misericordiae:
Vita, dulcedo, et spes nostra, salve.
Ad te clamamus, exsules filii Hevae.
Ad te suspiramus, gementes et flentes
in hac lacrimarum valle.
Eia ergo, advocata nostra,
illos tuos misericordes oculos ad nos converte.
Et Iesum, benedictum fructum ventris tui,
nobis post hoc exsilium ostende.
O clemens, O pia, O dulcis Virgo Maria.

### 2. Alma Redemptoris Mater

Alma Redemptoris Mater, quae pervia caeli
porta manes, et stella maris, succurre cadenti,
surgere qui curat, populo: tu quae genuisti,
natura mirante, tuum sanctum Genitorem,
Virgo prius ac posterius, Gabrielis ab ore
sumens illud Ave, peccatorum miserere.

### 3. Ave Regina Caelorum

Ave, Regina caelorum,
Ave, Domina angelorum:
Salve, radix, salve, porta,
Ex qua mundo lux est orta:
Gaude, Virgo gloriosa,
Super omnes speciosa,
Vale, o valde decora,
Et pro nobis Christum exora.

### 4. Regina Caeli

Regina caeli, laetare, alleluia:
Quia quem meruisti portare, alleluia,
Resurrexit, sicut dixit, alleluia,
Ora pro nobis Deum, alleluia.
