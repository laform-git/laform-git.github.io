# LDS-05 The Church Fell Away

> **Error 5 of 10.** Everything they offer depends on this one claim. If the Church never died, it never needed restoring.

## What they teach

After the Apostles died, they say, the Church fell into a Great Apostasy. Priesthood authority was taken from the earth. Doctrine was corrupted by Greek philosophy. Councils replaced revelation. For roughly 1,700 years there was no true Church anywhere on earth.

Then, in 1820, the Father and the Son appeared to fourteen-year-old Joseph Smith. When he asked which church to join, he was told *"that I must join none of them, for they were all wrong... that all their creeds were an abomination in his sight"* (Joseph Smith—History 1:19). In 1830 Smith organized the church in New York. Their scripture calls it *"the only true and living church upon the face of the whole earth"* (D&C 1:30).

**That is why the missionaries speak of the "Restoration."** The word sounds gentle. It means that every Catholic who ever lived, every martyr and saint and mother who taught her children to pray, belonged to a dead church.

## Why it is an error

- **It calls Christ a failed founder.** He promised his Church would never be overcome and that he would be with it always. A total apostasy means he broke both promises within a century.
- **It has no witnesses.** No one in the second, third or fourth century noticed the Church had died. The Christians of those centuries wrote about bishops, the Eucharist, baptism and the Trinity, and died for them.
- **It cannot explain the Bible itself.** The New Testament was gathered, copied and recognized as Scripture by the very Church they say had apostatized. If that Church was dead, why trust the Bible it handed down?
- **The "evidence" for apostasy is just Catholic doctrine.** What they call corruption, bishops, sacraments, creeds, is exactly what the earliest Christians already believed.

## Response

### Scripture ✅

- *"You are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church, and the powers of death shall not prevail against it"* (Mt 16:18).
- *"Lo, I am with you always, to the close of the age"* (Mt 28:20). Always. Not for sixty years.
- The Father will give the Church the Spirit of truth *"to be with you for ever"* (Jn 14:16), and he *"will guide you into all the truth"* (Jn 16:13).
- The Church is *"the pillar and bulwark of the truth"* (1 Tim 3:15). A pillar that fell for 1,700 years held nothing up.
- *"To him be glory in the church and in Christ Jesus to all generations, for ever and ever"* (Eph 3:21). All generations, including the ones between the Apostles and 1830.

### Catechism ✅

- CCC 77: the Apostles left bishops as their successors, handing on their own teaching office.
- CCC 811–816: the one Church of Christ, which we profess in the Creed as one, holy, catholic and apostolic, subsists in the Catholic Church.
- CCC 869: the Church is apostolic; she is built on the Apostles and is still taught, sanctified and guided by them through their successors.

### Councils ✅

- **Vatican II, *Lumen Gentium*** §8 and §20: the Church founded by Christ subsists in the Catholic Church, and the bishops have succeeded the Apostles by divine institution.
- **Vatican II, *Dei Verbum*** §7–8: what the Apostles handed on is preserved in unbroken succession until the end of time.

### Doctors 🟢

- **St. Irenaeus of Lyons**, around 180, faced sects who claimed to have the secret, true teaching the Church had lost. He answered by listing the bishops of Rome one by one, from Peter to his own day, and said that this unbroken line is *"most abundant proof that there is one and the same life-giving faith"* kept in the Church from the Apostles until now (*Against Heresies* III.3).
- **St. Augustine** faced the Donatists, who claimed the Church had died everywhere except in their own corner of Africa. He answered with four words: *securus iudicat orbis terrarum*, the whole world judges securely. A Church that exists only in one place and one generation is not the Church Christ promised to all nations.

### Saints 🟢

- Around 107, **St. Ignatius of Antioch**, on his way to die in Rome, wrote: *"Wherever the bishop appears, there let the people be, just as wherever Jesus Christ is, there is the Catholic Church"* (*To the Smyrnaeans* 8). He had known the Apostles. He saw no apostasy. He saw bishops, the Eucharist, and the Catholic Church.
- Around 155, **St. Justin Martyr** described for the emperor what Christians did on Sunday: readings from the Apostles and Prophets, a homily, prayers, the bread and wine that became *"the flesh and blood of that Jesus who was made flesh"* (*First Apology* 65–67). It is the Mass.

## One line at the door

> *"Jesus promised to be with his Church always. If it died after the Apostles, was he with it or not?"*

Then stop talking and let the question do its work.

🕯 **Reflect:** Do I trust that Christ kept his promise to guard his Church?
