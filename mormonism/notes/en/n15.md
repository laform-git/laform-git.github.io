# LDS-15 The Church's Official Response

> What the Holy See and the bishops have actually said about the Latter-day Saints.

## The Church's Official Response

✅ **LDS baptism is invalid.** On 5 June 2001 the Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith, with the approval of John Paul II, answered "Negative" to whether LDS baptism is valid. The accompanying explanation by Fr. Luis Ladaria, SJ, published by the Holy See, says the LDS understanding of Father, Son, and Holy Spirit differs so radically from Christian faith that it cannot even be considered a heresy arising from a misunderstanding of Christian doctrine. Converts are baptized absolutely, not conditionally.

🟢 **Dominus Iesus** (CDF, 2000, Ratzinger, approved by John Paul II) §16–17 teaches that those validly baptized in Churches and ecclesial communities separated from Rome are in real, though imperfect, communion with the Catholic Church. The 2001 ruling found that LDS baptism is not valid, so that communion does not exist.

🟢 **Parish records.** In 2008 the Congregation for the Clergy asked bishops not to allow LDS genealogical projects to copy Catholic parish registers, because the names are used for proxy baptisms of the dead.

🟢 **Sects or New Religious Movements: A Pastoral Challenge** (1986) (§1.1) counts as sects groups that "apart from the Bible, have other 'revealed' books or 'prophetic messages,'" and prescribes renewed catechesis and community.

🟢 The bishops of Alta and Baja California, in a joint **Pastoral Exhortation Concerning Proselytism**, name Mormons among groups drawing Catholics away from the Church.

🟢 **John Paul II**, addressing the Mexican bishops (12 May 1990), named weak catechesis and lukewarm witness as what the sects exploit.

🟢 **Charity is not compromise.** The Church works with Latter-day Saints on humanitarian relief and the defense of marriage and religious freedom. In March 2019 Pope Francis received LDS president Russell M. Nelson at the Vatican, the first such meeting. Cooperation in good works does not mean shared faith.
