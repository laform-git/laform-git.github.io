# LDS-00 Cheat Sheet — Latter-day Saints (Mormons)

> The 10 errors of the Latter-day Saints (Mormons), worst first. For each one: what they believe, the truth, your response, the verse to open, and the question to leave with them. Read it before you answer the door.

Evidence: ✅ Dogma · 🟢 Common doctrine · 🟡 Theological opinion · 🔴 Devotional

---

## 1. They teach that God was once a man

- **They believe:** God the Father was once a man like us, who progressed to godhood and has a body of flesh and bones.
- **The truth:** God is eternal, uncreated spirit. He never became God; he always was. ✅
- **Response:** "Moses says God is God from everlasting to everlasting. There was never a time when he was not God."
- **Verse:** Psalm 90:2
- **Ask:** "If God was once a man, who was God before him?"

## 2. They teach a different Jesus

- **They believe:** Jesus is Heavenly Father's firstborn spirit child, the spirit brother of Lucifer and of us all.
- **The truth:** Jesus is the eternal Son, begotten, not made. Everything that exists, angels included, was made through him. ✅
- **Response:** "John says that without him nothing was made. Lucifer was made through Jesus, so he cannot be his brother."
- **Verse:** John 1:1–3
- **Ask:** "Was Jesus always God, or did he become one?"

## 3. They worship three gods, not the Trinity

- **They believe:** The Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost are three separate gods, one only in purpose.
- **The truth:** One God in three Persons, one divine substance. ✅
- **Response:** "God says through Isaiah that no god was formed before him and none will be after him."
- **Verse:** Isaiah 43:10
- **Ask:** "How many gods does Isaiah say there are?"

## 4. They teach that men can become gods

- **They believe:** Faithful Latter-day Saints can be exalted to godhood and rule worlds of their own.
- **The truth:** We share God's life by grace, but the creature never becomes a separate god. ✅
- **Response:** "'You will be like God' was the serpent's promise in the garden."
- **Verse:** Genesis 3:5; Isaiah 44:6
- **Ask:** "Who first promised that we could become gods?"

## 5. They say the Church fell away

- **They believe:** After the Apostles died, a Great Apostasy wiped out the true Church until Joseph Smith restored it in 1830.
- **The truth:** Christ promised his Church would never fail, and he kept that promise. ✅
- **Response:** "Jesus said the gates of hell would never prevail. Was he wrong for 1,800 years?"
- **Verse:** Matthew 16:18; 28:20
- **Ask:** "Who kept the faith between the Apostles and 1830?"

## 6. They say the Bible was corrupted, and added new scripture

- **They believe:** The Bible lost "plain and precious" parts. The Book of Mormon and their other books restore them.
- **The truth:** God's word has not passed away, and public revelation ended with Christ. ✅
- **Response:** "Jesus said his words would never pass away. And the Book of Mormon copies whole chapters of the 1611 King James Bible."
- **Verse:** Matthew 24:35
- **Ask:** "Which ancient manuscript contains the parts you say were lost?"

## 7. They say angels restored the priesthood

- **They believe:** John the Baptist, and then Peter, James and John, appeared to Joseph Smith in 1829 to give him the priesthood.
- **The truth:** Christ's priesthood was never lost. It is handed on by the laying on of hands, from the Apostles to today's bishops. ✅
- **Response:** "Jesus holds his priesthood permanently. It never needed to come back, because it never left."
- **Verse:** Hebrews 7:24
- **Ask:** "When exactly was the priesthood lost, and who says so?"

## 8. They tie salvation to temple ordinances

- **They believe:** The highest heaven requires secret temple rites and eternal marriage, and the dead can be baptized by proxy.
- **The truth:** We are saved by grace, through faith working in love, in the sacraments Christ himself gave. 🟢
- **Response:** "Paul says we are saved by grace as God's gift, not by what we achieve."
- **Verse:** Ephesians 2:8–9
- **Ask:** "Who can ever know he has done 'all he can do'?"

## 9. They teach a life before birth and three heavens

- **They believe:** We lived as spirit children before birth. Almost everyone ends up in one of three kingdoms of glory.
- **The truth:** God creates each soul at conception, and there are two final destinies, heaven and hell. ✅
- **Response:** "Jesus speaks of two destinies, not three kingdoms."
- **Verse:** Matthew 25:46
- **Ask:** "Where does Jesus mention a telestial kingdom?"

## 10. Their prophets failed and reversed themselves

- **They believe:** Joseph Smith and the presidents after him are prophets who receive revelation for the whole world.
- **The truth:** Smith's prophecies failed, and later "revelations" overturned earlier ones on polygamy, race and Adam. ✅
- **Response:** "The Bible gives a test for a true prophet."
- **Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:22
- **Ask:** "What does that verse say about a prophet whose word does not come true?"

---

## At every door

The missionaries are usually two young people far from home. Be warm. Offer water. Use one verse. Ask one question. Do not promise to pray about the Book of Mormon: "Let's read the Bible together." When they leave, pray for them by name.
