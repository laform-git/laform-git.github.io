# LDS-12 Verses They Misread

> The Bible verses the missionaries open most often, and what each one really says. Read the verse in context with them; context answers most of them.

## Verses They Misread

### PSALM 82:6 / JOHN 10:34 — "YOU ARE GODS"

- **Claim:** Humans can become gods.
- **Response:** Psalm 82 rebukes unjust human judges, and the next verse says "you shall die like men." Jesus cites it to argue from the lesser to the greater: if even judges were called "gods," how much more the Son of God. It says nothing about exaltation.

### 1 CORINTHIANS 8:5 — "MANY GODS AND MANY LORDS"

- **Claim:** Paul admits there are many gods.
- **Response:** Paul is describing what pagans worship: "there may be so-called gods." The very next verse answers it: "yet for us there is one God, the Father... and one Lord, Jesus Christ" (1 Cor 8:6). The verse just before says it outright: "there is no God but one" (8:4).

### JOHN 17:21–22 — "THAT THEY MAY BE ONE, EVEN AS WE ARE ONE"

- **Claim:** The Father and Son are one only in purpose, the way believers are one.
- **Response:** Jesus prays that believers share, by grace, a unity that he has with the Father by nature. A copy is not the original. Elsewhere Jesus claims a oneness no believer could claim: "He who has seen me has seen the Father" (Jn 14:9).

### 1 CORINTHIANS 15:29 — BAPTISM FOR THE DEAD

- **Claim:** Paul endorses proxy baptism for the dead.
- **Response:** Paul says "they," not "we," describing an outside practice to argue for the resurrection. No apostle, Father, or early Church ever practiced it. The verse is obscure; a whole temple system built on it is not.

### EZEKIEL 37:16–17 — THE TWO STICKS

- **Claim:** The stick of Judah is the Bible; the stick of Joseph is the Book of Mormon.
- **Response:** God explains the sign himself in verses 19–22: the two kingdoms, Judah and Israel, will be reunited under one king. It is about peoples, not books.

### JOHN 10:16 — "OTHER SHEEP"

- **Claim:** Jesus meant the Nephites in America.
- **Response:** The other sheep are the Gentiles, brought in through the apostles' preaching (Acts 10; Eph 2:11–14). Jesus says they "will listen to my voice," which happened through the Church's mission, not a visit to America.

### GENESIS 1:26 — MADE IN GOD'S IMAGE

- **Claim:** God has a physical body, since we are made in his image.
- **Response:** God is spirit (Jn 4:24), "not a man" (Num 23:19), and a spirit has no flesh and bones (Lk 24:39). The image of God in us is our spiritual soul, intellect, and freedom (CCC 356–357, 1705).

### JEREMIAH 1:5 — "BEFORE I FORMED YOU"

- **Claim:** We lived as spirits before birth.
- **Response:** The verse expresses God's foreknowledge and call, not a pre-existing Jeremiah. Each soul is created directly by God at conception (CCC 366).

### 1 CORINTHIANS 15:40–42 — THREE DEGREES OF GLORY

- **Claim:** Paul describes the celestial, terrestrial, and telestial kingdoms.
- **Response:** Paul contrasts earthly bodies with resurrected bodies, like a seed and a plant. "Telestial" is not in the text; Smith invented the word.

### HEBREWS 5:4 — "CALLED BY GOD, AS AARON WAS"

- **Claim:** Priesthood requires a call through proper authority, which only the restored church has.
- **Response:** True, no one takes the honor on himself. That is the Catholic argument. The very next verses say Christ was called as high priest "after the order of Melchizedek" (Heb 5:5–6), and Christ called the Apostles, who laid hands on their successors (Acts 14:23; 2 Tim 1:6). A call through an unbroken line of hands is exactly what Hebrews describes.

### AMOS 3:7 — GOD REVEALS THROUGH PROPHETS

- **Claim:** The Church needs a living prophet today.
- **Response:** "In these last days he has spoken to us by a Son" (Heb 1:1–2). Christ is the fullness of revelation; there is no further public revelation to wait for (CCC 65–66).

### REVELATION 14:6 — AN ANGEL WITH THE GOSPEL

- **Claim:** This angel is Moroni bringing the restored gospel.
- **Response:** The angel proclaims the eternal gospel already given, not a new one. Paul warns: "even if we or an angel from heaven should preach to you a gospel contrary to what we preached, let him be accursed" (Gal 1:8).

### ISAIAH 29:4 — A VOICE FROM THE DUST

- **Claim:** A book coming out of the ground, the Book of Mormon.
- **Response:** The chapter is a judgment on Jerusalem, called Ariel in verses 1–2: the proud city will be humbled until it speaks low from the dust. It is not a buried book.

🕯 **Reflect:** When someone quotes a verse at me, do I know my Bible well enough to read the verses around it?
