# LDS-16 At the Door — 22 Things You Will Hear

## Door Responses — What You'll Hear, What to Say

Twenty-two lines LDS missionaries commonly use, with a short answer and one question to leave them with. The missionaries are usually young, sincere, and far from home. Be warm. The aim is one honest question they will carry back, not a doorstep victory.

### 1. "WE'D LIKE TO SHARE A MESSAGE ABOUT THE RESTORED GOSPEL."

- **Claim:** The gospel was lost and restored through Joseph Smith.
- **Response:** A restoration requires a total loss, and Christ promised there would be none (Mt 16:18; 28:20). The Church has handed on the same faith in unbroken succession. ✅ CCC 77, 811–816
- **Ask:** "If the gospel was lost, did Jesus fail to keep his promise to be with the Church always?"

### 2. "READ THE BOOK OF MORMON AND PRAY ABOUT IT" (MORONI 10:4).

- **Claim:** A burning in the bosom confirms the book is true.
- **Response:** Feelings are not the test of revelation; the heart can deceive (Jer 17:9). Scripture tells us to test spirits by doctrine (1 Jn 4:1–3; Gal 1:8), and the historical evidence can be tested too.
- **Ask:** "If I prayed and felt the Book of Mormon was false, would you accept that answer?"

### 3. "WE'RE CHRISTIANS TOO."

- **Claim:** Belief in Jesus makes the LDS Christian.
- **Response:** The question is which Jesus. The LDS Jesus is a spirit brother of Lucifer who progressed to godhood; the Christian Jesus is the eternal Son, begotten not made. The Church judged the difference so great that LDS baptism is invalid (CDF, 5 June 2001). ✅
- **Ask:** "Was Jesus always God, or did he become one?"

### 4. "FAMILIES CAN BE TOGETHER FOREVER."

- **Claim:** Temple sealing preserves family relationships for eternity.
- **Response:** The love of family is sanctified, not erased, in heaven. But Jesus says there is no marrying in the resurrection (Mt 22:30), and our deepest union will be with God, in whom we also find one another (CCC 1024, 1045).
- **Ask:** "What did Jesus mean when he said they neither marry nor are given in marriage?"

### 5. "JOSEPH SMITH WAS A PROPHET, LIKE MOSES."

- **Claim:** God called Smith as a modern prophet.
- **Response:** The biblical test of a prophet is fulfilled prophecy and fidelity to prior revelation (Dt 13:1–5; 18:22). Smith's 1832 prophecy of a temple in Independence, Missouri "in this generation" (D&C 84:2–5) was never fulfilled.
- **Ask:** "How does D&C 84 pass the test of Deuteronomy 18:22?"

### 6. "THE BIBLE HAS LOST PLAIN AND PRECIOUS PARTS."

- **Claim:** The Bible needs modern scripture to fill its gaps.
- **Response:** The manuscript evidence shows remarkable stability, and no manuscript contains the missing parts. Jesus says his words will not pass away (Mt 24:35). 🟢 CCC 105–108
- **Ask:** "Which of Jesus' words did pass away?"

### 7. "WE BELIEVE IN THE FATHER, THE SON, AND THE HOLY GHOST TOO."

- **Claim:** LDS faith is Trinitarian in substance.
- **Response:** The LDS Godhead is three separate gods united in purpose. Isaiah says there is no god before or after the Lord (Is 43:10; 44:6, 8). One God in three Persons is not three gods in agreement. ✅ CCC 253–255
- **Ask:** "Is the Father the same God as the Son, or a different god?"

### 8. "THE PRIESTHOOD AUTHORITY WAS LOST AND HAD TO BE RESTORED."

- **Claim:** Apostolic authority vanished after the apostles died.
- **Response:** The apostles appointed successors (Acts 1:20–26; 14:23; 2 Tim 2:2). Clement of Rome, writing around 96 AD, describes that succession as already established. ✅ CCC 861–862
- **Ask:** "When exactly was the priesthood lost, and who says so?"

### 9. "GOD HAS A BODY OF FLESH AND BONES."

- **Claim:** God the Father is an exalted man.
- **Response:** "God is spirit" (Jn 4:24); "God is not man" (Num 23:19; Hos 11:9); a spirit has no flesh and bones (Lk 24:39). ✅ CCC 202, 370
- **Ask:** "If God was once a man, who was God before him?"

### 10. "WE ARE SAVED BY GRACE, AFTER ALL WE CAN DO" (2 NEPHI 25:23).

- **Claim:** Grace completes what our effort begins.
- **Response:** Grace is first, not last. God's grace prompts and enables every good work we do (Eph 2:8–10; Phil 2:13). Trent teaches that nothing preceding justification merits it. ✅ CCC 1996–2005
- **Ask:** "Who can ever know he has done all he can do?"

### 11. "LOOK HOW FAST THE CHURCH IS GROWING, AND THE FRUIT IN OUR FAMILIES."

- **Claim:** Good fruit proves true doctrine.
- **Response:** Good people are found in many faiths; virtue proves goodness, not a doctrine's truth. Jesus' test of false prophets (Mt 7:15–20) includes their teaching, and Galatians 1:8 sets the gospel above any angel.
- **Ask:** "Would a good life prove the Book of Mormon true, or only that the person is good?"

### 12. "WE CAN DO BAPTISMS FOR YOUR ANCESTORS."

- **Claim:** The dead can accept the gospel through proxy ordinances.
- **Response:** "It is appointed for men to die once, and after that comes judgment" (Heb 9:27). The Church prays and offers the Mass for the dead (2 Mac 12:45; CCC 1032), but no one is baptized for them.
- **Ask:** "Would you ask my permission before baptizing my Catholic grandparents?"

### 13. "THE EARLY CHURCH FELL INTO GREAT APOSTASY."

- **Claim:** After the apostles, the Church lost the true gospel.
- **Response:** Read the second-century Christians: Ignatius of Antioch (c. 107) on bishops and the Eucharist, Justin Martyr (c. 155) on the Mass. They teach Catholic faith, not LDS doctrine. No early Christian taught that God was once a man.
- **Ask:** "Can you name one early Christian writer who taught what Joseph Smith taught?"

### 14. "WE HAVE A LIVING PROPHET TODAY."

- **Claim:** A prophet gives continuing revelation.
- **Response:** LDS prophets have reversed what earlier prophets taught and practiced, on polygamy (1890), Adam-God (1976), and the priesthood ban (1978). Public revelation closed with Christ (Heb 1:1–2; CCC 66). The Magisterium guards what was given; it does not overturn it. 🟢
- **Ask:** "If revelation can reverse, how do you know today's is not reversed tomorrow?"

### 15. "PLEASE DON'T CALL US MORMONS. WE ARE THE CHURCH OF JESUS CHRIST."

- **Claim:** Since 2018 their leaders ask for the full name, which shows it is Christ's own Church.
- **Response:** Use the name they prefer; it costs nothing and shows respect. But a name does not make a church Christ's. Continuity does. The Church has been called "catholic" since St. Ignatius of Antioch around 107, and its bishops trace back to the Apostles.
- **Ask:** "Does putting Christ's name on a church make it his, or does an unbroken line back to his Apostles?"

### 16. "JOSEPH SMITH SEALED HIS TESTIMONY WITH HIS BLOOD."

- **Claim:** Smith died a martyr, which proves his sincerity.
- **Response:** His murder by a mob was a crime. But he was in Carthage jail because, as mayor, he had the *Nauvoo Expositor*, a newspaper that had exposed his secret plural marriages, declared a nuisance and its press destroyed; charged with riot for that, he was then held for treason after declaring martial law. When the mob attacked, he fired a pistol smuggled into the jail. Christian martyrs die for the faith without resisting, like Stephen (Acts 7:59–60).
- **Ask:** "Was he in jail for preaching the gospel, or for destroying a newspaper and the martial law that followed?"

### 17. "THE BOOK OF MORMON HAS ELEVEN WITNESSES."

- **Claim:** Three witnesses saw an angel and the plates; eight more handled them.
- **Response:** The eight were almost all from the Whitmer and Smith families. All three of the main witnesses, Oliver Cowdery, David Whitmer and Martin Harris, later broke with Smith and were cut off from his church in 1837–1838. Cowdery (1848) and Harris (1870) later returned; none of them denied the book. But Whitmer later wrote that Smith himself had gone astray and that his later revelations were not from God. 🟢
- **Ask:** "If your own witnesses believed the book but rejected Smith's later revelations, should I trust those revelations?"

### 18. "THE BIBLE ITSELF MENTIONS LOST BOOKS, LIKE THE BOOK OF JASHER."

- **Claim:** Since the Bible cites books we no longer have, scripture was lost.
- **Response:** The Bible quotes other writings the way any author cites sources (Josh 10:13; 2 Sam 1:18; the RSV-CE calls it the Book of Jashar). Being cited does not make a book Scripture. The Church discerned the canon by the apostolic Tradition (CCC 120); a book being cited does not make it inspired.
- **Ask:** "If a sermon quotes a hymn, does that make the hymnal Scripture?"

### 19. "YOUR CHURCH CHANGES TOO. THE MASS USED TO BE IN LATIN."

- **Claim:** Catholics have changed as much as Latter-day Saints.
- **Response:** The Church can change discipline: the language of the Mass, rules on fasting, the age of first Communion. She has never reversed a doctrine of faith. The faith she professed in the Creed of 381 is the faith she professes today. Polygamy, Adam-God and the nature of God (one God in Alma 11:26–29, plural gods in Smith's 1844 sermons) are doctrines, not disciplines.
- **Ask:** "Can you show me one article of the Nicene Creed the Catholic Church has reversed?"

### 20. "THE HEAVENS ARE CLOSED FOR YOU. YOU HAVE NO PROPHET."

- **Claim:** Without a living prophet, God is silent in the Catholic Church.
- **Response:** Christ is the Prophet, and he has given everything (Heb 1:1–2). God still speaks in Scripture, in the sacraments, and through his saints. In Mexico we know the heavens are not closed: in 1531 Our Lady appeared to St. Juan Diego at Tepeyac, and millions came to Christ. 🔴 Approved apparition, not public revelation.
- **Ask:** "Is God silent where his Son is present on the altar every day?"

### 21. "WILL YOU PREPARE TO BE BAPTIZED ON THIS DATE?"

- **Claim:** Missionaries are trained to invite you to set a baptism date early.
- **Response:** Thank them, and say clearly that you were baptized once, in the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit. There is "one Lord, one faith, one baptism" (Eph 4:5), and it cannot be repeated (CCC 1272). Being rebaptized into a church that teaches a different God would mean leaving the Trinity.
- **Ask:** "Do you believe my baptism, in the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit, was real?"

### 22. "CAN WE COME BACK AND TEACH YOU THE FIRST LESSON?"

- **Claim:** Lessons lead toward baptism.
- **Response:** Yes, but on the Bible and on history: Genesis 1 and John 1 on who God is, and the Book of Abraham papyri on whether Smith could translate. Pray for them by name. They are often the most open people in their church to honest questions.
- **Ask:** "Next time, will you read Isaiah 43:10 with me and tell me how many gods there are?"

🕯 **Reflect:** These young missionaries gave two years of their lives for what they believe. What have I given for the truth I have been handed?
