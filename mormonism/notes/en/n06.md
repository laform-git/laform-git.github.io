# LDS-06 A Bible Corrupted, New Scripture Added

> **Error 6 of 10.** Weaken trust in the Bible, and a new book can take its place.

## What they teach

Their eighth Article of Faith: *"We believe the Bible to be the word of God as far as it is translated correctly; we also believe the Book of Mormon to be the word of God."* Notice the condition on the Bible and none on the Book of Mormon.

The Book of Mormon itself says that a *"great and abominable church"* took *"many parts which are plain and most precious"* out of the Bible (1 Nephi 13:26–29). To restore them, they have three more books of scripture: the Book of Mormon, the Doctrine and Covenants, and the Pearl of Great Price. Joseph Smith also produced his own revision of the Bible, which they call the Joseph Smith Translation.

The Book of Mormon even anticipates the objection and mocks it: *"A Bible! A Bible! We have got a Bible, and there cannot be any more Bible"* (2 Nephi 29:3).

## Why it is an error

- **It puts every verse on probation.** "As far as it is translated correctly" lets any verse be set aside the moment it contradicts LDS teaching.
- **The lost parts are nowhere.** We have thousands of Greek and Hebrew manuscripts, some within a few generations of the Apostles. Not one contains the "plain and precious parts."
- **Smith used no manuscripts.** His "translation" of the Bible worked from an English King James Bible, not from Hebrew or Greek.
- **Their own book copies the King James Bible.** Plates said to be buried in the fifth century contain the exact wording of an English translation made in 1611.
- **Revelation is complete.** God's public revelation reached its fullness in Christ. Anything that claims to correct or complete it is not from him.

## Response

### Scripture ✅

- *"Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not pass away"* (Mt 24:35).
- *"The grass withers, the flower fades; but the word of our God will stand for ever"* (Is 40:8; 1 Pet 1:25).
- *"The faith which was once for all delivered to the saints"* (Jude 3). Once for all, not delivered, lost and delivered again.
- *"In many and various ways God spoke of old to our fathers by the prophets; but in these last days he has spoken to us by a Son"* (Heb 1:1–2).
- *"Even if we, or an angel from heaven, should preach to you a gospel contrary to that which we preached to you, let him be accursed"* (Gal 1:8). The Book of Mormon was brought, they say, by an angel.

### Catechism ✅

- CCC 65–67: Christ is the Father's one, perfect and unsurpassable Word. There will be no further public revelation. Private revelations cannot surpass or correct it.
- CCC 105–108: God is the author of Scripture; the books teach firmly, faithfully and without error the truth God wanted put down for our salvation.
- CCC 120: the canon of Scripture was discerned by apostolic Tradition.

### Councils ✅

- **Rome (382, per the Decree of Damasus, whose dating is debated), Hippo (393), Carthage (397):** the same canon of Scripture we read today.
- **Trent**, Session IV (1546): the canon defined solemnly, received "with an equal affection of piety and reverence" together with apostolic Tradition.
- **Vatican II, *Dei Verbum*** §4: *"We now await no further new public revelation before the glorious manifestation of our Lord Jesus Christ."*

### Doctors 🟢

- **St. Jerome** moved to Bethlehem, learned Hebrew from Jewish teachers, and spent decades comparing manuscripts to translate the Bible faithfully. That is what translation means: real texts, real languages, years of work. Joseph Smith's "translation" had no manuscripts at all. Jerome also left us the line: *"Ignorance of Scripture is ignorance of Christ."*
- **St. Athanasius**, in his Easter letter of 367, listed exactly the twenty-seven books of our New Testament and wrote: *"Let no one add to these, nor take anything from them."*

### Saints 🟢

- In 303 the emperor Diocletian ordered Christians to hand over their Scriptures to be burned. **St. Felix of Thibiuca**, a bishop in North Africa, refused: *"I have the Scriptures, but I will not give them up."* He was beheaded. Christians who would die rather than surrender a page did not lose the "plain and precious parts."
- In 180, the **Scillitan Martyrs** of North Africa were asked what was in their case. *"Books, and the letters of Paul, a just man."* They were executed that day. The Scriptures were carried, guarded and died for, never lost.

## One line at the door

> *"Jesus said his words would never pass away. Which of his words did pass away, and which manuscript shows it?"*

Then stop talking and let the question do its work.

🕯 **Reflect:** Do I read the Scripture that martyrs died to protect?

## From the full reference

### Scripture and Revelation

**LDS teaching:** The Bible is true "as far as it is translated correctly" and has lost "plain and precious" parts. The Book of Mormon, Doctrine and Covenants, and Pearl of Great Price are added scripture, and revelation continues through a living prophet.

**Catholic teaching:** 🟢 Public revelation was completed in Christ; no new public revelation is to be expected (CCC 66–67). The canon is closed (CCC 120). Scripture and Tradition, guarded by the Magisterium, hand on the whole deposit; nothing was lost that needs a new prophet (Dei Verbum §8–10).

### The Book of Mormon

*"The scripture Joseph Smith published in 1830, which Latter-day Saints hold to be a record of ancient peoples in the Americas."*

Smith said he translated it from golden plates shown to him by the angel Moroni near Palmyra, New York. It tells of Israelites who sailed to the Americas around 600 BC and of Christ's visit to them after his resurrection. Latter-day Saints place it beside the Bible, the Doctrine and Covenants and the Pearl of Great Price.

The Catholic Church does not accept it as scripture: public revelation was completed in Christ and the canon is closed (CCC 66–67, 120). Critics point to its long passages copied from the 1611 King James Version and to the many changes made to its text since 1830.

### "AS FAR AS IT IS TRANSLATED CORRECTLY"

- **Claim:** The Bible is reliable only where it has been translated correctly (8th Article of Faith).
- **Response:** This lets any verse be set aside when it contradicts LDS teaching. Yet the thousands of Greek and Hebrew manuscripts, including the Dead Sea Scrolls, show remarkable stability. No manuscript contains the missing "plain and precious parts."
- **Ask:** "Which ancient manuscript contains the parts you say were lost?"

### THE JOSEPH SMITH TRANSLATION

- **Claim:** Smith produced an inspired revision of the Bible restoring lost passages.
- **Response:** Smith used no manuscripts; he rewrote the King James text. His revision of Genesis 50 adds a prophecy of a future seer named Joseph, son of Joseph, which is Joseph Smith, son of Joseph Smith Sr. A "restoration" that predicts its own author is a warning sign, not evidence.

### THE BOOK OF MORMON COPIES THE KING JAMES VERSION

- **Claim:** The plates are ancient American scripture from about 600 BC onward.
- **Response:** Long sections reproduce the 1611 King James Version nearly word for word, including Isaiah 2–14 (2 Nephi 12–24) and the Sermon on the Mount (3 Nephi 12–14). Plates buried centuries before 1611 should not contain the wording of an English translation made in 1611.

### THEY CHANGED THE BOOK OF MORMON ITSELF

- **Claim:** The Book of Mormon is "the most correct of any book on earth" (Joseph Smith).
- **Response:** The 1830 edition called Mary "the mother of God" and Jesus "the Eternal Father" (1 Nephi 11:18, 21). Later editions changed these to "the mother of the Son of God" and "the Son of the Eternal Father" which critics read as a move away from one God; LDS scholars say the edits only clarified the meaning. In 1981, 2 Nephi 30:6 changed "white and delightsome" to "pure and delightsome." Thousands of edits have been made since 1830.
- **Ask:** "If it was translated by the gift and power of God, why did its teaching about God need correcting?"
