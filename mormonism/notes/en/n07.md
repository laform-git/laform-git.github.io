# LDS-07 A Priesthood Restored by Angels

> **Error 7 of 10.** A restored Church needs restored authority. So they say angels brought it back.

## What they teach

When the Church fell away, they say, the authority to act in God's name was taken from the earth. On 15 May 1829, John the Baptist appeared to Joseph Smith and Oliver Cowdery by the Susquehanna River and conferred on them the Aaronic priesthood (D&C 13). Soon after, Peter, James and John appeared and gave them the higher Melchizedek priesthood (D&C 27:12).

Today almost every worthy Latter-day Saint male holds the priesthood. Boys are ordained deacons in the year they turn twelve, and men become elders. Only this priesthood, they say, can baptize, confirm, and seal families in the temple. Any other baptism lacks authority.

**A detail worth knowing.** The first detailed written accounts of these angelic visits appear only around 1834–1835, five or six years after they are said to have happened.

## Why it is an error

- **The priesthood was never lost.** Christ's priesthood is eternal, and he handed it on through the Apostles to men they ordained, and those men to others, in a line unbroken to today.
- **It revives a priesthood Christ ended.** The Aaronic priesthood belonged to the Old Covenant of animal sacrifice. Hebrews says it was set aside because it could not make anyone perfect.
- **It rests on unwitnessed angels.** The apostolic succession happened in public, before whole churches, and was recorded by the people who saw it. The LDS restoration happened by a riverbank before two men, and was described in detail only years later.
- **It makes a kingdom of priests into a club of officeholders.** Every baptized Christian shares in Christ's priesthood (1 Pet 2:9). The ordained priesthood serves that common priesthood. It is not a rank every male earns by worthiness.

## Response

### Scripture ✅

- *"He holds his priesthood permanently, because he continues for ever"* (Heb 7:24). It cannot be lost, because its holder cannot die.
- *"If perfection had been attainable through the Levitical priesthood... what further need would there have been for another priest to arise after the order of Melchizedek?"* (Heb 7:11). The Aaronic priesthood was fulfilled and replaced, not paused.
- *"His office let another take"* (Acts 1:20). The Apostles filled Judas' place. Succession began in the first chapter of Acts.
- Paul and Barnabas *"appointed elders for them in every church, with prayer and fasting"* (Acts 14:23). Paul tells Titus to *"appoint elders in every town"* (Ti 1:5) and Timothy to hand on what he received *"to faithful men who will be able to teach others also"* (2 Tim 2:2). Four generations in one verse.
- *"Rekindle the gift of God that is within you through the laying on of my hands"* (2 Tim 1:6).

### Catechism ✅

- CCC 1536: Holy Orders is the sacrament through which the mission Christ entrusted to his Apostles continues in the Church until the end of time.
- CCC 1544–1545: the priesthood of the Old Covenant is fulfilled in Christ, the one priest. The ordained ministry shares in his one priesthood.
- CCC 1555, 1576: the bishops, successors of the Apostles, hand on the gift of the Spirit by the laying on of hands.
- CCC 1546–1547: every baptized person shares in Christ's priesthood; the ordained priesthood is at its service.

### Councils ✅

- **Trent**, Session XXIII (1563): Holy Orders is a true sacrament instituted by Christ, conferred by the laying on of hands.
- **Vatican II, *Lumen Gentium*** §20–21: the bishops have succeeded the Apostles by divine institution, and the line has been preserved from the beginning.

### Doctors 🟢

- **St. John Chrysostom**, who hid to escape being made a bishop because he felt unworthy, wrote *On the Priesthood*: the priesthood is exercised on earth but belongs among heavenly things, *"for neither man, nor angel, nor archangel, nor any other created power, but the Paraclete himself, instituted this vocation"* (III.4). Not even an angel can give it. Only the Holy Spirit, through those Christ chose.

### Saints 🟢

- Around 96, **St. Clement of Rome**, the third successor of Peter, wrote to the church in Corinth: *"The Apostles received the Gospel for us from the Lord Jesus Christ... They appointed their first converts... to be bishops and deacons... and afterwards they gave instructions that, when these should fall asleep, other approved men should succeed to their ministry"* (*1 Clement* 42, 44). Written while St. John was still alive. Succession was already the rule.

## One line at the door

> *"Hebrews says Jesus holds his priesthood permanently, because he lives forever. How could it be lost?"*

Then stop talking and let the question do its work.

🕯 **Reflect:** Do I thank God for the priests whose hands, through an unbroken line, bring me Christ in the sacraments?

## From the full reference

### PRIESTHOOD RESTORED BY ANGELS

- **Claim:** John the Baptist and Peter, James and John appeared in 1829 to restore the Aaronic and Melchizedek priesthoods.
- **Response:** Christ has one eternal priesthood (Heb 7:24), shared through apostolic succession by the laying on of hands (2 Tim 1:6; CCC 1576). The Aaronic priesthood ended when Christ's sacrifice fulfilled it (Heb 7:11–18).
