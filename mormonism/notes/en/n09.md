# LDS-09 A Life Before Birth and Three Heavens

> **Error 9 of 10.** Where we came from and where we are going. They rewrite both ends of the story.

## What they teach

**Before birth.** Every human being lived first as a spirit child of Heavenly Parents. Some of these spirits were "noble and great" and chosen as leaders before they were born (Abraham 3:22–23). Underneath even the spirit, they say, is an eternal "intelligence" that *"was not created or made, neither indeed can be"* (D&C 93:29). Birth on earth is a step in a long progression: to gain a body and be tested.

**After death.** The dead go to a spirit world, where those who never heard the LDS gospel can accept it, helped by proxy ordinances done for them in temples. At the final judgment, nearly everyone receives one of three kingdoms of glory, which Smith described in a vision of 1832 (D&C 76): the **celestial** (the highest, with exaltation in its top level), the **terrestrial** (good people who did not accept the full LDS gospel), and the **telestial** (liars, adulterers, the wicked). Only a few, the "sons of perdition" who knew the truth fully and fought it, go to outer darkness.

**Why it appeals.** It removes the fear of hell for almost everyone and makes this life one chapter among many. But it is not what Jesus taught.

## Why it is an error

- **The soul is created, not pre-existent.** God creates each human soul directly, at the moment of conception. We did not exist before. Life is a gift that begins, not a stage in an endless progression.
- **"Uncreated intelligence" denies creation.** If something in us was never created, then God is not the Creator of all things. He is only an organizer of what already existed.
- **Jesus speaks of two destinies, not three kingdoms.** Eternal life or eternal punishment, the narrow way or the wide road.
- **It removes the urgency of this life.** Scripture sets the decision now: *"it is appointed for men to die once, and after that comes judgment"* (Heb 9:27), not a second round of preaching.
- **Purgatory is not a lesser heaven.** It is purification of those already saved, on their way to heaven.

## Response

### Scripture ✅

- The Lord *"formed the spirit of man within him"* (Zech 12:1). The spirit is formed, made, in each person.
- *"Before I formed you in the womb I knew you"* (Jer 1:5). God knew Jeremiah, the way he knows all things before they happen. It does not say Jeremiah was there.
- *"They will go away into eternal punishment, but the righteous into eternal life"* (Mt 25:46). Two places, both eternal.
- *"Enter by the narrow gate; for the gate is wide and the way is easy, that leads to destruction"* (Mt 7:13). Jesus does not describe hell as nearly empty.
- The rich man and Lazarus: *"Between us and you a great chasm has been fixed"* (Lk 16:26). No crossing over after death.
- *"We must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, so that each one may receive good or evil, according to what he has done in the body"* (2 Cor 5:10). In the body, in this life.

### Catechism ✅

- CCC 366: every spiritual soul is created immediately by God, not produced by the parents, and it is immortal.
- CCC 1021–1022: each person receives his eternal recompense in his immortal soul at the very moment of death, in a particular judgment.
- CCC 1023–1029: heaven is the perfect life with the Holy Trinity.
- CCC 1030–1031: purgatory is the final purification of the elect, entirely different from the punishment of the damned.
- CCC 1033–1037: hell is real and eternal, the state of definitive self-exclusion from God.

### Councils ✅

- **Synod of Constantinople (543):** a synod condemned the teaching that human souls existed before their bodies.
- **Benedict XII, *Benedictus Deus*** (1336): immediately after death, souls go to heaven, to purification, or to hell.
- **Lateran V (1513):** the soul is immortal and individual, one for each human body.
- **Pius XII, *Humani Generis*** §36 (1950): the Catholic faith obliges us to hold that souls are immediately created by God.

### Doctors 🟢

- **St. Thomas Aquinas** (*ST* I q.118 a.3): the soul was not created before the body. The soul is the form of the body; it is made for this body, and created together with it.
- **St. Jerome** fought the followers of Origen, who taught that souls lived before birth and fell into bodies. In his Letter 124 to Avitus he exposed Origen's claim that souls fell from heaven through their own negligence and were bound to bodies as a consequence of sin, and warned Avitus against it.

### Saints 🟢

- In July 1917 at Fatima, Our Lady showed three shepherd children a vision of hell. **St. Jacinta Marto**, seven years old, was so shaken that she spent the rest of her short life offering prayers and sacrifices for sinners, saying, *"How sorry I am for the souls who go to hell!"* (Sr. Lúcia, *Memoirs*). She and her brother **St. Francisco** were canonized in 2017. 🔴 Approved private revelation, not doctrine. The doctrine is Christ's own words.

## One line at the door

> *"Jesus said, 'They will go away into eternal punishment, but the righteous into eternal life.' Where does he mention a third kingdom?"*

Then stop talking and let the question do its work.

## From the full reference

### PREMORTAL EXISTENCE

- **Claim:** Every human lived as a spirit child of Heavenly Parents before birth.
- **Response:** The soul is created by God, not born of heavenly parents, and does not exist before conception (CCC 366). The Church rejected the pre-existence of souls in the sixth century.

### THREE KINGDOMS OF GLORY

- **Claim:** Nearly everyone reaches one of three heavens; only a few go to "outer darkness."
- **Response:** Scripture presents two final destinies, eternal life and eternal punishment (Mt 25:46). Purgatory is purification on the way to heaven, not a lesser heaven (CCC 1030–1031).
