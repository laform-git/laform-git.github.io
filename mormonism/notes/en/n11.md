# LDS-11 Who They Are — Origins and Why They Differ

> Where the movement came from, and the one hinge every error turns on.

## Origins — Who the Latter-day Saints Are

Founded by Joseph Smith Jr. (1805–1844). Smith claimed a "First Vision" in 1820 in which God the Father and Jesus Christ appeared to him as two separate beings, and in 1823 visits from the angel Moroni, who led him to golden plates buried near Palmyra, New York. The Book of Mormon was published and the church founded in 1830. After Smith was killed by a mob in Carthage, Illinois (1844), Brigham Young led the main body to Salt Lake City (1847), still its headquarters. The church is led by a president regarded as a living prophet.

**Today.** The church reported 17,887,212 members at the end of 2025, with 204 temples in operation. Mexico has the largest membership outside the United States: 1,572,287 members and 14 temples. The current president is Dallin H. Oaks, a former Utah Supreme Court justice, who succeeded Russell M. Nelson in October 2025. (Membership counts everyone on the rolls; far fewer attend weekly.)

**The name.** The official name is The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Since 2018 its leaders have asked people not to use "Mormon" or "LDS." Members are still widely called Mormons, and the missionaries will not be offended if you ask what they prefer. In Spanish they call themselves *Santos de los Últimos Días*; the young men at your door are *élderes*.

Mainstream archaeology and DNA evidence do not support the Book of Mormon's claim that Native American peoples descend from ancient Israelites. This is a historical problem, not only a theological one.

## Why They Differ

- **A different God.** A God who was once a man is not the eternal Creator.
- **A different Jesus.** The brother of Lucifer is not the eternal Son "begotten, not made."
- **A different Trinity.** Three gods are not one God in three Persons.
- **A different scripture.** The Book of Mormon copies the 1611 KJV and has itself been changed.
- **A different destiny.** Humans do not become gods; they share God's life by grace.
- **A different church.** Christ's Church never disappeared and needed no restoration.
- That is why the Church does not count LDS baptism as Christian, while still calling Catholics to charity toward Latter-day Saints.

The hinge is the first error. Change who God is, and every other doctrine changes with it: Christ becomes a brother, the Trinity a council, salvation a promotion, and the Church a restoration project.

The answer is not only argument. Catholics who know their faith and live it with visible joy are the real response.
