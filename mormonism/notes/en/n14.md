# LDS-14 The Strangest Claims

> Claims most Catholics have never heard. Use them gently: many Latter-day Saints have never heard them either.

## The Strangest Claims

Many Latter-day Saints know little of these. Several are now openly acknowledged in the church's own Gospel Topics essays (2013–2015).

### KOLOB

- **Claim:** God dwells near a star or planet called Kolob (Book of Abraham 3:2–9).
- **Response:** God is not located near any star; he fills heaven and earth (Jer 23:24), and the highest heaven cannot contain him (1 Kgs 8:27).

### THE BOOK OF ABRAHAM PAPYRI

- **Claim:** Smith translated the Book of Abraham from Egyptian papyri written by Abraham.
- **Response:** Fragments of the papyri were rediscovered in 1966. Egyptologists identify them as ordinary funerary texts from around the first century BC, with no mention of Abraham. The LDS church's own essay concedes the characters do not match the translation.

### THE SEER STONE IN A HAT

- **Claim:** The Book of Mormon was translated from golden plates.
- **Response:** Witnesses described Smith placing a seer stone in a hat and reading words while the plates were often covered or absent. The church published a photograph of the stone in 2015. The same stone had earlier been used for treasure hunting.

### POLYGAMY

- **Claim:** God commanded plural marriage (D&C 132).
- **Response:** The church's own essays acknowledge Smith married about 30 to 40 women, including women already married to other men and a girl several months short of 15. The practice ended in 1890 under federal pressure. Christ restored marriage to "the two shall become one flesh" (Mt 19:4–6).

### SKIN COLOR AS A CURSE

- **Claim:** The Book of Mormon says God cursed the Lamanites with "a skin of blackness" (2 Nephi 5:21).
- **Response:** Black men were barred from the LDS priesthood from 1852 until 1978. The church now disavows the racial theories once used to justify it. Scripture: "God shows no partiality" (Acts 10:34); all are one in Christ (Gal 3:28).

### ADAM IS GOD

- **Claim:** Brigham Young taught in 1852 that Adam is "our Father and our God."
- **Response:** Later LDS leaders rejected this in 1976 as false doctrine. A prophet contradicted by his own successors is not speaking for God (Dt 18:22).

### THE GARDEN OF EDEN IN MISSOURI

- **Claim:** Smith taught that Eden was in Jackson County, Missouri, and that Adam lived at Adam-ondi-Ahman (D&C 116).
- **Response:** Genesis places Eden near the Tigris and Euphrates (Gen 2:14). Missouri is not a biblical location.

### HEAVENLY MOTHER

- **Claim:** Humans have a Heavenly Mother as well as a Heavenly Father.
- **Response:** Scripture knows one God, with no divine consort. Israel's prophets condemned the worship of a "queen of heaven" goddess (Jer 7:18; 44:17–19).

🕯 **Reflect:** Many Latter-day Saints are sincere, family-centered, and devout. Do I respect the person while being clear about the error?
