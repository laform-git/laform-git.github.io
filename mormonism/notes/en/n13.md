# LDS-13 Their Rules — Temple, Garments, Word of Wisdom, Tithing

> The practices that shape a Latter-day Saint's week. Some are admirable. The problem is what they are tied to.

## Their Own Teachings and Rules

Much of LDS discipline is genuinely good: chastity before marriage, family evenings, generous giving, fasting, two years of missionary service at a young age. Say so honestly. The error is not the discipline but what it is made to earn: worthiness for the temple, and through the temple, exaltation.

### TEMPLE ENDOWMENT AND GARMENTS

- **Claim:** Secret temple rites and sacred undergarments are needed for exaltation.
- **Response:** Christ taught openly: "I have said nothing in secret" (Jn 18:20). The rites closely track the Masonic ritual Smith joined in 1842, weeks before introducing the endowment. The Christian sacraments are public and instituted by Christ.

### THE TEMPLE RECOMMEND

- **Claim:** Only members judged worthy in an interview with their bishop and stake president may enter the temple.
- **Response:** Worthiness here means keeping a list: tithing, the Word of Wisdom, chastity, support for church leaders. Non-member parents cannot attend their own child's temple wedding. In the Catholic Church every wedding Mass is open, and every sinner may come to confession. Christ came "not to call the righteous, but sinners" (Mk 2:17).

### TITHING

- **Claim:** A full tithe, ten percent of income, is required to hold a temple recommend.
- **Response:** Generosity is a Christian duty, and the Church asks the faithful to provide for her needs (CCC 2043). But no sacrament is ever sold or withheld for money. "You received without pay, give without pay" (Mt 10:8).

### THE WORD OF WISDOM

- **Claim:** Faithful members must avoid alcohol, tobacco, coffee, and tea.
- **Response:** Self-discipline is good, and the Church has her own fasts. But Jesus declared all foods clean (Mk 7:19), and Paul warns against being judged over food and drink (Col 2:16).

### FASTING

- **Claim:** Members fast for two meals on the first Sunday of each month and give the money saved as a fast offering for the poor.
- **Response:** A good practice, close to Catholic tradition. Fasting joined to almsgiving is biblical (Is 58:6–7; Mt 6:16–18). Affirm it, and tell them about Lent and Friday penance.

### MISSIONARY SERVICE

- **Claim:** Young men serve two-year missions from age eighteen, young women eighteen months from age nineteen, usually at their own family's expense.
- **Response:** Their sacrifice is real; honor it. The question is what they are sent to preach. Zeal is not proof of truth (Rom 10:2). The Church has sent missionaries to every nation for twenty centuries, many of them martyrs.

### ETERNAL (CELESTIAL) MARRIAGE

- **Claim:** Marriage sealed in the temple lasts forever and is required for the highest glory.
- **Response:** "In the resurrection they neither marry nor are given in marriage" (Mt 22:30). Marriage is a holy sacrament for this life, pointing to the eternal union of Christ and the Church (CCC 1603, 1617).

### BAPTISM FOR THE DEAD

- **Claim:** Members are baptized on behalf of the dead so they can accept the gospel.
- **Response:** "It is appointed for man to die once, and after that comes judgment" (Heb 9:27). The Church prays and offers Mass for the dead, which Scripture supports (2 Mac 12:46), but does not baptize for them.

🕯 **Reflect:** Many Latter-day Saints live their rules more faithfully than many Catholics live ours. What would they see if they watched my week?
