# LDS-01 God Was Once a Man

> **Error 1 of 10, the worst.** Everything else rests on who God is. Get God wrong and every other doctrine is built on sand.

## What they teach

God the Father was once a mortal man on another world. He obeyed eternal laws, progressed, and became a god. He has a body of flesh and bones. Lorenzo Snow, the church's fifth president, put it in a couplet every Latter-day Saint knows: *"As man now is, God once was; as God now is, man may become."*

Joseph Smith taught it openly at a funeral sermon in April 1844, the King Follett Discourse: *"God himself was once as we are now, and is an exalted man."* Their own scripture adds that *"the Father has a body of flesh and bones as tangible as man's"* (D&C 130:22).

It sounds warm, even hopeful. But a God who was once a man is not the God of the Bible. He is a creature who got ahead.

**Today they speak of it less.** Asked about it in 1997, church president Gordon B. Hinckley told *Time*: *"I don't know that we teach it. I don't know that we emphasize it."* The teaching was never withdrawn. It is still in the King Follett Discourse and in the church's own manuals. Many young missionaries have simply never thought it through.

## Why it is an error

- **It makes God a creature.** A being who became God had a beginning. Whatever had a beginning was caused by something before it, and that something is the real God.
- **It leaves an endless line of gods.** If the Father had a Father, who had a Father, there is no first God at all, only an infinite chain of exalted men. Nothing explains why anything exists.
- **It makes God a body.** A body is made of parts, occupies a place, and can be in only one place at a time. God fills heaven and earth (Jer 23:24).
- **It changes worship.** We adore God because he is infinitely above us, the source of all being. One does not worship a senior colleague.

## Response

### Scripture ✅

- *"Before the mountains were brought forth, or ever thou hadst formed the earth and the world, from everlasting to everlasting thou art God"* (Ps 90:2). Not "became God." Was God, from everlasting.
- *"For I the LORD do not change"* (Mal 3:6). A God who progressed from man to God changed in the biggest way possible.
- *"God is not man, that he should lie"* (Num 23:19). *"For I am God and not man, the Holy One in your midst"* (Hos 11:9).
- *"God is spirit, and those who worship him must worship in spirit and truth"* (Jn 4:24). And the risen Jesus himself says *"a spirit has not flesh and bones as you see that I have"* (Lk 24:39).
- *"Before me no god was formed, nor shall there be any after me"* (Is 43:10). God says he knows of no other god (Is 44:8). If he was once a man under another god, he would know.

### Catechism ✅

- CCC 202: Jesus confirms that God is "the one Lord."
- CCC 212: God is "He who Is," from everlasting to everlasting, unchanging.
- CCC 213: God alone is Being itself; every creature receives its being from him.
- CCC 370: God is pure spirit, in whom there is no difference between the sexes; he is neither man nor woman.

### Councils ✅

- **Lateran IV (1215):** there is *"only one true God, eternal and immeasurable, almighty, unchangeable, incomprehensible and ineffable."*
- **Vatican I, *Dei Filius*** ch. 1 (1870): God is *"one, singular, completely simple and unchangeable spiritual substance,"* really and essentially distinct from the world.

### Doctors 🟢

- **St. Thomas Aquinas** (*ST* I q.3 a.1): God is not a body. Every body can be moved and has parts, but God is the first, unmoved cause of everything. (*ST* I q.9 a.1): God is altogether unchangeable, because he is pure act and lacks nothing he could gain.
- **St. Augustine** knew this error from the inside. As a young Manichaean he could only picture God as a vast body spread through space, and he says that was the main cause of his errors (*Confessions* V.10; VII.1). When he finally grasped that God is spirit, without size or place, it opened the way to his conversion.

### Saints 🟢

- Around 155, **St. Justin Martyr**, a philosopher who had searched every school before finding Christ, wrote to the Roman emperor that Christians dedicate themselves *"to the unbegotten and impassible God"* (*First Apology* 25). Unbegotten: no father before him. Impassible: nothing can change him. Justin was beheaded in Rome around 165. The first Christians died for a God who never had a beginning.
- After his conversion, **St. Augustine**, who once pictured God as a body, prayed: *"Most high, most good, most powerful... unchangeable, yet changing all things"* (*Confessions* I.4).

## One line at the door

> *"Psalm 90 says God is God from everlasting to everlasting. If your Heavenly Father was once a man, who was God before him?"*

Then stop talking and let the question do its work.

🕯 **Reflect:** Do I worship a God infinitely greater than myself, or a bigger version of me?

## From the full reference

### God

**LDS teaching:** God the Father was once a man who progressed to godhood and has a body of flesh and bone. "As man now is, God once was; as God now is, man may become" (Lorenzo Snow).

**Catholic teaching:** ✅ God is one, eternal, uncreated, pure spirit, utterly other than his creation (Jn 4:24; Is 43:10; CCC 42, 202, 212). He did not become God; he is the source of all being.

### GENESIS 1:26 — MADE IN GOD'S IMAGE

- **Claim:** God has a physical body, since we are made in his image.
- **Response:** God is spirit (Jn 4:24), "not a man" (Num 23:19), and a spirit has no flesh and bones (Lk 24:39). The image of God in us is our spiritual soul, intellect and freedom (CCC 356–357, 1705).
