# LDS-10 Failed and Reversed Prophecies

> **Error 10 of 10.** Their whole system rests on living prophets. Scripture gives a simple test for prophets.

## What they teach

Joseph Smith was a prophet, seer and revelator, like Moses. Every president of the church after him is also a prophet who receives revelation for the whole world. Members sustain him as such twice a year at General Conference. Their scripture keeps growing: the Doctrine and Covenants is a collection of revelations, and two "Official Declarations" were added in 1890 and 1978.

The missionaries will often say: "Wouldn't you want a living prophet today, like the ancient Church had?"

## Why it is an error

The test is God's own: *"When a prophet speaks in the name of the LORD, if the word does not come to pass or come true, that is a word which the LORD has not spoken"* (Dt 18:22). Here is the record.

### Prophecies that failed

- **The temple in Missouri.** In September 1832 Smith announced that a temple would be built in Independence, Missouri, and that *"this generation shall not all pass away"* until it stood (D&C 84:2–5). The Saints were driven out of Jackson County the next year. That generation has long passed away. No LDS temple stands on that lot.
- **Fifty-six years.** On 14 February 1835 Smith told church leaders that *"the coming of the Lord, which was nigh... even fifty-six years should wind up the scene"* (History of the Church 2:182). That pointed to 1891.
- **The Civil War prophecy.** Members point to D&C 87 (1832), which foretells war beginning in South Carolina. 🟡 But South Carolina was already in open conflict with Washington over nullification that very year, and the prophecy goes on to say war would be *"poured out upon all nations"*, making *"a full end of all nations."* That did not happen.

### Revelations that were reversed

- **Polygamy.** D&C 132 (1843) called plural marriage part of the "new and everlasting covenant." In 1890, under federal laws that threatened to seize church property, President Wilford Woodruff ended it (Official Declaration 1).
- **Race and priesthood.** From 1852, Black men were barred from the priesthood and Black members from temple ordinances. In 1978 a new revelation lifted the ban (Official Declaration 2). The church now disavows the theories once used to justify it.
- **Adam is God.** In 1852 Brigham Young taught from the pulpit that Adam *"is our Father and our God, and the only God with whom we have to do."* In 1976 President Spencer W. Kimball denounced that teaching as false doctrine.

### Why reversal matters

A prophet who corrects his predecessor is saying the predecessor did not speak for God. If revelation can be reversed, no revelation can be trusted, including today's.

## Response

### Scripture ✅

- *"If the word does not come to pass or come true, that is a word which the LORD has not spoken"* (Dt 18:22).
- *"Beware of false prophets, who come to you in sheep's clothing"* (Mt 7:15).
- *"In these last days he has spoken to us by a Son"* (Heb 1:1–2). The Son is not waiting for an update.
- *"The faith which was once for all delivered to the saints"* (Jude 3).

### Catechism ✅

- CCC 65–66: Christ is the Father's definitive Word; there will be no further public revelation.
- CCC 67: private revelations do not improve or complete Christ's revelation. The Church does not accept "revelations" that claim to surpass or correct it.
- CCC 86: the Magisterium is not above the word of God but serves it, teaching only what has been handed on.

### Councils ✅

- **Vatican I, *Pastor Aeternus*** ch. 4 (1870): the Holy Spirit was promised to Peter's successors *"not so that they might, by his revelation, make known some new doctrine, but that, by his assistance, they might religiously guard and faithfully expound the revelation transmitted by the Apostles."* Even the Pope cannot do what LDS prophets claim to do.
- **Vatican II, *Dei Verbum*** §10: the teaching office listens to, guards and explains the word of God; it does not add to it.

### Doctors 🟢

- **St. John of the Cross**, quoted in the Catechism (CCC 65): *"In giving us his Son, his only Word (for he possesses no other), he spoke everything to us at once in this sole Word, and he has no more to say."* Anyone who now asks God for a new revelation, he adds, offends him, *"by not fixing his eyes entirely on Christ"* (*Ascent of Mount Carmel* II.22).
- **St. Thomas Aquinas** (*ST* II-II q.174 a.6 ad 3): after the Apostles, God still sends prophets, but *"not to declare any new doctrine of faith, but for the direction of human acts."*

### Saints 🟢

- Around 434, **St. Vincent of Lérins** gave the rule for telling true teaching from novelty: hold *"what has been believed everywhere, always, and by all"* (*Commonitorium* 2). Doctrine can grow in understanding, he said, like a child into a man, but always *"in the same doctrine, the same sense, and the same meaning"* (23). Growth, never reversal.

## One line at the door

> *"If a later prophet can reverse an earlier one, how do you know today's revelation won't be reversed tomorrow?"*

Then stop talking and let the question do its work.

🕯 **Reflect:** Do I fix my eyes on Christ, or keep looking for something new?

## From the full reference

### A LIVING PROPHET

- **Claim:** The church president receives revelation for the whole world.
- **Response:** Revelation from LDS presidents has reversed earlier revelation, on polygamy (1890) and on priesthood and race (1978). The Church's Magisterium does not add new revelation; it guards what was given once for all (Jude 3; CCC 86).

### AMOS 3:7 — GOD REVEALS THROUGH PROPHETS

- **Claim:** The Church needs a living prophet today.
- **Response:** "In these last days he has spoken to us by a Son" (Heb 1:1–2). Christ is the fullness of revelation; there is no further public revelation to wait for (CCC 65–66).
