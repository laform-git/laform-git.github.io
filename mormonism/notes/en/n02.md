# LDS-02 A Different Jesus

> **Error 2 of 10.** They use the name of Jesus constantly. The question is which Jesus.

## What they teach

Before the world began, Heavenly Father and a Heavenly Mother had countless spirit children. Jesus was the firstborn of them. Every human being was born there too, and so was Lucifer. Their manual *Gospel Principles* teaches that Lucifer was also a spirit son of Heavenly Father. So Jesus is our elder brother, and Lucifer's.

In a council in heaven, they say, Jesus offered a plan of salvation and Lucifer offered a rival plan. The Father chose Jesus, and Lucifer rebelled (Moses 4:1–4; Abraham 3:27–28). Jesus then progressed to godhood, as the Father had before him. They also identify Jesus with Jehovah of the Old Testament, and the Father with Elohim.

Some of their leaders went further and taught that the Father begot Jesus' body with Mary in the ordinary physical way. Bruce R. McConkie, an apostle, wrote it plainly in *Mormon Doctrine* (1966).

**Why it matters.** When a missionary says "We believe in Jesus Christ," he means it sincerely. But a firstborn spirit child who became a god is not the eternal Word who was God in the beginning. Same name, different Person.

## Why it is an error

- **It makes the Son a creature.** A firstborn spirit child had a beginning. Arius taught the same in the fourth century: "there was a time when the Son was not." The Church answered at Nicaea.
- **It makes Satan his equal in origin.** If Jesus and Lucifer are brothers born of the same parents, the difference between them is only in their choices, not in what they are. Scripture puts an infinite gap between the Creator and every creature, Satan included.
- **It empties the Cross.** Only God can pay the infinite debt of sin. A spirit brother who earned his divinity cannot.
- **It denies the virgin birth as the Church understands it.** Mary conceived by the Holy Spirit, not by a divine father in a body.

## Response

### Scripture ✅

- *"In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God... all things were made through him, and without him was not anything made that was made"* (Jn 1:1, 3). Lucifer was made. So he was made through Jesus. A creature cannot be the brother of his Creator.
- *"In him all things were created, in heaven and on earth, visible and invisible, whether thrones or dominions or principalities or authorities"* (Col 1:16). Paul names the angelic ranks one by one. Every one of them, the fallen ones included, was created in Christ.
- *"Before Abraham was, I am"* (Jn 8:58). Not "I was born first." I AM.
- The prophet Micah says the ruler born in Bethlehem has an origin *"from of old, from ancient days"* (Mic 5:2). Isaiah calls the child *"Mighty God, Everlasting Father"* (Is 9:6).
- *"The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you"* (Lk 1:35). The angel speaks of the Spirit, not of a bodily father.

### Catechism ✅

- CCC 241–242: the Son is eternally begotten of the Father, consubstantial with him.
- CCC 391–395: Satan is a fallen angel, a creature. His power is not infinite; he is not the equal or brother of the Son.
- CCC 464–469: Jesus is one divine Person, true God and true man.
- CCC 484–486, 496–497: Jesus was conceived by the power of the Holy Spirit, without a human or bodily father.

### Councils ✅

- **Nicaea (325):** *"begotten, not made, consubstantial with the Father."* The Council condemned anyone who says "there was a time when he was not."
- **Chalcedon (451):** one Person, the eternal Son, in two natures, divine and human.
- **Lateran IV (1215):** *"The devil and the other demons were created by God naturally good, but they became evil by their own doing."* Satan is God's creature, not his son.

### Doctors 🟢

- **St. Athanasius** spent his life fighting the claim that the Son had a beginning. Against the Arians he argued that if the Son is not eternal, then the Father was once without his Word and his Wisdom, which is absurd.
- **St. Hilary of Poitiers**, exiled to Asia Minor for defending Nicaea, wrote *On the Trinity* there. The Son is born of the Father from all eternity, he taught, with no time before his birth, because the Father was never without the Son.

### Saints 🟢

- In 155, **St. Polycarp**, bishop of Smyrna and a disciple of St. John, was ordered to curse Christ and go free. He answered: *"Eighty and six years I have served him, and he never did me any wrong. How can I blaspheme my King who saved me?"* (*Martyrdom of Polycarp* 9). He was burned alive. He died for a King, not for an elder brother.

## One line at the door

> *"John says that without Jesus nothing was made that was made. Was Lucifer made? Then how can he be Jesus' brother?"*

Then stop talking and let the question do its work.

🕯 **Reflect:** If Christ is eternal God, what does that change about the weight of his death for me?

## From the full reference

### Jesus Christ

**LDS teaching:** Jesus is the firstborn spirit child of Heavenly Father and a Heavenly Mother, the spirit brother of Lucifer and of every human being, who progressed to godhood.

**Catholic teaching:** ✅ "Begotten, not made, consubstantial with the Father" (Nicene Creed). The Word was God from eternity (Jn 1:1–3). Christ is no creature, and so in no sense a brother of Lucifer, a fallen angel (CCC 391–393). Nicaea condemned Arius for making the Son a created, advanced being.
