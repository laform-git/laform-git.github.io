# LDS-17 Quick Reference — LDS vs. Catholic

> The whole series on one page: what they claim, what the Church teaches, and where to find it.

## Quick Reference — LDS vs. Catholic

### GOD

- **Claim:** God was once a man with a body of flesh and bone.
- **Response:** God is eternal, uncreated, unchanging spirit, never a man. ✅ CCC 202, 212; Jn 4:24; Ps 90:2

### JESUS CHRIST

- **Claim:** Jesus is the spirit brother of Lucifer who became a god.
- **Response:** Eternally begotten, not made, consubstantial with the Father. All things, Lucifer included, were made through him. ✅ Nicene Creed; Jn 1:1–3

### TRINITY

- **Claim:** Three separate gods.
- **Response:** One God in three Persons, one divine substance. ✅ CCC 253–256; Mt 28:19

### HUMAN DESTINY

- **Claim:** Humans can become gods.
- **Response:** We share God's life by grace; the Creator–creature line is eternal. ✅ CCC 300, 460

### THE CHURCH

- **Claim:** Total apostasy; Joseph Smith restored it.
- **Response:** The gates of hell never prevailed. ✅ Mt 16:18; CCC 811–816

### SCRIPTURE

- **Claim:** The Bible is corrupt; new scripture was needed.
- **Response:** Revelation is complete in Christ; the Book of Mormon copies the KJV and has been revised. 🟢 CCC 66, 120

### PRIESTHOOD

- **Claim:** Angels restored the Aaronic and Melchizedek priesthoods in 1829.
- **Response:** Christ's one priesthood never left; it passes by the laying on of hands from the Apostles. ✅ Heb 7:24; CCC 1576

### SALVATION

- **Claim:** Exaltation through temple ordinances.
- **Response:** Grace through faith working in love, in the sacraments Christ instituted. 🟢 Gal 5:6; CCC 1987

### BEFORE BIRTH AND AFTER DEATH

- **Claim:** We lived before birth; nearly everyone reaches one of three kingdoms of glory.
- **Response:** God creates each soul at conception; there are two final destinies. ✅ CCC 366, 1033; Mt 25:46

### PROPHETS

- **Claim:** A living prophet receives new revelation for the world.
- **Response:** Smith's prophecies failed and later presidents reversed earlier revelations. Public revelation closed with Christ. ✅ Dt 18:22; Heb 1:1–2; CCC 66–67

### BAPTISM

- **Claim:** Only LDS baptism has authority.
- **Response:** LDS baptism is not Christian baptism, because it invokes three gods. Converts are baptized absolutely. ✅ CDF, 5 June 2001
