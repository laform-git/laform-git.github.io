## Script

### Scene 1 · The myth

You were told the Church kept the world in the dark.

### Scene 2 · The questions

So why did a Roman emperor who hated Christians complain that they fed not only their own poor, but his as well?

Why was the Big Bang theory first proposed by a Catholic priest?

### Scene 3 · More questions

Who invented the hospital, and why?

And who, sixteen centuries ago, called slavery itself a sin?

### Scene 4 · The evidence

This series follows the evidence: monks saving the books of the ancient world, cathedral schools growing into the first universities, a bishop building a city of care for lepers no one would touch.

Women given the right to say no at the altar. Criminals offered a second chance. Martyrs who died for strangers.

### Scene 5 · The witnesses

Most of the witnesses here aren't Catholic. Some are the Church's enemies.

### Scene 6 · Honesty

And we won't hide the failures: the Crusades, the Inquisition, the abuse crisis. You'll get the honest accounting too.

### Scene 7 · The question

Then one question: what made ordinary people do all this?

### Scene 8 · Roll call (one title on screen per beat)

Thirteen chapters.

Keepers of the Books. The School and the University. Faith and Science.

The Invention of the Hospital. Charity and the Poor. The End of Slavery.

Mercy and Second Chances. Marriage and the Dignity of Women. Mission and Martyrs.

Beauty. The Hard Questions. And the Source.

One tree.

### Scene 9 · Close

By their fruits you will know them.

Get to know the Church.

**Final card:** THE CHURCH · Get to know her. · Chapter One begins now.
