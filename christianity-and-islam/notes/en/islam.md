# Islam

*"The monotheistic religion that traces itself to the preaching of Muhammad in seventh-century Arabia and holds the Qur'an to be God's final revelation."*

Muslims believe in one God, Creator and Judge, and hold that Muhammad (c. 570–632) is the last of the prophets. Their practice centers on the Five Pillars: the profession of faith (*shahada*), prayer five times a day (*salat*), almsgiving (*zakat*), the fast of Ramadan (*sawm*) and pilgrimage to Mecca (*hajj*). They honor Abraham, Moses and Jesus as prophets and hold Mary in high regard, but deny the Trinity and the divinity of Christ, and the Qur'an denies that Jesus was crucified (4:157).

The Church's own teaching is set out in *Nostra Aetate* 3: she regards Muslims with esteem, as those who adore the one God, living and subsistent, merciful and almighty, who revere Jesus as a prophet, honor Mary, and await the day of judgment; and she urges that past quarrels be set aside for mutual understanding. CCC 841 (following *Lumen Gentium* 16) adds that Muslims, professing to hold the faith of Abraham, adore with us the one, merciful God.
