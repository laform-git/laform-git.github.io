# Crusades

## What Preceded the First Crusade (1096)

The Crusades did not emerge in a vacuum. Starting in the 7th century, Arab and Islamic armies had expanded across an enormous region — lands that had been Christian for centuries.

**By 711 AD — before the First Crusade — these territories had been conquered:**

- Saudi Arabia (origin of the expansion)
- Syria (637), Iraq (637), Egypt (641)
- Libya, Tunisia (698), Algeria, Morocco (709)
- Spain and Portugal (711) — Islamic forces crossed into Western Europe
- Iran (651), Jordan, Israel/Palestine (636), Lebanon
- Afghanistan, Pakistan/Sindh (711)
- Armenia, Cyprus

This represents the loss of the historic heartlands of Christianity — Jerusalem, Antioch, Alexandria, Carthage — to military conquest over roughly 80 years.

## Why the Crusades Were Called

In 1095, Pope Urban II called the First Crusade in response to a request from the Byzantine Emperor Alexios I, whose empire was being overrun by Seljuk Turks. The goal was to recapture Jerusalem and restore access to Christian holy sites, which had been blocked or made dangerous for pilgrims.

The Crusades were not initiated as an unprovoked offensive — they were a military response, however complex and at times brutal in execution, to centuries of Islamic expansion into Christian lands.

## Historical Nuance

The Crusades were mixed in their conduct and motivation. Alongside genuine religious zeal were political ambition, violence against Jews and Eastern Christians, and military failures. They cannot be reduced to either pure heroism or pure villainy.

Context matters: judging 11th–13th century military campaigns by 21st century moral standards, without accounting for the geopolitical realities that provoked them, distorts more than it illuminates.

## Practical Takeaway

Knowing this history is essential for Catholic apologetics. The "Crusades were unprovoked Christian aggression" narrative ignores four centuries of Islamic military conquest of Christian lands. The more accurate question is: what took so long?
