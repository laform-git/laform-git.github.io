# Monotheism

*"The belief in one God — the foundational affirmation of Jewish, Christian, and Islamic faith."*

Israel's monotheism was revolutionary in the ancient world: one God, not a pantheon; a personal God, not an impersonal force; a creator God who stands outside and above creation, not identified with it.

Christian monotheism is Trinitarian: there is one God who is Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. This is not a compromise of monotheism but its deepest revelation — the one God is an eternal communion of love. The unity of God is the unity of three Persons in one nature.
