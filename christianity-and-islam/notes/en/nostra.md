# NostraAetate

*"Vatican II's Declaration on the Relation of the Church to Non-Christian Religions, promulgated by Paul VI on 28 October 1965."*

The shortest document of the Council, it begins from the unity of the human race, which has one origin and one final end in God. It teaches that the Church "rejects nothing that is true and holy" in other religions, which "often reflect a ray of that Truth which enlightens all men," while she remains bound to proclaim Christ, "the way, the truth and the life" (n. 2).

Its sections treat Hinduism and Buddhism (n. 2); Muslims, whom the Church regards with esteem as worshippers of the one God (n. 3); the Jewish people, rejecting the charge that Jews then or now bear collective guilt for Christ's death and deploring antisemitism (n. 4); and universal brotherhood, condemning discrimination on grounds of race, color, condition of life or religion (n. 5).
