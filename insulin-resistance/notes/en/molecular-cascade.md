# Insulin Resistance — Molecular Cascade

## Why This Matters

Insulin resistance is not a broken lock — it is a jammed chain of dominoes inside the cell. Understanding exactly where the chain jams explains why berberine, exercise, and fasting work at the molecular level.

---

## The Players — Quick Reference

| Term | Plain English |
| --- | --- |
| Insulin Receptor (IR) | The lock on the cell surface |
| Phosphorylation | Sticking a phosphate tag onto a protein — turns it on or off |
| IRS-1 | First relay inside the cell — the first domino |
| PI3K | Enzyme that lights the assemble-here flare |
| PIP2 / PIP3 | Membrane signals — PIP3 is the active flare |
| PDK1 + mTORC2 | Two enzymes that together fully activate Akt |
| Akt | Grand central station — activates all downstream effects |
| GLUT4 | The actual glucose door in muscle and fat cells |
| AS160 | Brake keeping GLUT4 closed — Akt releases it |
| GSK3 | Blocks glycogen storage — Akt switches it off |
| FOXO1 | Gene switch telling liver to make sugar — Akt ejects it |
| mTORC1 / SREBP | Build fat and protein — Akt fires them up |
| DAG | Fat byproduct that jams IRS-1 |
| Ceramide | Fat byproduct that disables Akt |
| PKCθ/ε | Enzyme activated by DAG — puts wrong tag on IRS-1 |
| PP2A | Enzyme activated by ceramide — strips Akt's activation tags |

## The Cascade — Step by Step

### Step 1 — Insulin binds the receptor

- Insulin binds the two α-subunits on the outside of the cell
- Changes receptor shape, bringing the two inner β-subunits together

### Step 2 — Autophosphorylation (lock activates itself)

- The β-subunits tag each other with phosphate groups on tyrosine residues
- This is the literal molecular moment of the key turning the lock
- ✅ Rock solid: textbook receptor tyrosine kinase mechanism

### Step 3 — IRS-1 docking (first domino)

- Active receptor phosphorylates IRS-1
- Tyrosine-phosphate tags act as Velcro docking stations for the next proteins
- Most critical relay point — if jammed here, the whole chain fails

### Step 4 — PI3K lights the flare

- PI3K docks onto IRS-1, converts PIP2 into PIP3
- PIP3 = the assemble-here signal on the inner cell wall
- PTEN erases PIP3 — the natural off-switch

### Step 5 — Akt fully activated

- PIP3 recruits PDK1 and Akt to the membrane
- PDK1 tags Akt at Thr308; mTORC2 tags Akt at Ser473
- Both tags required = Akt fully ON
- ✅ Rock solid: dual phosphorylation is textbook

### Step 6 — Akt fires 4 payoffs simultaneously

**Payoff 1 — Glucose doors open**

- Akt switches off brake AS160
- GLUT4 vesicles fuse to cell surface — glucose flows in
- ✅ Rock solid

**Payoff 2 — Glycogen storage**

- Akt blocks GSK3 → glycogen synthesis ramps up
- ✅ Rock solid

**Payoff 3 — Liver shuts off glucose production**

- Akt ejects FOXO1 from nucleus → genes PEPCK and G6Pase go silent
- ✅ Rock solid

**Payoff 4 — Fat and protein synthesis**

- Akt fires mTORC1 and SREBP-1c → storage and building mode
- This branch stays partially active even in insulin resistance — IR blocks glucose uptake AND keeps promoting fat storage simultaneously
- ✅ Rock solid

### Step 7 — The growth branch (MAPK, parallel)

- IRS-1 also feeds: Grb2 → Ras → Raf → MEK → ERK
- Handles cell growth, not glucose — stays active even when metabolic branch fails

## Where Insulin Resistance Breaks the Chain

### Saboteur 1 — DAG jams Step 3 (IRS-1)

- Ectopic fat (fat inside muscle or liver cells) produces DAG
- DAG activates PKCθ (muscle) or PKCε (liver)
- PKC slaps an inhibitory serine phosphate on IRS-1 — the wrong tag
- IRS-1 can no longer relay the signal — first domino never falls
- 🟢 Strong evidence

### Saboteur 2 — Ceramide disables Step 5 (Akt)

- Ectopic fat also produces ceramide
- Ceramide activates PP2A, which strips activating phosphates OFF Akt
- Even if Steps 1–4 worked, the signal dies here
- 🟢 Strong evidence

### The Cruel Irony

- Both saboteurs come from fat in the wrong place
- Fat-storage branch (Payoff 4) keeps running while glucose handling is jammed
- IR simultaneously blocks glucose uptake AND continues promoting fat storage

---

## One-Breath Summary

Insulin binds → receptor self-activates → IRS-1 tagged → PI3K lights PIP3 flare → Akt double-tagged ON → GLUT4 opens + glycogen stored + liver shut off + fat built. Resistance = DAG jams IRS-1 at Step 3, ceramide kills Akt at Step 5. Both caused by fat in the wrong place.

---

## Why Berberine, Exercise & Fasting All Work

- All three activate AMPK — a parallel bypass that circumvents the jammed cascade
- AMPK drives GLUT4 to the surface independently of IRS-1 and Akt
- This is why they restore insulin sensitivity even when the top of the cascade is broken
- See: [[Berberine — Mechanisms & Timing]]

---

## Key Correction

> The receptor (lock) usually works fine. The jam is inside — at IRS-1 (Step 3, DAG) or Akt (Step 5, ceramide). The key fits. The message gets lost.

---

## Practical Takeaway

Draining ectopic fat from muscle and liver — through exercise, caloric deficit, or reducing refined carbohydrate load — is the most direct way to unjam both saboteur points and restore insulin sensitivity.

## Addendum (2026-07-31): Selective Insulin Resistance — Why IR Makes MORE Fat, Not Less

**The paradox**

If insulin's job includes building fat, shouldn't resistance to insulin mean *less* fat gets made? It would — if resistance were uniform across the whole cascade. It isn't.

**The mechanism: resistance is pathway-specific, not all-or-nothing**

Looking back at Step 6 above: Akt fires 4 payoffs from one signal. In insulin resistance, the saboteurs (DAG at IRS-1, ceramide at Akt) don't disable all 4 payoffs equally.

- **Payoff 1 (GLUT4/glucose uptake)** — becomes resistant early and severely. This is the pathway most sensitive to the DAG/ceramide jam. 🟢
- **Payoff 4 (mTORC1/SREBP-1c → fat and protein synthesis)** — stays largely insulin-sensitive, sometimes indefinitely. This is documented in the literature as **"selective hepatic insulin resistance."** ✅ The liver becomes deaf to insulin's glucose-control message while remaining fully receptive to insulin's fat-building message.

**Why this makes things worse, step by step**

1. Payoff 1 is jammed → blood glucose stays elevated after meals.
2. Pancreas responds the only way it can: secretes **more insulin** to try to force the resistant glucose pathway open (compensatory hyperinsulinemia).
3. That extra insulin can't fix the jammed glucose pathway (Steps 3/5 are still blocked) — but it **fully reaches the still-sensitive fat-building pathway** (Payoff 4, SREBP-1c → ACC/FAS → de novo lipogenesis, see Lipid Triad note).
4. Net result: elevated blood sugar **and** elevated triglyceride/fat production simultaneously — not despite the resistance, but *because of* the compensatory response to it.

**The analogy**

Two doors, same key. The glucose-door's lock wears out (resistant) — so the body cuts more keys (more insulin) to force it. The fat-door's lock never wore out. Now it's getting hit with far more keys than normal, and swings open harder and more often than before the resistance ever started.

**Practical takeaway**

This is why fixing insulin resistance can't be partial or glucose-only — as long as any compensatory hyperinsulinemia persists, it keeps fueling the fat-synthesis branch even on days when fasting glucose looks fine.
