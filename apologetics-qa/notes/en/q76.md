# Q76 · Are the children of parents who get an annulment considered illegitimate?

**Short answer**
No. Church law states that children born of a marriage entered in good faith, even if later declared null, are legitimate.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ A marriage entered in good faith by at least one party is a "putative marriage", and its children are legitimate (canons 1061 §3, 1137).
- ✅ A declaration of nullity has no effect on the parents' duties toward their children.

**Why it matters**
Children are never the "mistake" of a failed marriage. They are gifts of God with full dignity, and their parents' obligations continue.
