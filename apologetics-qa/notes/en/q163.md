# Q163 · Why does God command us to love and worship Him? Isn't that selfish of Him?

**Short answer**
God gains nothing from our worship; He is already perfectly happy. He commands it because worshiping the true God is what is right and what makes us whole. Loving Him is our happiness, not His need.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Adoration acknowledges God as Creator and Savior, and frees us from turning in on ourselves and from idolatry (CCC 2096–2097).
- ✅ The Church prays: "although you have no need of our praise, yet our thanksgiving is itself your gift, since our praises add nothing to your greatness but profit us for salvation" (Roman Missal, Common Preface IV).

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 When we see something beautiful, praising it completes our joy (C. S. Lewis).
- 🟢 Everyone worships something: money, success, popularity. Worshiping the true God puts everything else in its proper place.
- 🟢 A doctor who tells you to breathe isn't selfish; you need air.

**For class**
What do people around you "worship" without calling it worship? How does that affect them?
