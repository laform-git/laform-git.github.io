# Q130 · Abortion is necessary in overpopulated areas and avoids the financial and emotional burden a child may bring.

**Short answer**
Hardship is real and deserves help. But we don't solve financial or social problems by ending the lives of the people who seem to cause them. And many countries now face falling birth rates, not overpopulation.

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 If a two-year-old became a financial burden, no one would accept ending the child's life. The question is again whether the unborn child is one of us.
- 🟢 Fertility in most of the world has fallen sharply; many countries are now below replacement level and worry about aging populations.
- 🟢 Hunger is mostly caused by poverty, war and poor distribution, not lack of space.

**What the Church teaches**
✅ Population questions must be addressed respecting human dignity and the rights of families, never by coercion or killing (CCC 2372; *Populorum Progressio* 37).
