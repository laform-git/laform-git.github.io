# Q131 · Abortion is necessary for women's equality.

**Short answer**
Women's equality is a just goal. But equality that requires a mother to end her child's life to compete with men accepts a workplace and society built for men. True equality makes room for motherhood.

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 Early feminists such as Susan B. Anthony and Elizabeth Cady Stanton are often cited as opposing abortion 🟡 (some quotes attributed to them are disputed).
- 🟢 Equal pay, maternity leave, flexible work, childcare and education support help women without setting mother against child.
- 🟢 Half of aborted children are girls; in some countries sex-selective abortion targets girls specifically.

**What the Church teaches**
✅ The Church affirms the equal dignity of women and calls for social structures that respect their roles at home and in public life (CCC 2334; *Mulieris Dignitatem*; John Paul II, *Letter to Women*, 1995).
