# Q87 · Why do Catholics say repetitive prayers when Jesus warned against "vain repetition" (Mt 6:7)?

**Short answer**
Jesus warned against *vain* or empty babbling, like pagans who thought many words would force the gods to listen. He did not forbid repetition from the heart; He Himself repeated the same prayer.

**In Scripture**
- 🟢 Right after the warning, Jesus teaches a prayer to repeat: the Our Father (Mt 6:9–13).
- 🟢 In Gethsemane He prayed three times, "saying the same words" (Mt 26:44).
- 🟢 Psalm 136 repeats "for His steadfast love endures for ever" 26 times.
- 🟢 In heaven the creatures "never cease to sing, Holy, holy, holy" (Rev 4:8).

**What the Church teaches**
✅ Repeated prayers like the Jesus Prayer can lead to deep, continuous prayer of the heart (CCC 2667–2668).

**Why it matters**
Repetition is how love speaks. A couple says "I love you" every day; it is not vain unless it is said without meaning it.
