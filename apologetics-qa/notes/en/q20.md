# Q20 · Do miracles really happen?

**Short answer**
Yes. A miracle is an event that only God's direct action can explain. If God exists and created nature, He is not prevented from acting in it.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Jesus' miracles are signs that the Kingdom has come and that He is the Son of God (CCC 547–549).
- ✅ The Church still investigates miracles carefully, for example in canonization causes, with medical boards at Lourdes and in Rome.

**Evidence and reasoning**
- 🟢 Rejecting miracles in advance assumes there is no God, which is the very question at issue.
- 🟢 The Lourdes Medical Bureau has examined thousands of claimed cures; the Church has recognized only about 70 as miracles, showing how strict the process is.
- 🟡 Not every claimed miracle is real; the Church is cautious and many claims are rejected.

**Why it matters**
Christianity rests on a miracle: the Resurrection. If miracles are impossible, the faith falls; if they are possible, the evidence must be weighed.
