# Q102 · Why did the Church wage the Crusades against Muslims?

**Short answer**
The Crusades were, at their origin, a response to centuries of Muslim conquest of Christian lands and an urgent plea from the Byzantine emperor. They also included serious crimes, which the Church has acknowledged.

**Context**
- 🟢 From the 7th century, Muslim armies conquered the Christian Middle East, North Africa and Spain.
- 🟢 In 1071 the Seljuk Turks defeated the Byzantines at Manzikert and took most of Asia Minor; pilgrims to Jerusalem faced violence.
- 🟢 In 1095 Pope Urban II answered Emperor Alexios I's call for help (Council of Clermont).

**The dark side**
- 🟢 Massacres at Jerusalem (1099), attacks on Jewish communities in the Rhineland (1096), and the sack of Christian Constantinople (1204), which Pope Innocent III condemned.
- ✅ In 2000 Saint John Paul II asked forgiveness for violence committed by Christians in the service of truth.

**Why it matters**
History is more complex than either "pure holy war" or "pure aggression". Honest Catholics can explain the causes and admit the sins.
