# Q88 · Where did the Hail Mary and the Rosary come from?

**Short answer**
The first half of the Hail Mary is straight from the Gospel; the second half is the Church's prayer asking Mary's intercession. The Rosary grew in the Middle Ages as a way to pray and meditate on the life of Christ with Mary.

**History**
- 🟢 "Hail, full of grace, the Lord is with you" (Lk 1:28) and "Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb" (Lk 1:42).
- 🟢 "Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners…" was added over time; the full form was set in the 1568 Roman Breviary.
- 🟢 Laypeople who could not pray the 150 Psalms prayed 150 Hail Marys instead. Tradition links the Rosary to Saint Dominic (13th century) 🟡.
- 🟢 Pope Pius V promoted it in 1569; Saint John Paul II added the Luminous Mysteries in 2002 (*Rosarium Virginis Mariae*).

**What the Church teaches**
✅ The Rosary is a summary of the Gospel and a school of contemplation (CCC 2678, 2708).
