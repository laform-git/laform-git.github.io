# Q15 · How do we know that anything invisible exists? There's no way to prove it.

**Short answer**
We rely on invisible realities all the time: thoughts, love, numbers, logic, gravity, even other people's minds. We know them by their effects and by reason, not by sight.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The Creed confesses God as maker "of all things visible and invisible" (CCC 325).
- ✅ The invisible things of God are known through the things He has made (Rom 1:20; CCC 32).

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 "Only what can be seen is real" cannot itself be seen or measured, so it fails its own test.
- 🟢 Science itself infers invisible things from their effects: fields, forces, dark matter.
- 🟢 You cannot see your friend's love, yet you rightly believe in it from what they do.

**Why it matters**
Proof is wider than seeing. Faith deals with invisible realities, but not with unreasonable ones.
