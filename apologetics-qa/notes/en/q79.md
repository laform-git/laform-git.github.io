# Q79 · Where is the Sacrament of Holy Orders in the Bible?

**Short answer**
Jesus chose the Twelve, gave them the power to celebrate the Eucharist ("Do this", Lk 22:19) and to forgive sins (Jn 20:22–23). The apostles passed on this ministry by laying on hands.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Holy Orders has three degrees: bishop, priest and deacon (CCC 1536, 1554).
- ✅ It gives a permanent character and configures the man to Christ the Head (CCC 1581–1582).

**In Scripture**
- 🟢 The Seven are ordained with prayer and laying on of hands (Acts 6:6), the origin of deacons.
- 🟢 "Do not neglect the gift you have, which was given you… when the council of elders laid their hands upon you" (1 Tim 4:14).
- 🟢 Paul and Barnabas "appointed elders (*presbyteroi*) for them in every church" (Acts 14:23).
- 🟢 "Priest" comes from the Greek *presbyteros*, "elder".

**Why it matters**
Without priests there is no Eucharist and no sacramental absolution. Pray for vocations.
