# Q129 · If abortion is outlawed, rich women will travel to get abortions, and poor women will be stuck with unwanted babies or get dangerous back-alley abortions.

**Short answer**
Inequality is a real problem, but the solution is to help poor mothers, not to make it easier to end their children's lives. A law protecting life is not unjust because some people evade it.

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 The rich can evade many laws (taxes, labor laws); we don't abolish those laws for that reason.
- 🟢 If the unborn child is a human being, poverty does not make ending their life right.
- 🟢 Real justice for poor women means healthcare, housing, jobs, childcare and support, the very things pro-life groups and Church charities provide.

**What the Church teaches**
✅ The Church calls for laws protecting the unborn together with real solidarity with mothers in difficulty (CCC 2273; *Evangelium Vitae* 87–90).
