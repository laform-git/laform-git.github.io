# Q159 · If God is love and loves everyone, how can hell exist? How can God love people in hell?

**Short answer**
Because love does not force. God created us free so that we could love Him; that same freedom means we can refuse Him forever. Hell is God respecting a final "no". He never stops willing the good of those who refuse Him.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ "We cannot be united with God unless we freely choose to love Him" (CCC 1033).
- ✅ "God predestines no one to go to hell" (CCC 1037).
- ✅ God "desires all men to be saved" (1 Tim 2:4).

**Reasoning**
🟢 C. S. Lewis: "The doors of hell are locked on the inside." In the end there are those who say to God "Thy will be done", and those to whom God says "thy will be done".

**Why it matters**
Hell shows how seriously God takes our freedom and our love. Heaven is not forced on anyone.
