# Q3 · What is metaphysics, and why does it matter?

**Short answer**
Metaphysics is the study of being as such: what it means for anything to exist, and the first causes of everything. It matters because every other field quietly assumes answers to its questions.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The Church holds that reason can rise from created things to their first cause (CCC 31–36).
- ✅ Saint John Paul II called for a philosophy of "genuinely metaphysical range", able to move from what we see to what grounds it (*Fides et Ratio* 83).
- ✅ Key ideas used in theology come from metaphysics: substance and accident (the Eucharist), nature and person (the Trinity, Christ), act and potency (God as pure act).

**Objection**
"Only science gives real knowledge." 🟢 That claim is itself not a scientific finding; it is a metaphysical claim. Science studies how things behave; metaphysics asks why there is anything at all.

**Why it matters**
Without metaphysics, faith becomes feeling and science becomes the only judge of truth.

**For class**
Why is there something rather than nothing? Can a lab experiment answer that?
