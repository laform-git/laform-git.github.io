# Q119 · Can natural family planning (NFP) be misused?

**Short answer**
Yes. NFP is a method, and like any tool it can be used with a selfish attitude. Couples are called to be generous toward life and to avoid pregnancy only for serious or just reasons, discerned together before God.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Spacing births is legitimate for "just reasons", not from selfishness (CCC 2368).
- ✅ *Humanae Vitae* speaks of "serious motives" arising from physical or psychological conditions or external circumstances (HV 10, 16).
- ✅ Spouses decide the number of children; the Church does not set a number (CCC 2368; *Gaudium et Spes* 50).

**Examples of just reasons**
🟢 Health risks, serious financial hardship, the care needs of other children.

**Why it matters**
Openness to life is an attitude of the heart, not a calculation. NFP used generously keeps that openness.
