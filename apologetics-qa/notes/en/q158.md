# Q158 · If hell is the complete absence of God, but God is being itself, how can hell exist?

**Short answer**
Good point: nothing can exist without God holding it in being. Hell is not the absence of God's sustaining power, but the absence of communion with Him: being kept in existence while having refused His love.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Hell is "the state of definitive self-exclusion from communion with God and the blessed" (CCC 1033).
- ✅ Its chief punishment is eternal separation from God, in whom alone we find the life and happiness we were made for (CCC 1035).

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 God is present to everything by sustaining it, but present by friendship only to those who welcome Him.
- 🟡 Some theologians describe hell as God's love experienced as torment by those who reject it, like light that hurts eyes that refuse it.

**Why it matters**
Hell is not God's revenge; it is the result of refusing the only source of joy.
