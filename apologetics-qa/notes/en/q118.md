# Q118 · Isn't contraception the same as natural family planning, since they have the same purpose and result?

**Short answer**
No. A good goal does not make every means good. Contraception changes the act itself to remove its life-giving power; NFP respects the act and simply chooses not to engage in it at certain times.

**What the Church teaches**
✅ Using the infertile periods is legitimate; contraception "deliberately frustrates" the natural power to generate life (*Humanae Vitae* 16; CCC 2370).

**Comparison**
- 🟢 Two students want good grades: one studies, one cheats. Same goal, different acts.
- 🟢 Someone who wants to lose weight can eat less, or eat and then purge. Same goal; one respects the body, the other does not.
- ✅ The difference is between *not doing* something and *doing something and then undoing its meaning*.

**Why it matters**
Morality is not only about results but about what we actually choose to do.
