# Q141 · What is the ethical treatment of embryos? Is surrogate motherhood permitted?

**Short answer**
Human embryos must be treated as human beings: never created to be discarded, frozen indefinitely, experimented on or selected. Surrogate motherhood is not permitted, because it separates motherhood from marriage and treats a child as a product to be contracted.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The embryo must be "defended in its integrity, cared for, and healed, as far as possible, like any other human being" (CCC 2274).
- ✅ Freezing embryos exposes them to grave risks and is incompatible with their dignity (*Dignitas Personae* 18–19).
- ✅ Surrogate motherhood is contrary to the unity of marriage and the dignity of procreation (*Donum Vitae* II,A,3; CCC 2376).
- ✅ Pope Francis has called for a universal ban on surrogacy (Address to diplomats, January 2024).

**Hard reality**
🟡 Hundreds of thousands of frozen embryos exist. Whether "embryo adoption" is permissible is debated; *Dignitas Personae* raised serious concerns without a final judgment.
