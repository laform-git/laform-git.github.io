# Q21 · Why is the Old Testament God so jealous, harsh and cruel?

**Short answer**
It is the same God in both Testaments. The Old Testament shows God patiently teaching a people step by step, in a violent ancient world. His "jealousy" is the love of a husband who will not share His bride with idols.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The Old Testament is God's word and keeps its value, though it contains "matters imperfect and provisional" (CCC 121–122, *Dei Verbum* 15).
- ✅ God's revelation is gradual; it reaches fullness in Christ (CCC 53, 65).
- ✅ The same Old Testament calls God "merciful and gracious, slow to anger" (Ex 34:6).

**Keys to reading**
- 🟢 God's "jealousy" means faithful, exclusive covenant love, not insecurity.
- 🟢 Ancient writers used strong images of anger to express how seriously God takes sin.
- 🟢 Read the Old Testament in the light of Jesus, who shows the Father's face (Jn 14:9).

**Why it matters**
Caricatures of a "mean Old Testament God" ignore the many texts of tenderness, like Hosea 11 and Isaiah 49:15.
