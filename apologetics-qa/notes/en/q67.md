# Q67 · Why isn't eating the Eucharist cannibalism if it is really the Body of Christ?

**Short answer**
Cannibalism means killing a person and eating dead flesh in its natural form. In the Eucharist, Christ is alive, glorified and whole; He is not killed, cut up or diminished, and He is present sacramentally, under the appearances of bread and wine.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Christ is present whole and entire in each part of the host; breaking it does not divide Him (CCC 1377).
- ✅ It is the risen Christ we receive, who "dies no more" (Rom 6:9).

**Key differences**
- 🟢 Cannibalism destroys a person; Communion unites us to a living Person.
- 🟢 Cannibalism takes life; Communion gives life (Jn 6:57).
- 🟢 The appearances (taste, look, chemistry) of bread remain; the substance is Christ.

**History**
🟢 Pagan Romans accused early Christians of cannibalism, which shows the early Church really did believe in the Real Presence.
