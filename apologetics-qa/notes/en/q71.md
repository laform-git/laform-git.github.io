# Q71 · What is required for a Catholic marriage to be valid and sacramental?

**Short answer**
A valid Catholic marriage needs a man and a woman who are free to marry, who give full and free consent, and who marry in the proper form, normally before a priest or deacon and two witnesses. It is a sacrament when both spouses are baptized.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Consent is the indispensable element; it must be free, informed and not forced (CCC 1626–1628).
- ✅ Catholics must normally marry in the Church's form (CCC 1631; canon 1108).
- ✅ Spouses must intend what marriage is: permanent, faithful and open to children (CCC 1664).

**Things that block validity (examples)**
🟢 An existing marriage, being too closely related, being under the legal age, or excluding children or permanence from one's intention.

**Why it matters**
These conditions protect the couple. A marriage is a covenant, and both people must truly know and want what they promise.
