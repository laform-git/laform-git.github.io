# Q38 · Why don't Catholics accept sola scriptura?

**Short answer**
Because the Bible itself does not teach it. Christ's word reaches us through Scripture and Tradition, interpreted by the Church He founded.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Scripture and Sacred Tradition form one deposit of faith; the Magisterium interprets it (CCC 80–85, 97).

**Reasons**
- 🟢 Scripture points to oral tradition: "Hold to the traditions you were taught, either by word of mouth or by letter" (2 Thess 2:15).
- 🟢 Scripture calls the Church "the pillar and foundation of the truth" (1 Tim 3:15).
- 🟢 The Bible does not contain its own table of contents. The canon was discerned by the Church over centuries.
- 🟢 Christians who share *sola scriptura* still disagree deeply on baptism, the Eucharist and salvation, which shows the need for an authoritative interpreter (2 Pet 3:16).

**Why it matters**
Catholics love Scripture; the Church gave it to the world. They simply read it as part of the family that wrote and preserved it.
