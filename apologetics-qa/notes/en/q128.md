# Q128 · If abortion is banned, women will die. What about saving the mother's life, or an ectopic pregnancy?

**Short answer**
The Church never asks a mother to die. Treatments needed to save her life are allowed even if, as an unintended side effect, the child dies. What is forbidden is directly killing the child as the means.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Direct abortion is always wrong; but medical treatment aimed at curing the mother, with the child's death foreseen but not intended, can be permitted (principle of double effect; CCC 2263, 1756).
- ✅ Doctors must try to save both lives whenever possible.

**Examples**
- 🟢 Cancer of the uterus: removing the uterus to save the mother is allowed, even if the child cannot survive.
- 🟢 Ectopic pregnancy (the embryo implanted in the fallopian tube): removing the damaged section of tube is accepted by Catholic moralists.
- 🟡 Whether the drug methotrexate is acceptable in ectopic cases is debated among Catholic ethicists.
- 🟢 In late pregnancy, early delivery can save the mother while giving the baby a chance.
