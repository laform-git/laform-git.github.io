# Q100 · Is the theory of evolution incompatible with Catholic doctrine?

**Short answer**
No, if evolution is understood as a scientific account of how living bodies developed. What the Church rejects is the philosophical claim that everything is the product of blind chance with no Creator, and the idea that the human soul evolved from matter.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Pius XII (*Humani Generis*, 1950) allowed study of the evolution of the human body, while holding that souls are created directly by God.
- ✅ Saint John Paul II (1996) said new knowledge leads to recognizing evolution as "more than a hypothesis".
- ✅ Each human soul is created immediately by God (CCC 366).
- ✅ God is the Creator of all, and creation includes secondary causes (CCC 306–308).

**Open questions**
🟡 How to reconcile the doctrine of one first human couple and original sin with population genetics is still discussed by theologians.

**Why it matters**
A Catholic can study biology fully and still believe God made everything and gave each person a spiritual soul.
