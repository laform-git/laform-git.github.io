# Q22 · Why did God order genocide and the destruction of entire cities?

**Short answer**
These are among the hardest texts in Scripture, and honest Catholics admit it. The Church reads them as part of a gradual revelation, written in the language of ancient warfare, and always in light of Christ, who forbids killing the innocent.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Revelation is progressive; the Old Testament contains "imperfect and provisional" elements (CCC 122).
- ✅ The direct killing of the innocent is always gravely wrong (CCC 2261).
- ✅ Christ is the key to reading all of Scripture (CCC 129).

**Ways scholars read these texts**
- 🟡 Ancient war accounts often used exaggerated "total destruction" language; other passages in the same books show the peoples still living there (compare Joshua 10–11 with Judges 1).
- 🟡 Some Church Fathers, such as Origen, read the conquests spiritually, as the soul's war against sin.
- 🟡 Others see them as God's judgment on extreme evils such as child sacrifice, carried out in a specific time that cannot be repeated.

**Why it matters**
No one can use these texts to justify violence today; Christ's command is to love enemies (Mt 5:44).
