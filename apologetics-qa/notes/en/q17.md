# Q17 · Is the Bible inspired by God?

**Short answer**
Yes. God is the principal author of Scripture, and He used human authors who wrote as true authors, with their own style, culture and skills.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ "All Scripture is inspired by God" (2 Tim 3:16). The books of both Testaments, whole and entire, have God as their author (CCC 105).
- ✅ The human writers used their own faculties, yet wrote what God wanted written (CCC 106).
- ✅ Scripture teaches "firmly, faithfully and without error" the truth God wanted put down for our salvation (CCC 107, *Dei Verbum* 11).

**How we know**
🟢 The Church received these books, used them in worship and recognized them as inspired; the same Church that Christ founded discerned the canon (CCC 120).

**Why it matters**
The Bible is not just old wisdom. In it God still speaks, so it must be read in the Spirit in which it was written (CCC 111).
