# Q61 · If non-Catholics can be saved, why try to convert them?

**Short answer**
Because Christ commanded it, because the fullness of truth and grace is found in His Church, and because love wants to share the best it has. "Can be saved" is not the same as "has everything God wants to give."

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ "Go therefore and make disciples of all nations" (Mt 28:19). The Church is missionary by her very nature (CCC 849–851).
- ✅ People can go astray without the Gospel, and the Church has "the obligation and also the sacred right to evangelize all men" (CCC 848).

**Reasons**
- 🟢 Knowing Christ brings joy, meaning and forgiveness now, not only after death.
- 🟢 The sacraments, especially the Eucharist and confession, are gifts no one should lack.
- ✅ Evangelizing is always an invitation; faith must be freely accepted (CCC 160, 2106).

**Why it matters**
If you found a cure, you would not keep it to yourself just because some people recover without it.
