# Q8 · If God is good, why is there evil and suffering? Why do bad things happen to good people?

**Short answer**
No short answer fully explains evil. The Christian answer is the whole faith: God created a good world, gave real freedom, permits evil only to bring a greater good from it, and entered our suffering Himself on the Cross.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Evil is not a "thing" God made; it is the lack of a good that should be there (CCC 309–311).
- ✅ Moral evil comes from the misuse of freedom by angels and humans; God permits it out of respect for that freedom (CCC 311).
- ✅ God draws good even from evil; the greatest evil, the death of His Son, became our redemption (CCC 312).

**Hard questions**
- 🟢 Natural suffering (disease, disasters) belongs to a world "in a state of journeying" toward perfection (CCC 310).
- 🟡 Why a particular person suffers is often not given to us to know; Job is told to trust, not given a theory.

**Why it matters**
God does not stand outside suffering. In Christ He shares it and promises that it will not have the last word.

**For class**
Is there a time something painful in your life later produced something good? Does that answer everything?
