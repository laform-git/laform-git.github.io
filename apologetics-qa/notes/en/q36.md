# Q36 · Where are bishops and apostolic succession in the Bible?

**Short answer**
The apostles appointed successors and leaders by laying on hands, and told them to do the same. That chain of ordination continuing to today is apostolic succession.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The apostles took care to appoint successors so the mission would continue after their death (CCC 861).
- ✅ Bishops succeed the apostles as shepherds of the Church (CCC 862).

**In Scripture**
- 🟢 Acts 1:20–26: Judas' "office" (*episkopē*) is given to Matthias.
- 🟢 Paul ordains Timothy by laying on hands (2 Tim 1:6) and tells him: what you heard from me "entrust to faithful men who will be able to teach others also" (2 Tim 2:2), four generations in one verse.
- 🟢 Titus is sent to "appoint elders in every town" (Titus 1:5).

**In early history**
🟢 Clement of Rome (c. 96) writes that the apostles appointed bishops and provided that others would succeed them. Irenaeus (c. 180) lists the bishops of Rome back to Peter.

**Why it matters**
Succession is how we know today's teaching is connected to the apostles' teaching.
