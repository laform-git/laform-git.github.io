# Q14 · Does the human soul exist? Is it immortal?

**Short answer**
Yes. The soul is the spiritual principle that makes a human body alive and human. Because it is spiritual, it does not die when the body dies.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The human being is one unity of body and soul; the soul is the "form" of the body (CCC 362–365).
- ✅ Each soul is created directly by God, not produced by the parents, and it is immortal (CCC 366).
- ✅ At the end of time, the soul will be reunited with the risen body (CCC 997).

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 We understand universal ideas (justice, number, truth) that are not material things. A power that grasps the immaterial is not itself merely material.
- 🟢 What is not made of parts cannot fall apart, which is what dying is for a body.

**Objection**
"Neuroscience shows the mind is just the brain." 🟡 It shows the mind depends on the brain to work in this life, as a musician depends on an instrument. It does not show they are the same thing.

**Why it matters**
If we have souls, every person has lasting worth, and this life is not the whole story.
