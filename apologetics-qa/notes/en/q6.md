# Q6 · Does God exist? How can we know that God exists?

**Short answer**
Yes. The Church teaches that God's existence can be known with certainty by human reason from the world around us, even before revelation.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ "God, the first principle and last end of all things, can be known with certainty from the created world by the natural light of human reason" (CCC 36, quoting Vatican I).
- ✅ The paths start from the world (change, causes, order) and from the human person (conscience, longing for truth and happiness) (CCC 31–35).

**Main arguments**
- 🟢 From motion and causes: things that change or depend on others need a first cause that depends on nothing (Aquinas, Five Ways).
- 🟢 From contingency: everything around us could have not existed; something must exist necessarily.
- 🟢 From order: the universe is intelligible and finely ordered.
- 🟢 From conscience: we feel bound by a moral law we did not invent.
- 🟡 Each argument is debated by philosophers; together they are strong.

**Why it matters**
Belief in God is not a leap in the dark. It is the most reasonable answer to why anything exists.
