# Q24 · Did Jesus Christ exist? Are the Gospels historically accurate?

**Short answer**
Yes. Almost all historians, believers or not, accept that Jesus of Nazareth existed and was crucified under Pontius Pilate. The Gospels are ancient biographies close to the events, based on eyewitness testimony.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The Gospels have historical character and faithfully hand on what Jesus did and taught (CCC 126; *Dei Verbum* 19).
- ✅ They were formed in three stages: Jesus' life, the apostles' preaching, and the written Gospels (CCC 126).

**Evidence**
- 🟢 Non-Christian sources mention Jesus: Tacitus (Roman historian, c. 116) and Josephus (Jewish historian, c. 93).
- 🟢 Paul's letters, written about 20–30 years after Jesus, already contain early creeds (1 Cor 15:3–8).
- 🟢 The Gospels have far more and earlier manuscripts than any other ancient biography.
- 🟡 Scholars debate the exact dates and authorship of each Gospel.

**Why it matters**
Christianity is not a myth or a philosophy; it rests on events that happened in history.
