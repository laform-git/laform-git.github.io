# Q160 · I talk to God in prayer, but why doesn't He talk back? Does God answer prayers?

**Short answer**
God rarely speaks in an audible voice, but He does speak: through Scripture, the Church, our conscience, other people, events, and a quiet peace in the heart. Learning to hear Him takes silence and time.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ In Scripture "the Father who is in heaven comes lovingly to meet His children, and talks with them" (CCC 104, *Dei Verbum* 21).
- ✅ Prayer is a "battle"; dryness and distraction are normal, and perseverance in faith matters more than feelings (CCC 2725–2731).
- ✅ Contemplative prayer is a gaze of faith and silent love (CCC 2715–2717).

**Practical tips**
- 🟢 Read a Gospel passage slowly before praying (*lectio divina*).
- 🟢 Leave silence at the end of prayer.
- 🟢 Notice small nudges toward good, peace when you choose rightly, and a word from a friend or a homily.

**For class**
Try five minutes of silence with a Gospel passage. What, if anything, stayed with you?
