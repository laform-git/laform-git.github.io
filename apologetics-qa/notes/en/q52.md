# Q52 · Where is the Divine Motherhood of Mary in the Bible? How can Mary be the Mother of God?

**Short answer**
A mother is mother of a person, not just of a body. The person Mary bore is Jesus, and Jesus is God. So Mary is truly the Mother of God, not because she existed before God, but because God took flesh from her.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Mary is *Theotokos*, "God-bearer", defined at the Council of Ephesus (431) (CCC 495, 466).
- ✅ The title protects the truth about Jesus: He is one divine Person, not a man who became joined to God later.

**In Scripture**
🟢 Elizabeth calls her "the mother of my Lord" (Lk 1:43), and "Lord" here is God's title. The child born of her "will be called holy, the Son of God" (Lk 1:35). "God sent forth His Son, born of a woman" (Gal 4:4).

**Why it matters**
Getting Mary's title right means getting Jesus right. If Mary is not Mother of God, Jesus is not truly God.
