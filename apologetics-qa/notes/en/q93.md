# Q93 · What do Buddhists believe?

**Short answer**
Buddhism follows the teaching of Siddhartha Gautama, the Buddha (5th century BC). It teaches that life is marked by suffering caused by craving, and that one can be freed (nirvana) through the Noble Eightfold Path. Most forms do not center on a personal Creator God.

**Key ideas**
- 🟢 **Four Noble Truths:** suffering exists; it comes from craving; it can end; the Eightfold Path leads to its end.
- 🟢 **Eightfold Path:** right view, intention, speech, action, livelihood, effort, mindfulness and concentration.
- 🟢 **No permanent self (anatta)**, rebirth and karma.
- 🟢 Main branches: Theravada and Mahayana (including Zen and Tibetan Buddhism).

**What the Church teaches**
✅ Buddhism "realizes the radical insufficiency of this changeable world" and teaches a path to liberation (*Nostra Aetate* 2). Catholics can admire its discipline and compassion.

**Main differences**
✅ For Christians, the self is a real, lasting person loved by a personal God, and fulfillment is communion with Him, not the extinction of desire.
