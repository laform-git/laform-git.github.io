# Q127 · Illegal abortions are dangerous, so abortion should be legal.

**Short answer**
Concern for women's safety is right. But the argument assumes the unborn child is not a human being; if they are, making their killing safer for others does not make it right. The answer is to protect both mother and child.

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 We don't legalize other harmful acts to make them safer for those who commit them.
- 🟡 Pre-1973 U.S. claims of thousands of deaths from illegal abortion were later admitted by Dr. Bernard Nathanson, a co-founder of NARAL, to have been inflated; maternal deaths were falling sharply already because of antibiotics and better medicine. Data in other countries vary.
- 🟢 Women in crisis need medical care, material help and support; pregnancy centers and parishes provide this.

**What the Church teaches**
✅ Society must protect the life of the unborn and support women (CCC 2273–2274; *Evangelium Vitae* 99).
