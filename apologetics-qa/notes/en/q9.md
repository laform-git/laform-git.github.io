# Q9 · Why do animals suffer even though they never disobey God?

**Short answer**
Animal pain belongs to a material world of growth, decay and death, which God permits as part of a creation still on its way to perfection. Animals do not suffer as punishment for sin.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Creation was made "in a state of journeying" toward its final perfection, with both constructive and destructive forces in nature (CCC 310).
- ✅ Animals are God's creatures and we owe them kindness; causing them needless suffering is contrary to human dignity (CCC 2416–2418).
- ✅ All creation "groans" and waits to be set free (Rom 8:19–22).

**Points to consider**
- 🟢 Pain in animals protects life: it warns of danger and injury.
- 🟡 Animals feel pain, but most theologians hold they lack the self-reflection that turns pain into lasting anguish for humans.
- 🟡 Whether animals share in the new creation is not defined by the Church.

**Why it matters**
How we treat animals reflects how seriously we take God as their Creator.

**For class**
Why would a world without any pain also be a world without warning, growth or courage?
