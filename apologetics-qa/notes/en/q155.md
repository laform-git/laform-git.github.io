# Q155 · What actually happens at Mass, and why does it matter? How is the Mass a sacrifice?

**Short answer**
At Mass, Christ's one sacrifice on the Cross is made present, not repeated, and we are fed with His Body and Blood. Heaven and earth join in worship of the Father.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The Eucharist "re-presents" (makes present) the sacrifice of the Cross; it is one and the same sacrifice, offered in an unbloody manner (CCC 1366–1367).
- ✅ Christ offered Himself "once for all" (Heb 7:27); the Mass makes that one offering present across time.
- ✅ The Mass has two main parts: the Liturgy of the Word and the Liturgy of the Eucharist (CCC 1346).

**What happens**
- 🟢 We gather and ask forgiveness.
- 🟢 God speaks in the readings.
- 🟢 The gifts are offered; at consecration bread and wine become Christ.
- 🟢 We receive Him in Communion and are sent on mission.

**Why it matters**
The Mass is the most important hour of the week: the Cross, the Resurrection and heaven, present on the altar.
