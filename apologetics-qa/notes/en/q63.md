# Q63 · Where is the Sacrament of Confirmation in the Bible?

**Short answer**
In Acts, people who were already baptized received the Holy Spirit when the apostles laid hands on them. That laying on of hands, after baptism, is Confirmation.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Confirmation completes baptismal grace, gives the Holy Spirit in fullness, and strengthens us to witness to Christ (CCC 1285, 1303).
- ✅ It marks the soul permanently and is received once (CCC 1304).

**In Scripture**
- 🟢 Samaritans baptized by Philip receive the Spirit only when Peter and John come and lay hands on them (Acts 8:14–17).
- 🟢 In Ephesus, Paul baptizes, then lays hands, "and the Holy Spirit came on them" (Acts 19:5–6).
- 🟢 Hebrews 6:2 lists "the laying on of hands" among the basic teachings.

**Why it matters**
Confirmation is personal Pentecost: the Spirit's gifts (wisdom, courage, and more) for living and defending the faith.
