# Q29 · What happens to our bodies after we die? What does the Bible say about the resurrection of the body?

**Short answer**
At death the soul separates from the body, which decays. At the end of time God will raise our bodies and reunite them with our souls, transformed and glorified for those who are saved.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ "We believe in the resurrection of the body" (Apostles' Creed; CCC 988–991).
- ✅ We will rise with our own bodies, but transformed, like Christ's risen body (CCC 999; Phil 3:21).
- ✅ All the dead will rise, "those who have done good, to the resurrection of life, and those who have done evil, to the resurrection of judgment" (Jn 5:29; CCC 998).

**In Scripture**
- 🟢 Daniel 12:2; 2 Maccabees 7; 1 Corinthians 15:35–44, Paul's image of the seed that dies and rises as a plant.

**Why it matters**
Christians do not hope to escape the body. God made us body and soul and will save the whole person. This is why the Church honors the bodies of the dead.
