# Q138 · What is the morality of embryonic stem cell research? Is it ever permitted?

**Short answer**
Research that destroys human embryos to obtain their stem cells is never permitted, because the embryo is a human life. Research with adult stem cells, umbilical cord blood and reprogrammed cells is good and encouraged.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Using embryos as research material, destroying them, is gravely immoral (CCC 2275; *Dignitas Personae* 32, 2008).
- ✅ Adult stem cell research, which does not harm the donor, is legitimate (*Dignitas Personae* 32).

**Science**
- 🟢 Adult and umbilical cord stem cells are already used in approved treatments, such as bone marrow transplants for leukemia.
- 🟢 Induced pluripotent stem cells (iPS), developed by Shinya Yamanaka (Nobel Prize 2012), reprogram adult cells to an embryonic-like state without destroying embryos.

**Why it matters**
Science and ethics can work together: good ends must be pursued by good means.
