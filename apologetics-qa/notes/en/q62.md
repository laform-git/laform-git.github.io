# Q62 · Where is the Sacrament of Reconciliation (Confession) in the Bible? Why confess to a priest?

**Short answer**
On Easter night Jesus breathed on the apostles and said: "Receive the Holy Spirit. If you forgive the sins of any, they are forgiven; if you retain the sins of any, they are retained" (Jn 20:22–23). To forgive or retain, they must hear the sins.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Only God forgives sins, and Christ gave this power to the apostles and their successors to exercise in His name (CCC 1441–1442, 1461).
- ✅ Grave sins must be confessed to receive God's forgiveness through the Church; venial sins are also recommended (CCC 1456–1458).
- ✅ The priest is bound by absolute secrecy, the "seal" of confession (CCC 1467).

**In Scripture**
🟢 "Confess your sins to one another" (Jas 5:16); God "gave us the ministry of reconciliation" (2 Cor 5:18).

**Why a priest?**
🟢 Sin hurts the whole Church; the priest represents both Christ and the community. And hearing "I absolve you" brings a certainty no private prayer can.

**Why it matters**
Confession is not a court of punishment but a place of healing.
