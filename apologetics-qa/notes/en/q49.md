# Q49 · Do Catholics worship Mary or honor her excessively?

**Short answer**
No. Catholics worship God alone. Mary receives the highest honor given to any creature because God chose her to be the Mother of His Son, but she remains a creature, saved by Christ.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Devotion to Mary "differs essentially from the adoration given to the Incarnate Word, and equally to the Father and the Holy Spirit" (CCC 971).
- ✅ All Mary's greatness comes from Christ and leads to Him (CCC 487, 964).
- 🟢 Theologians call this honor *hyperdulia*: the highest veneration, still infinitely below adoration (*latria*).

**In Scripture**
🟢 "All generations will call me blessed" (Lk 1:48). Elizabeth, filled with the Spirit, honors her: "Blessed are you among women" (Lk 1:42).

**Why it matters**
Honoring the mother honors the Son. Every true Marian devotion ends with Mary saying, "Do whatever He tells you" (Jn 2:5).

**For class**
Look at any Marian image. Where is she pointing or looking? What does that teach?
