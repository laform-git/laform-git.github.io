# Q151 · If God already knows what we need, why do we need to ask Him in prayer?

**Short answer**
Prayer doesn't inform God; it changes us and opens us to receive. God has also chosen to make our prayers real causes in His plan, giving us the dignity of taking part in His work.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Jesus Himself commands us to ask: "Ask, and it will be given you" (Mt 7:7; CCC 2613).
- ✅ Prayer of petition expresses our dependence on God and our trust in Him (CCC 2629–2633).

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 Saint Thomas Aquinas: we pray not to change God's plan but to obtain what God planned to give through our prayers.
- 🟢 Parents often know what children need, yet want them to ask, because asking builds the relationship.

**Why it matters**
Asking keeps us humble and close to God, and it trains our desires to match His.
