# Q145 · Does the Church ever permit war and self-defense? What about "turning the other cheek" (Mt 5:39)?

**Short answer**
Yes. Self-defense and the defense of others can be not only allowed but a duty, and a nation may defend itself in a just war under strict conditions. "Turning the other cheek" concerns not taking personal revenge for insults, not abandoning the innocent to violence.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Legitimate defense is permitted; for those responsible for others, it can be a grave duty (CCC 2263–2265).
- ✅ War may be justified only if: the damage by the aggressor is lasting, grave and certain; all other means have failed; there is serious prospect of success; and it will not produce evils worse than the evil to be eliminated (CCC 2309).
- ✅ Even in war, civilians must be respected; indiscriminate destruction is a crime (CCC 2313–2314).

**About Mt 5:39**
🟢 In Jesus' culture, a slap on the right cheek was an insult, not a deadly attack. He teaches us to refuse revenge.
