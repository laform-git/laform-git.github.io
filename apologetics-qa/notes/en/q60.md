# Q60 · Does the Church teach that non-Catholics can be saved?

**Short answer**
Yes, it is possible, but always through Christ and His Church, even when people do not know it. "Outside the Church there is no salvation" means all salvation comes from Christ through His Body, not that everyone outside its visible walls is lost.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Those who knowingly refuse to enter or remain in the Church, knowing it was founded by Christ as necessary, cannot be saved (CCC 846).
- ✅ Those who, through no fault of their own, do not know Christ or His Church, but seek God sincerely and try to do His will as they know it through conscience, "may achieve eternal salvation" (CCC 847).
- ✅ Other Christians are joined to the Church by baptism, though not in full communion (CCC 838).

**Why it matters**
The Church is both humble and confident: God's grace is wider than our maps, and Christ's Church is still His chosen way.
