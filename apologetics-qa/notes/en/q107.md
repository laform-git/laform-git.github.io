# Q107 · How can I trust the Church when there have been so many bad popes?

**Short answer**
Because the Church's foundation is Christ, not the holiness of her leaders. Some popes lived scandalous lives, yet none of them changed the faith into error. That survival is itself a sign of the Holy Spirit's protection.

**History**
- 🟢 Of about 266 popes, a small number were notoriously corrupt, for example John XII (10th century) and Alexander VI (Borgia, 15th century).
- 🟢 Many popes were saints and martyrs, and several recent popes have been canonized.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Infallibility protects solemn teaching, not personal behavior (CCC 891).
- ✅ The Church is holy, yet includes sinners and is always in need of purification (CCC 827).

**In Scripture**
🟢 Jesus said of the scribes and Pharisees: "Practice and observe whatever they tell you, but not what they do" (Mt 23:2–3). Peter himself denied Christ and was still made shepherd.

**Why it matters**
We don't follow a pope because he is perfect; we follow Christ, who keeps His promise to the Church through imperfect men.
