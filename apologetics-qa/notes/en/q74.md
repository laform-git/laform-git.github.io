# Q74 · Why does the Church forbid divorce? Why can't divorced and remarried Catholics receive Communion?

**Short answer**
Jesus Himself taught that a valid marriage cannot be dissolved by human decision: "Whoever divorces his wife and marries another commits adultery" (Mk 10:11). The Church cannot change Christ's words. A civil divorce does not end a valid sacramental marriage in God's eyes.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ A ratified and consummated marriage cannot be dissolved by any human power (CCC 1640, 2382).
- ✅ Separation and even civil divorce can be tolerated for serious reasons, such as protecting a spouse or children (CCC 2383).
- ✅ Those who remarry civilly while a prior valid marriage exists are in a situation objectively contrary to God's law, and should not receive Communion while it continues, but they remain members of the Church and are invited to Mass, prayer and the parish (CCC 1650–1651).
- 🟡 Pastoral application after *Amoris Laetitia* (2016) is discussed by bishops and theologians.

**Why it matters**
The rule protects the meaning of the promise "until death do us part", and the Church accompanies those in pain.
