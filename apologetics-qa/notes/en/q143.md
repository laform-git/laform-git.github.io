# Q143 · What are the permitted alternatives to in vitro fertilization? Is assisted fertility okay?

**Short answer**
Yes, many treatments are permitted: those that help the marital act achieve conception, rather than replace it. Restorative reproductive medicine looks for and treats the underlying causes of infertility.

**What the Church teaches**
✅ Medical means that assist the conjugal act, or help it reach its natural end, are legitimate (*Donum Vitae* II,B,6; *Dignitas Personae* 12–13).

**Examples**
- 🟢 Diagnosing and treating conditions such as endometriosis, blocked tubes, hormonal imbalances or low sperm count.
- 🟢 Fertility awareness methods to identify the best time to conceive.
- 🟢 Approaches like NaProTechnology and other "restorative reproductive medicine".
- 🟡 Some techniques (such as GIFT) are debated among Catholic ethicists.

**Other paths**
✅ Adoption and foster care are beautiful forms of parenthood, and spiritual fruitfulness through service is real (CCC 2379).
