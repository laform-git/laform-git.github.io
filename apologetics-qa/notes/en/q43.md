# Q43 · What is predestination, and how do Calvinists and Catholics differ on it?

**Short answer**
Predestination means God, in His eternal plan, freely chooses to bring people to salvation. Catholics believe God wills all to be saved and predestines no one to hell. Classic Calvinism teaches that God chooses some for salvation and passes over others.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ "God predestines no one to go to hell"; going there requires a free turning away from God, persisting to the end (CCC 1037).
- ✅ God "desires all men to be saved" (1 Tim 2:4).
- ✅ God's eternal plan includes each person's free response to grace (CCC 600).

**Classic Calvinism**
🟢 Summarized as TULIP: total depravity, unconditional election, limited atonement (Christ died only for the elect), irresistible grace, perseverance of the saints.

**Catholic response**
- ✅ Christ died for all (2 Cor 5:15; CCC 605).
- ✅ Grace can be resisted (Acts 7:51).
- 🟡 Within Catholic theology, how grace and freedom fit together is still a mystery, and schools differ.

**Why it matters**
No one should believe God made them to be lost. His offer is real for everyone.
