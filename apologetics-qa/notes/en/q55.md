# Q55 · Can I be a good Catholic if I have no devotion to Mary? Why is it good to have devotion to Mary?

**Short answer**
A Catholic must believe what the Church teaches about Mary, and particular devotions like the Rosary are not required. But loving Mary is part of loving Jesus well, and the saints all found she brought them closer to Him.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The Marian dogmas (Mother of God, perpetual virginity, Immaculate Conception, Assumption) belong to the faith.
- ✅ "The Church's devotion to the Blessed Virgin is intrinsic to Christian worship" (CCC 971, quoting Paul VI).
- ✅ Private devotions are encouraged, not imposed (CCC 1674–1676).

**Why it helps**
- 🟢 She is the first and most faithful disciple, a model of faith and obedience (CCC 967).
- 🟢 She is our mother; children grow by spending time with their mother.
- 🟢 Every Marian prayer is centered on Christ; the Rosary meditates on His life.

**Why it matters**
Start small: one Hail Mary a day can open a friendship.
