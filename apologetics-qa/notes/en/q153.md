# Q153 · How can I possibly find time to pray when I hardly have time to work and eat?

**Short answer**
Start small and attach prayer to what you already do. A few minutes every day, plus short prayers through the day, can transform a busy life. We make time for what we love.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ "Pray constantly" (1 Thess 5:17): prayer can be woven into the rhythm of the day (CCC 2697–2698, 2742–2745).
- ✅ Morning and evening prayer, grace at meals and Sunday Mass give daily life a rhythm (CCC 2698).

**Practical ideas**
- 🟢 One minute on waking: offer your day to God.
- 🟢 Grace before meals.
- 🟢 A decade of the Rosary in the car or on a walk.
- 🟢 Short "arrow prayers": "Jesus, I trust in you."
- 🟢 Two minutes before sleep to thank God and examine the day.

**Why it matters**
Saints with busy lives, like Thomas More or Gianna Beretta Molla, prayed most because they had the most to do.

**For class**
Choose one small moment in your day to turn into prayer this week.
