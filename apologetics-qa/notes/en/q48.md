# Q48 · Why doesn't the Catholic Church proclaim that certain people are in hell?

**Short answer**
Because only God sees the heart and the final moment of a life. The Church can say an act is gravely wrong, but she leaves the judgment of persons to God.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Hell is real and eternal, and it is the state of definitive self-exclusion from God (CCC 1033–1035).
- ✅ "Although we can judge that an act is in itself a grave offense, we must entrust judgment of persons to the justice and mercy of God" (CCC 1861).
- ✅ The Church prays for all to be saved (CCC 1037, 1058).

**Why the difference with saints?**
🟢 To canonize, the Church has positive signs: heroic virtue and miracles. There is no comparable sign that someone is lost, and God's mercy can reach a person in ways we do not see.

**Why it matters**
We take hell seriously as a real possibility for ourselves, but we never give up hope for anyone.
