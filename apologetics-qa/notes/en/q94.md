# Q94 · What do Muslims believe?

**Short answer**
Muslims believe in one God (Allah), who revealed the Qur'an to the prophet Muhammad (c. 570–632). They honor Abraham, Moses and Jesus as prophets, but deny that Jesus is God's Son or that He died on the Cross.

**Key beliefs**
- 🟢 **Five pillars:** the creed ("There is no god but God, and Muhammad is His messenger"), prayer five times a day, almsgiving, fasting in Ramadan, and pilgrimage to Mecca.
- 🟢 **Jesus (Isa):** born of the Virgin Mary, a great prophet, but not divine.
- 🟢 Judgment day, heaven and hell.

**What the Church teaches**
✅ Muslims "profess to hold the faith of Abraham, and together with us they adore the one, merciful God" (CCC 841; *Lumen Gentium* 16). The Church calls for mutual understanding (*Nostra Aetate* 3).

**Main differences**
✅ The Trinity, the divinity of Christ, His death and Resurrection, and salvation by grace.
