# Q139 · What's wrong with pornography if the actors consent and no one gets hurt?

**Short answer**
Pornography turns persons into objects for use, harms those who watch it and those who make it, and distorts how we see sexuality and love. The idea that "no one gets hurt" is not true.

**What the Church teaches**
✅ Pornography offends against chastity, perverts the conjugal act, gravely injures the dignity of its participants, and immerses everyone involved "in the illusion of a fantasy world" (CCC 2354). Civil authorities should prevent its production and distribution.

**Harms**
- 🟢 Performers often report coercion, exploitation and trauma; some content involves trafficking or abuse.
- 🟢 Studies link heavy use to compulsive habits, relationship problems and unrealistic expectations 🟡 (research is still developing).
- 🟢 It teaches viewers to see people as bodies to consume.

**Hope**
🟢 Freedom is possible: confession, accountability, filters, and support groups help many people.
