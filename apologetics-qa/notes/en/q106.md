# Q106 · How can the Church preach morality if priests have sexually abused children?

**Short answer**
The abuse, and the cover-ups by some bishops, were grave crimes and sins that betrayed victims and the Gospel. They do not make moral truth false; they show what happens when it is not lived. The Church must keep repenting, protecting children and supporting survivors.

**Facts**
- 🟢 Investigations in many countries revealed decades of abuse and mishandling by Church leaders.
- 🟢 Reforms include the U.S. bishops' *Dallas Charter* (2002), background checks and safe-environment training, and Pope Francis' *Vos estis lux mundi* (2019), requiring reports and holding bishops accountable.
- 🟢 Studies show abuse happens across many institutions; this explains nothing away but helps prevention.

**What Jesus said**
✅ "Whoever causes one of these little ones… to sin, it would be better for him to have a great millstone fastened round his neck" (Mt 18:6). Scandal is a grave fault (CCC 2285).

**Why it matters**
The truth of the Gospel never depended on the virtue of its messengers, but its credibility is harmed by their sins.
