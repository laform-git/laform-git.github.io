# Q46 · How can the Catholic Church presume to canonize saints?

**Short answer**
Canonizing does not make anyone a saint; only God does that. It is the Church's solemn recognition, after careful investigation, that a person lived heroic virtue and is in heaven, and can be offered as a model and intercessor.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ By canonizing, the Church recognizes the power of the Spirit of holiness within her and offers models and intercessors (CCC 828).
- ✅ Christ gave the Church authority to "bind and loose" (Mt 16:19; 18:18).

**The process**
- 🟢 Investigation of life and writings → "Venerable" (heroic virtue).
- 🟢 A verified miracle → "Blessed" (martyrs need no miracle for this step).
- 🟢 A second miracle → "Saint".
- 🟢 Medical boards and theologians review each miracle; many proposed miracles are rejected.

**Why it matters**
Saints prove that holiness is possible in every time, place and walk of life: mothers, students, doctors, workers.
