# Q26 · Why do we need a redeemer, and how did Jesus Christ redeem us?

**Short answer**
Sin broke our friendship with God, and we cannot repair it by ourselves. Jesus, true God and true man, repaired it from within humanity by His obedient love unto death and His Resurrection.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Since the Fall, all humans are born without original holiness and inclined to sin (CCC 402–405).
- ✅ Christ freely offered His life as a sacrifice of love for all (CCC 606–609, 616).
- ✅ By His death He frees us from sin; by His Resurrection He opens new life (CCC 654).

**Why a God-man?**
🟢 Only a human could offer reparation on behalf of humanity, and only God could make it worth enough for all. Jesus is both (Saint Anselm's classic argument).

**Why it matters**
Redemption is not God changing His mind about us; it is God giving us a way home we could never build ourselves.
