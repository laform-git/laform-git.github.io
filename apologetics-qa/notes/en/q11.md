# Q11 · How can God be merciful and just at the same time?

**Short answer**
In God, mercy and justice are not rivals. Justice gives each their due; mercy gives more than is due. The Cross shows both at once: sin is taken seriously, and the sinner is forgiven.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ God is "merciful and gracious, slow to anger, abounding in steadfast love", yet He does not ignore evil (Ex 34:6–7; CCC 210–211).
- ✅ In Christ's sacrifice, God's justice and mercy meet: He "made Him to be sin who knew no sin" for our sake (2 Cor 5:21; CCC 615).
- ✅ Mercy does not cancel the need for repentance; God respects our freedom to refuse it (CCC 1864).

**Image**
🟢 A judge who pays the fine of the guilty person himself is both just (the debt is paid) and merciful (the guilty goes free).

**Why it matters**
Without justice, mercy would mean evil does not matter. Without mercy, justice would leave no one standing. Christians need both.
