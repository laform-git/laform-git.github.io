# Q31 · Did Jesus establish a Church? Does this Church have authority?

**Short answer**
Yes. Jesus chose the Twelve, gave them a mission and promised: "On this rock I will build my Church" (Mt 16:18). He gave them real authority to teach, forgive and govern in His name.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Christ founded the Church by preaching the Kingdom, choosing the apostles and giving Himself on the Cross (CCC 763–766).
- ✅ "He who hears you hears me" (Lk 10:16). The apostles' authority passed to their successors, the bishops (CCC 77, 861–862).
- ✅ The Magisterium serves the Word of God; it is not above it (CCC 85–86).

**In Scripture**
🟢 Mt 16:18–19 and 18:17–18 (binding and loosing); Mt 28:18–20 (teach all nations); 1 Tim 3:15 (the Church, "pillar and foundation of the truth").

**Why it matters**
A Bible needs an interpreter. Christ did not leave a book alone; He left a living community with authority.
