# Q147 · Why is the Church against transgenderism and sex change?

**Short answer**
The Church teaches that our sex, male or female, is a gift received from God, part of who we are body and soul, not something to be changed. She also insists that people experiencing gender dysphoria be treated with love, respect and real care.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ "Everyone, man and woman, should acknowledge and accept his sexual identity" (CCC 2333).
- ✅ The Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith teaches that any sex-change intervention, as a rule, risks threatening the unique dignity a person received from conception (*Dignitas Infinita* 60, 2024).
- ✅ Surgical mutilations without a therapeutic reason are contrary to the moral law (CCC 2297).
- ✅ Every person must be respected and never subjected to unjust discrimination or violence (*Dignitas Infinita* 55).

**Pastoral approach**
🟢 Gender dysphoria causes real suffering. Young people need patient listening, good medical and psychological care, and a community that loves them.
