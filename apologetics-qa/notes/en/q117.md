# Q117 · Why does the Church forbid artificial birth control but permit natural family planning (NFP)? What is the Theology of the Body?

**Short answer**
Contraception actively blocks the life-giving meaning of the marital act. NFP does nothing to the act; couples simply abstain during fertile times when they have serious reasons to avoid pregnancy. The Theology of the Body explains that our bodies speak a language of total self-giving.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Spouses may space births for just reasons by using the natural rhythms of fertility (CCC 2368, 2370).
- ✅ Contraception separates the unitive and procreative meanings of the marital act, which God united (CCC 2366, 2370; *Humanae Vitae* 12).

**Theology of the Body**
🟢 A series of 129 talks by Saint John Paul II (1979–1984). Its key idea: the body reveals that we are made to be a gift for another. Married love says with the body "I give you all of myself", including fertility.

**Why it matters**
NFP asks for communication, self-control and respect between spouses, and many couples say it strengthens their marriage.
