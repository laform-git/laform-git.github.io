# Q13 · Is there only one God, or many?

**Short answer**
There is only one God. Reason can show it, and revelation confirms it.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ "Hear, O Israel: the Lord our God is one Lord" (Dt 6:4; Mk 12:29). The first article of the Creed is "I believe in one God" (CCC 200–202).
- ✅ The Trinity does not mean three gods: one divine nature, three Persons (CCC 253).

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 God is infinite and perfect. Two infinite beings would have to differ in something, so one would lack what the other has, and would not be infinite.
- 🟢 The order of the universe points to one source, not rival powers.

**Objection**
"Christians worship three gods." ✅ No. The Father, Son and Holy Spirit are not three beings sharing a nature like three people; they are one God, each Person fully God.

**Why it matters**
Belief in one God frees us from fear of competing powers and gives us one final loyalty.
