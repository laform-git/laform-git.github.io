# Q156 · How can we be happy in heaven knowing some of our loved ones went to hell?

**Short answer**
This is a hard question, and the full answer is beyond us. In heaven we will see as God sees, perfectly united to His love and justice, and no sorrow will be able to take away our joy (Rev 21:4). We also do not know that any particular person is in hell (see Q48).

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ God "will wipe away every tear from their eyes" (Rev 21:4).
- ✅ Hell is the free, definitive self-exclusion from communion with God; God respects that choice (CCC 1033).
- ✅ We never judge that a particular person is lost (CCC 1861) and we pray for all (CCC 1037).

**Ways theologians reason**
- 🟡 Saint Thomas Aquinas taught that the blessed are so united to God's will that they rejoice in His justice.
- 🟡 Others stress that in heaven we will understand that the lost truly chose to reject love, and that God did everything to save them.

**Why it matters**
For now, this question is a call to pray and work for the salvation of those we love.
