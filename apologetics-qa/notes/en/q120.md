# Q120 · Why is the Church against cohabitation and premarital sex?

**Short answer**
Because sexual union says with the body "I give myself to you completely and forever". That promise is only made in marriage. Living as married without the commitment of marriage contradicts the body's language.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Sex is reserved to marriage; fornication is gravely contrary to the dignity of persons and of human sexuality (CCC 2353).
- ✅ "Trial marriages" cannot guarantee sincerity and fidelity; "carnal union is morally legitimate only when a definitive community of life between a man and a woman has been established" (CCC 2391).
- ✅ Chastity is a virtue for everyone, a "school of self-mastery" (CCC 2337–2339).

**In Scripture**
🟢 "Shun immorality" (1 Cor 6:18); "Let marriage be held in honor among all, and let the marriage bed be undefiled" (Heb 13:4).

**Research**
🟡 Many studies have linked cohabitation before marriage to higher divorce rates, though the effect has weakened in recent decades and researchers debate the causes.
