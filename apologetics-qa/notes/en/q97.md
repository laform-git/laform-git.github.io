# Q97 · How do I dialogue with Mormons?

**Short answer**
Be warm and respectful, and focus on the nature of God. Latter-day Saints use Christian words with different meanings: they believe God the Father was once a man and that Father, Son and Spirit are three separate gods.

**Who they are**
🟢 The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints was founded by Joseph Smith in New York in 1830. Besides the Bible they accept the *Book of Mormon*, *Doctrine and Covenants* and *Pearl of Great Price*.

**Key points to discuss**
- ✅ **One God, eternal and unchanging:** "Before me no god was formed, nor shall there be any after me" (Isa 43:10); God is not a man (Num 23:19).
- ✅ **No "Great Apostasy":** Jesus promised the gates of hell would not prevail against His Church (Mt 16:18).
- 🟢 **Evidence:** ask about the lack of archaeological support for the *Book of Mormon* peoples.
- ✅ **Baptism:** the Catholic Church does not recognize Mormon baptism as valid, because of their different understanding of the Trinity.

**Tips**
🟢 Missionaries are often young and sincere. Share your own faith in Christ and ask about their "burning in the bosom" experience with gentleness.
