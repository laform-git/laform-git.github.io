# Q116 · Why is the Church against homosexuality if some people are born gay?

**Short answer**
The Church does not condemn people for having same-sex attraction, whatever its cause. She teaches that the attraction itself is not a sin, but that sexual acts belong only in marriage between a man and a woman. Every person is called to chastity, and every person deserves respect.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The origins of same-sex attraction "remain largely unexplained" (CCC 2357).
- ✅ People with these attractions "must be accepted with respect, compassion and sensitivity. Every sign of unjust discrimination in their regard should be avoided" (CCC 2358).
- ✅ Homosexual acts cannot be approved because they are not open to life and the complementarity of man and woman (CCC 2357).
- ✅ All are called to chastity, and can grow in holiness through friendship, prayer and the sacraments (CCC 2359).

**Reasoning**
🟢 Having an inclination, even one we did not choose, does not by itself make acting on it right. Everyone has inclinations they are asked not to act on.

**Why it matters**
Catholics must never mock or reject people; the call to chastity applies to everyone, married or single.
