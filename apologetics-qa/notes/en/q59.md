# Q59 · What happens to babies who die without baptism?

**Short answer**
The Church entrusts them to God's mercy and has real hope for their salvation. Limbo was a theological opinion, never a dogma.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ "The Church can only entrust them to the mercy of God… the great mercy of God who desires that all men should be saved, and Jesus' tenderness toward children… allow us to hope that there is a way of salvation for children who have died without Baptism" (CCC 1261).
- ✅ This is why the Church urges parents not to delay baptism.
- 🟢 In 2007 the International Theological Commission concluded there are "serious theological and liturgical grounds for hope" that such infants are saved.

**About limbo**
🟡 Limbo (a state of natural happiness without the vision of God) was a common opinion for centuries but was never defined, and it is not in the current Catechism.

**Why it matters**
Grieving parents can entrust their child to a loving God with genuine hope.
