# Q92 · What do Hindus believe?

**Short answer**
Hinduism is a family of traditions from India rather than one system. Common beliefs include an ultimate reality (Brahman), many gods seen as forms or aspects of the divine, rebirth (samsara) shaped by karma, and liberation (moksha) through knowledge, devotion or right action.

**Key ideas**
- 🟢 **Scriptures:** the Vedas, the Upanishads, and the Bhagavad Gita.
- 🟢 **Dharma:** duty and right order in life.
- 🟢 **Paths:** devotion (bhakti), knowledge (jnana) and action (karma yoga).

**What the Church teaches**
✅ "In Hinduism, men contemplate the divine mystery and express it through an inexhaustible abundance of myths and through searching philosophical inquiry" (*Nostra Aetate* 2). The Church rejects nothing true and holy in other religions, while proclaiming Christ as "the way, the truth and the life".

**Main differences**
- ✅ One life, not many: "it is appointed for men to die once, and after that comes judgment" (Heb 9:27; CCC 1013).
- ✅ Salvation is God's gift in Christ, not escape from the cycle of rebirth by our own effort.
