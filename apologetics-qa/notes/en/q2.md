# Q2 · Does absolute truth exist, or is all truth relative?

**Short answer**
Absolute truth exists. Opinions and tastes vary, but facts about reality do not change because people disagree about them.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Relativism, the idea that no truth holds for everyone, cannot be stated without contradicting itself: "all truth is relative" is offered as true for everyone.
- ✅ Benedict XVI warned of a "dictatorship of relativism" that recognizes nothing as definitive and leaves the self as the only measure (homily, April 18, 2005).
- ✅ The moral law is written on the heart and binds all people (Rom 2:14–15; CCC 1954–1956).

**Objection**
"Cultures disagree, so truth is relative." 🟢 Disagreement shows that people hold different beliefs, not that every belief is true. Cultures once disagreed about whether the earth was round.

**Why it matters**
Without absolute truth, no one could be wrong, and so no one could ever learn, repent or be treated unjustly.
