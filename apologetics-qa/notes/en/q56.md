# Q56 · Why are there sacraments, and where are they in the Bible?

**Short answer**
Sacraments are visible signs, instituted by Christ, that truly give the grace they signify. We are body and soul, so God reaches us through things we can see, touch and hear, as Jesus did with water, bread, oil and touch.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ There are seven sacraments, all instituted by Christ (CCC 1113–1114, 1210).
- ✅ They act *ex opere operato*: by the power of Christ, not the holiness of the minister (CCC 1127–1128).

**In Scripture (one example each)**
- 🟢 Baptism: Mt 28:19; Jn 3:5.
- 🟢 Confirmation: Acts 8:14–17.
- 🟢 Eucharist: Lk 22:19–20; Jn 6:53.
- 🟢 Confession: Jn 20:22–23.
- 🟢 Anointing of the Sick: Jas 5:14–15.
- 🟢 Holy Orders: Lk 22:19; 2 Tim 1:6.
- 🟢 Matrimony: Mt 19:4–6; Eph 5:31–32.

**Why it matters**
In the sacraments we don't just remember Jesus; we meet Him.

**For class**
Why would God use physical things to give spiritual gifts? Think of a hug or a ring.
