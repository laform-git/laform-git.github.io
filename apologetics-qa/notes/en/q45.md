# Q45 · Why do Catholics pray to saints and honor them?

**Short answer**
Catholics ask the saints to pray for them, just as we ask friends on earth to pray for us. The saints are alive in Christ and close to God. Honoring them is not worship; worship is for God alone.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The communion of saints unites the Church on earth, in purgatory and in heaven (CCC 954–957).
- ✅ The saints in heaven intercede for us (CCC 956, 2683).
- ✅ Adoration (*latria*) belongs only to God; saints receive veneration (*dulia*).

**In Scripture**
- 🟢 "The prayer of a righteous man has great power" (Jas 5:16).
- 🟢 God "is not God of the dead, but of the living" (Mk 12:27).
- 🟢 In Revelation 5:8 the elders in heaven offer "the prayers of the saints".
- 🟢 "We are surrounded by so great a cloud of witnesses" (Heb 12:1).

**Why it matters**
Death does not break the family of God. The saints are friends and models who cheer us on.
