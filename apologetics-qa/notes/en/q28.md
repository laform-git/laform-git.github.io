# Q28 · What is the redemptive value of a Christian's suffering? What is the use of "offering it up"?

**Short answer**
United to Christ, our suffering is not wasted. It can be joined to His sacrifice and become prayer and love for others. "Offering it up" means giving God what hurts, trusting Him to use it.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Saint Paul: "In my flesh I complete what is lacking in Christ's afflictions for the sake of His body, the Church" (Col 1:24).
- ✅ Through His Passion, Christ gave suffering a new meaning; it can unite us to His redemptive Passion (CCC 1505, 1521).
- ✅ Saint John Paul II explained this in *Salvifici Doloris* (1984).

**What it does not mean**
- 🟢 It does not mean seeking pain or refusing treatment.
- 🟢 It does not mean Christ's sacrifice was insufficient; it means He lets us share in it.

**Why it matters**
A sick grandmother praying for her grandchildren is doing real, powerful work.
