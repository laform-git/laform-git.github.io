# Q42 · Is our salvation assured if we believe in Jesus Christ and accept Him as our personal Savior?

**Short answer**
Accepting Christ is essential, but salvation is a journey, not a single moment. We can have firm hope and confidence in God, but not presumption, because we remain free to turn away.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Christian hope trusts God's promises and the help of His grace to persevere (CCC 1817–1821).
- ✅ Presumption, expecting salvation without conversion or merit, is a sin against hope (CCC 2092).
- ✅ We cannot know with certainty our own state of grace by feeling, but we can trust God's mercy (CCC 2005).

**In Scripture**
🟢 "Work out your salvation with fear and trembling" (Phil 2:12); "Let anyone who thinks that he stands take heed lest he fall" (1 Cor 10:12); Paul disciplines himself lest he be "disqualified" (1 Cor 9:27). Also: "Whoever endures to the end will be saved" (Mt 24:13).

**Why it matters**
A Catholic is not anxious about salvation but confident in God, while staying faithful day by day, especially through confession.
