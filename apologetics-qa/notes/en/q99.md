# Q99 · Does Catholicism contradict science? Has the Church historically opposed science and technology?

**Short answer**
No. The Church founded universities, funded astronomy for centuries and produced many scientists. Conflicts like the Galileo affair were real but exceptional, not the rule.

**What the Church teaches**
✅ "Methodical research in all branches of knowledge… can never conflict with the faith, because the things of the world and the things of faith derive from the same God" (CCC 159).

**Evidence**
- 🟢 The medieval Church built the university system (Bologna, Paris, Oxford).
- 🟢 Catholic scientists include Copernicus (a cleric), Gregor Mendel (an Augustinian friar, father of genetics), Georges Lemaître (a priest, Big Bang theory), and Nicolas Steno (geology, later a bishop).
- 🟢 Historian J. L. Heilbron wrote that the Church gave more support to astronomy over six centuries than probably any other institution.
- 🟢 The Vatican Observatory still runs today.

**Why it matters**
The "war between science and religion" is largely a 19th-century myth that historians of science have revised.
