# Q77 · Does the "unchastity" in Matthew 19:9 mean divorce is permitted in some cases?

**Short answer**
The Catholic reading is no. The Greek word *porneia* most likely refers to an unlawful union, such as a marriage within forbidden family degrees, which was never a true marriage. Jesus' teaching in Mark and Luke has no exception at all.

**The texts**
- 🟢 Mark 10:11–12 and Luke 16:18: no exception.
- 🟢 Matthew 5:32 and 19:9: "except for *porneia*".
- 🟢 Paul: "the wife should not separate from her husband… and the husband should not divorce his wife" (1 Cor 7:10–11).

**Interpretations**
- 🟡 *Porneia* as an invalid union (common Catholic view; compare Acts 15:20, 29).
- 🟡 The exception allows separation, but not remarriage.
- ✅ Either way, the Church teaches a valid, consummated marriage cannot be dissolved (CCC 2382).

**Why it matters**
One verse must be read with all the others. Jesus' disciples were shocked by His strictness (Mt 19:10), which only makes sense if He did not allow easy divorce.
