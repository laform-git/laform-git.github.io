# Q91 · How do I dialogue with Seventh-day Adventists?

**Short answer**
Start from what you share: faith in Christ, love of Scripture and a serious moral life. Then talk calmly about the main differences: the Sabbath, the soul after death, and the role of Ellen G. White.

**Who they are**
🟢 Adventists arose in the U.S. in the 1840s–1860s; Ellen G. White (1827–1915) is regarded by them as a prophetic voice. They are known for keeping Saturday as the Sabbath and for healthy living.

**Main topics**
- ✅ **Sunday:** Christians worship on Sunday, the day of the Resurrection, the "Lord's day" (Rev 1:10; Acts 20:7; 1 Cor 16:2). Sunday fulfills the Sabbath (CCC 2174–2176).
- ✅ **The soul:** Adventists teach "soul sleep" until the resurrection; Catholics hold the soul lives on after death (Lk 23:43; Phil 1:23; CCC 366, 1022).
- ✅ **Hell:** Adventists teach the wicked are annihilated; the Church teaches hell is eternal (Mt 25:46; CCC 1035).
- 🟢 **Authority:** ask who decides between disputed readings of Scripture.

**Tips**
🟢 Respect their sincerity and discipline. Ask questions more than you lecture.

**For class**
Why did the first Christians gather on Sunday instead of Saturday?
