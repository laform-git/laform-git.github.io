# Q34 · What is papal infallibility and where is it in the Bible?

**Short answer**
Infallibility means the Holy Spirit protects the Pope from error when he solemnly defines a doctrine of faith or morals for the whole Church. It does not mean he is sinless or always right.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The Pope is infallible when, as supreme teacher, he proclaims by a definitive act a doctrine of faith or morals (CCC 891; Vatican I, 1870).
- ✅ The same gift belongs to the bishops united with the Pope, especially in an ecumenical council (CCC 891–892).
- ✅ It is rarely used; clear examples are the Immaculate Conception (1854) and the Assumption (1950).

**What it is not**
- 🟢 Not impeccability: popes sin and go to confession.
- 🟢 Not every opinion, interview or decision of a pope.

**In Scripture**
🟢 Lk 22:32 ("I have prayed that your faith may not fail… strengthen your brethren"); Mt 16:18–19; Jn 16:13 (the Spirit "will guide you into all truth").

**Why it matters**
If Christ wanted His truth handed on intact, He needed a way to protect it from human error.
