# Q154 · If God is unchangeable, how can we say God became man in the Incarnation?

**Short answer**
In the Incarnation, the divine nature did not change into a human one. The eternal Son took on a human nature in addition to His divine nature. The change was on the human side: humanity was united to God.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Christ is one divine Person with two natures, divine and human, "without confusion, change, division or separation" (Council of Chalcedon, 451; CCC 467).
- ✅ The liturgy sings: "What He was, He remained; what He was not, He assumed" (CCC 469).

**Image**
🟢 When a king puts on a soldier's uniform to fight beside his men, he does not stop being king; he adds a new way of being with them. (Every image is imperfect.)

**Why it matters**
God came truly close without ceasing to be God. That's why Jesus can save us: He is fully one of us and fully God.
