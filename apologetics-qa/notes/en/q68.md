# Q68 · How is the Real Presence possible if Christ's physical characteristics are not visible?

**Short answer**
Because what changes is the *substance*, what a thing is, not the *accidents*, how it appears to the senses. After consecration, it looks, tastes and weighs like bread, but it is Christ.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The substance of bread and wine is changed into Christ's Body and Blood; the appearances remain (CCC 1373–1376).
- ✅ This presence "cannot be apprehended by the senses… but only by faith" (CCC 1381, quoting Aquinas).

**An everyday comparison**
🟢 A man in a costume looks like a bear but is a man: appearance and reality can differ. In the Eucharist this happens in a unique, supernatural way, not by disguise but by God's power.

**Why God does it this way**
- 🟢 To let us receive Him in a humble, approachable form.
- 🟢 To call forth faith, trusting His word over our eyes, as at the Annunciation and the Resurrection.

**For class**
What things do you trust are real even though your senses cannot detect them? How is the Eucharist similar and different?
