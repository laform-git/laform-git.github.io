# Q80 · Why can't Catholic priests marry? What is priestly celibacy for?

**Short answer**
Celibacy for priests in the Latin Church is a discipline, not a dogma. It allows the priest to give himself entirely to God and his people, as Jesus did, and it points to the Kingdom where "they neither marry nor are given in marriage" (Mt 22:30).

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ In the Latin Church, priests are normally chosen from men who commit to celibacy "for the sake of the kingdom of heaven" (CCC 1579; Mt 19:12).
- ✅ In the Eastern Catholic Churches, married men may be ordained priests, but bishops are celibate and priests do not marry after ordination (CCC 1580).
- 🟢 Some married former Protestant ministers have been ordained Catholic priests with permission.

**In Scripture**
🟢 Jesus was celibate; Paul recommends celibacy for undivided devotion to the Lord (1 Cor 7:32–35).

**Why it matters**
A celibate priest is a sign that God alone can fill a human heart, and he is free to be "father" to many.
