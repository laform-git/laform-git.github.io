# Q39 · Why are the Protestant Ten Commandments numbered differently from the Catholic ones?

**Short answer**
The Bible lists the commandments without numbers, so different traditions divide the same text differently. Nothing is added or removed; only the grouping changes.

**The two main divisions**
- ✅ Catholics and Lutherans follow Saint Augustine: the ban on false gods and on images is one commandment, and coveting is split into two (neighbor's wife, neighbor's goods) (CCC 2066).
- ✅ The Orthodox and most Protestants follow other early writers: false gods and images are two commandments, and coveting is one.

**Why it matters**
🟢 Both divisions contain the same words of Exodus 20 and Deuteronomy 5. The Catholic Catechism prints the full biblical text alongside its short formula.
