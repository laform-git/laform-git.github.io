# Q134 · Women will get abortions anyway, whether it is legal or not.

**Short answer**
People break many laws anyway; we still keep laws against theft and assault, because laws teach, protect and reduce harm. Evidence suggests laws and support both reduce the number of abortions.

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 The purpose of law is not to stop every wrong act but to protect the vulnerable and shape what society sees as acceptable.
- 🟡 Studies of restrictions and abortion rates in different countries and U.S. states reach varied conclusions; many find restrictions reduce abortions, others find smaller effects.
- 🟢 Laws work best together with real help: healthcare, financial aid and adoption support.

**What the Church teaches**
✅ Civil law must protect the right to life of every human being (CCC 2273); the Church also calls for a "culture of life" that makes abortion unthinkable, not just illegal (*Evangelium Vitae* 95).
