# Q136 · Pro-lifers who support capital punishment are inconsistent: they want to save unborn babies but kill criminals.

**Short answer**
There is a real difference: the unborn child is completely innocent; a criminal has been found guilty of a grave crime. But the Church now teaches that the death penalty is inadmissible today, so a consistent Catholic ethic protects every human life.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Direct killing of the innocent is always gravely wrong (CCC 2261).
- ✅ In 2018 Pope Francis revised the Catechism: "the death penalty is inadmissible because it is an attack on the inviolability and dignity of the person" (CCC 2267).
- 🟡 Catholics discuss how this revision relates to earlier teaching that allowed capital punishment in some cases.

**Reasoning**
🟢 Innocence and guilt matter morally, so the two issues are not identical. Yet both call Catholics to respect the dignity of every person.
