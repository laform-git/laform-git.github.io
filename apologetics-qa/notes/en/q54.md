# Q54 · Where is the Assumption of Mary in the Bible?

**Short answer**
The Assumption means that at the end of her earthly life Mary was taken body and soul into heaven. It is not described directly in Scripture, but it is rooted in it and in the Church's ancient faith.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Defined by Pius XII in 1950 (*Munificentissimus Deus*) (CCC 966).
- ✅ It is a special share in her Son's Resurrection and a foretaste of ours (CCC 966).

**Biblical roots**
- 🟢 Revelation 11:19–12:1: the Ark of the Covenant is seen in heaven, and then "a woman clothed with the sun".
- 🟢 Others were taken up before: Enoch (Gen 5:24), Elijah (2 Kgs 2:11).
- 🟢 The one preserved from sin (Q50) was fittingly preserved from bodily corruption.

**Tradition**
🟢 The feast of Mary's "Dormition" was celebrated widely in East and West by the 6th–7th centuries. No church ever claimed to have her bodily relics.

**Why it matters**
Our bodies matter. What God did for Mary He promises to all who belong to Christ.
