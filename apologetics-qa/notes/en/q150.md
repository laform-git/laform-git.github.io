# Q150 · Why doesn't God just appear to us, so we can know He exists?

**Short answer**
God did appear: in Jesus Christ. But God respects our freedom; He wants love, not forced submission. If He overwhelmed us with His glory, we could not freely choose Him.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ "Whoever has seen me has seen the Father" (Jn 14:9). In Christ, God has said everything (CCC 65).
- ✅ God leaves signs in creation and conscience, enough for honest seekers, but not so overpowering that faith becomes forced (CCC 31–35, 160).

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 Many people saw Jesus' miracles and still did not believe (Jn 12:37); seeing does not automatically produce faith.
- 🟢 A love that cannot say no is not love. God draws gently.
- 🟢 "Blessed are those who have not seen and yet believe" (Jn 20:29).

**For class**
Would you rather be forced to love someone, or freely choose to? Why does God want the second?
