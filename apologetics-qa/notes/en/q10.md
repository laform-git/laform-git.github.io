# Q10 · If God knows the future, do we really have free will?

**Short answer**
Yes. God knowing what we will freely choose does not cause the choice, just as remembering what someone did yesterday does not force them to have done it.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ God is outside time; to Him "all moments of time are present in their immediacy" (CCC 600).
- ✅ God's providence includes our free actions; He uses them without destroying them (CCC 306–308).
- ✅ Human freedom is real and makes us responsible for our acts (CCC 1730–1733).

**How to think about it**
- 🟢 Knowing is not causing. God sees what we choose; He does not make us choose it.
- 🟢 Because God is eternal, He does not "foresee" as if from a point in time; He sees all of history in one eternal present.
- 🟡 How grace and freedom work together in detail was debated for centuries (Dominicans and Jesuits); the Church allowed both views.

**Why it matters**
Our choices count. God's knowledge is not a script we are forced to act out.

**For class**
If you watch a recording of a game you already know the result of, did your knowing cause the result?
