# Q50 · Where is the Immaculate Conception of Mary in the Bible? How can Mary be without sin?

**Short answer**
The Immaculate Conception means Mary was preserved from original sin from the first moment of her existence, by a special grace from God, in view of Christ's merits. She was saved by Christ, but in advance.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Defined by Pope Pius IX in 1854 (*Ineffabilis Deus*) (CCC 490–492).
- ✅ Mary was redeemed "in a more exalted fashion", by being preserved rather than cleansed (CCC 492).

**In Scripture**
- 🟢 The angel greets her as *kecharitomene*, "full of grace" or "one who has been graced" (Lk 1:28), a unique title used in place of her name.
- 🟢 Genesis 3:15 foretells enmity between the woman and the serpent.
- 🟢 The Ark of the Covenant had to be pure to carry God's presence; Mary is the new Ark (Lk 1:35, 39–56; compare 2 Sam 6).

**Image**
🟢 Someone can be saved from a pit by being pulled out after falling in, or by being caught before falling. Mary was caught before.

**Why it matters**
It shows what grace can do and what God wants for all of us: a humanity fully free from sin.
