# Q19 · Does the Bible, especially Genesis and Exodus, contradict science?

**Short answer**
No. Genesis was not written to teach physics or geology; it teaches who made the world and why. Science studies how the world works. Truth cannot contradict truth.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Faith and science cannot truly conflict, because the same God gave both reason and revelation (CCC 159).
- ✅ The creation accounts use symbolic language to express that God created everything, in order and goodness, with man at its summit (CCC 337).
- ✅ Saint Augustine already warned Christians not to make Scripture say things about nature that it does not teach.

**Examples**
- 🟢 The "days" of Genesis 1 form a teaching structure, not a stopwatch.
- 🟢 The Big Bang theory was first proposed by a Catholic priest, Georges Lemaître (1927).
- 🟡 Some events in Exodus are hard to confirm archaeologically; scholars debate details, not the faith that God freed His people.

**Why it matters**
Students should not have to choose between their science class and their faith.
