# Q32 · Did Jesus intend there to be only one Church?

**Short answer**
Yes. Jesus spoke of "my Church", in the singular, and prayed "that they may all be one" (Jn 17:21). Division among Christians is a wound, not His plan.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The Church of Christ "subsists in" the Catholic Church (CCC 816; *Lumen Gentium* 8).
- ✅ Many elements of truth and holiness exist outside her visible structure, and other Christians are truly our brothers and sisters (CCC 817–819).
- ✅ Working for Christian unity is a duty of every Catholic (CCC 820–822).

**In Scripture**
🟢 "One body and one Spirit… one Lord, one faith, one baptism" (Eph 4:4–5); "one flock, one shepherd" (Jn 10:16).

**Why it matters**
Unity is not uniformity, but it does mean one faith, one set of sacraments and one communion under Christ's chosen shepherds.
