# Q5 · What is the relationship between faith and reason?

**Short answer**
Faith and reason come from the same God, so they cannot truly contradict each other. Reason prepares for faith and can defend it; faith completes what reason can reach alone.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ "Faith and reason are like two wings on which the human spirit rises to the contemplation of truth" (*Fides et Ratio*, opening line).
- ✅ There can never be a real conflict between faith and reason, nor between faith and science rightly done (CCC 159).
- ✅ Reason alone can know that God exists; revelation tells us who God is, such as the Trinity (CCC 36–38; Vatican I).

**Two errors to avoid**
- 🟢 Fideism: believing with no use for reason.
- 🟢 Rationalism: accepting only what reason can prove on its own.

**Why it matters**
A Catholic never has to choose between thinking and believing. When they seem to clash, either the science or the theology has been misunderstood.
