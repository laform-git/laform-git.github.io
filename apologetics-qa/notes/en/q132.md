# Q132 · Making abortion illegal means forced parenthood.

**Short answer**
Once a child exists, the parents already are parents; the question is whether the child will live. No one is forced to raise a child: adoption allows parents to give the child life and a family.

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 Parenthood begins at conception, biologically. Abortion does not avoid becoming a parent; it ends the life of one's child.
- 🟢 Adoption gives life without requiring the birth parents to raise the child.
- 🟢 Fathers also have duties; justice requires that men support the mothers of their children.

**What the Church teaches**
✅ Adoption is a generous form of fruitfulness, and the Church encourages and supports it (CCC 2379). The Church asks fathers to take responsibility (CCC 2221).
