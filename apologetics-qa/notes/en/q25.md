# Q25 · Did Jesus really rise from the dead? Is Jesus Christ divine or human?

**Short answer**
Yes, He truly rose, bodily, on the third day. And Jesus is both: true God and true man, one divine Person with two natures.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The Resurrection is a real, historical event with signs and witnesses, and it goes beyond history as the entry of Christ's humanity into glory (CCC 639, 647).
- ✅ Jesus is "true God and true man", not part God and part man, nor a confused mix (CCC 464, 467; Council of Chalcedon, 451).

**Evidence for the Resurrection**
- 🟢 The empty tomb, first found by women, whose testimony counted little in that culture; an invented story would not choose them.
- 🟢 Many appearances, including to groups and to skeptics such as James and Paul (1 Cor 15:5–8).
- 🟢 The disciples' change from fear to boldness, even to death.

**Jesus' divinity**
✅ He forgives sins, accepts worship, calls Himself "I AM" (Jn 8:58), and Thomas calls Him "my Lord and my God" (Jn 20:28).

**Why it matters**
"If Christ has not been raised, your faith is futile" (1 Cor 15:17). Everything stands or falls here.

**For class**
