# Q16 · How can God be three Persons if He is only One?

**Short answer**
God is one in nature and three in Persons. "Nature" answers *what* God is; "person" answers *who*. There is one "what" and three "who's": Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The Trinity is one God; the three Persons do not share the divinity as if divided, each is God whole and entire (CCC 253).
- ✅ The Persons are really distinct by their relations: the Father begets, the Son is begotten, the Spirit proceeds (CCC 254–255).
- ✅ The Trinity is a mystery known only by revelation, not a contradiction (CCC 237).

**Why it is not a contradiction**
🟢 A contradiction would be "one God and three gods", or "one Person and three Persons". The Church says one *God* and three *Persons*, which are different kinds of "one" and "three".

**Images (all imperfect)**
🟡 Saint Augustine's mind, knowledge and love: one mind, three real acts. Every image limps; none replaces the mystery.

**Why it matters**
God is not lonely. From all eternity He is a communion of love, and we are made for that communion.
