# Q41 · Why don't Catholics accept sola fide?

**Short answer**
Catholics believe we are saved by grace through faith, not by our own works. But saving faith is a living faith that works through love. The only place the Bible says "faith alone" is to deny it: "a man is justified by works and not by faith alone" (Jas 2:24).

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Justification is a free gift of God's grace; no one can earn the first grace (CCC 1996, 2010).
- ✅ Justification is not only being declared righteous but being truly made holy within (CCC 1989).
- ✅ Faith must be lived in charity; "faith apart from works is dead" (Jas 2:26; CCC 1814–1815).

**Common ground**
🟢 In the 1999 *Joint Declaration on the Doctrine of Justification*, Catholics and Lutherans agreed: "By grace alone, in faith in Christ's saving work and not because of any merit on our part, we are accepted by God." Differences remain in emphasis and details 🟡.

**Why it matters**
Grace does not leave us as we were. It changes us, and good works are the fruit of that change.
