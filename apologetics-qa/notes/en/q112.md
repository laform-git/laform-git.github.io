# Q112 · What does the Bible say about artificial birth control?

**Short answer**
The Bible does not mention modern contraception by name, but it presents children as a blessing and sexual union as ordered to life and love. The Church's teaching rests on Scripture, Tradition and natural law together.

**In Scripture**
- 🟢 "Be fruitful and multiply" (Gen 1:28); "children are a heritage from the Lord" (Ps 127:3).
- 🟡 Onan is punished for spilling his seed to avoid conceiving (Gen 38:8–10). Catholics have traditionally read this as condemning contraception; others say his sin was refusing his duty to his brother's widow.
- 🟢 Some scholars link the Greek *pharmakeia* (sorcery, Gal 5:20) to potions, including those used to prevent or end pregnancy 🟡.

**What the Church teaches**
✅ Every action intended to render procreation impossible is intrinsically wrong (CCC 2370; *Humanae Vitae* 14, 1968).

**History**
🟢 Until 1930 all major Christian denominations opposed contraception.
