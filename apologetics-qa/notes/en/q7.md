# Q7 · Is God really a person, or just a force?

**Short answer**
God is personal: He knows, wills and loves. He is not a blind energy, though He is far beyond any human person.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ God reveals His name to Moses, "I AM WHO AM", and speaks, calls and makes covenants, which only a person does (Ex 3:14; CCC 203–206).
- ✅ God is one God in three Persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit (CCC 253–255).
- ✅ "God is love" (1 Jn 4:8), and love requires knowing and choosing.

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 The cause cannot give what it does not have. Intelligence and love exist in the world, so their first cause cannot be less than personal.
- 🟢 A force cannot be prayed to, cannot forgive and cannot love; the God of the Bible does all three.

**Why it matters**
If God were only a force, prayer would be talking to the weather. Because He is personal, we can know Him and be known by Him.
