# Q148 · What is the meaning of life?

**Short answer**
We were made by God to know Him, to love Him, to serve Him in this life, and to be happy with Him forever. Every good desire of the heart points there.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ God "created man to make him share in His own blessed life" (CCC 1).
- ✅ "The desire for God is written in the human heart, because man is created by God and for God" (CCC 27).
- ✅ Saint Augustine: "You have made us for yourself, O Lord, and our heart is restless until it rests in you" (CCC 30).

**Reasoning**
🟢 Nothing finite fully satisfies us: success, pleasure and possessions always leave us wanting more. That hunger points to an infinite Good.

**Why it matters**
If life has a purpose, every day, task and relationship can be part of a love story with God.

**For class**
What have you wanted that, once you got it, didn't satisfy as much as you expected? What does that tell you?
