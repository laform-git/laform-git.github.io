# Q133 · Abortion is necessary in cases of rape or incest.

**Short answer**
Rape and incest are horrific crimes, and victims deserve deep compassion, justice and care. But the child conceived is innocent, and a second act of violence does not undo the first.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The life of every innocent human being must be protected, whatever the circumstances of conception (CCC 2270, 2273).
- ✅ Rape is an intrinsically evil act and a deep wound (CCC 2356).
- ✅ Victims of sexual violence may receive treatment to prevent conception after an assault, as long as it does not end an existing pregnancy (U.S. bishops' *Ethical and Religious Directives*, no. 36).

**Facts**
🟢 Surveys of women obtaining abortions (Guttmacher Institute, 2004) found about 1% cited rape, and fewer cited incest, as a reason.

**How to respond**
🟢 With gentleness: victims need to be believed, protected and supported, not lectured. Programs exist to support women who become pregnant from rape and children conceived this way.

**For class**
How can we defend the unborn child while showing great tenderness to a victim?
