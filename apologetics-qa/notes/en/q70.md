# Q70 · Does the Church have a chauvinistic, male-dominated view of marriage?

**Short answer**
No. The Church teaches that husband and wife have equal dignity and that marriage is a mutual gift of self. Where Christians have treated women badly, they betrayed the Gospel rather than followed it.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Man and woman are created with equal dignity in God's image (CCC 369, 2334).
- ✅ Marriage requires the free consent of both spouses; no one can be forced (CCC 1625–1628).
- ✅ The submission in Ephesians 5 is mutual: "Be subject to one another out of reverence for Christ" (Eph 5:21; *Mulieris Dignitatem* 24).

**History**
🟢 The Church's insistence on free consent protected women from forced marriages, and her ban on divorce protected wives from being discarded.

**Why it matters**
Christian marriage asks both spouses to lay down their lives for each other, like Christ.
