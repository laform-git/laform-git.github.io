# Q149 · I believe in God, but why do I still have doubts?

**Short answer**
Questions and difficulties are normal, even for saints. Having a question is not the same as rejecting the faith. Bring your doubts to God and look for answers; faith often grows stronger through them.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Saint John Henry Newman: "Ten thousand difficulties do not make one doubt" (quoted in CCC 157). A difficulty is a question we can't yet answer; a doubt is refusing to believe.
- ✅ Voluntary doubt that disregards or refuses truth is a sin; involuntary doubt is a hesitation that can be overcome (CCC 2088).
- ✅ Faith seeks understanding (CCC 158).

**Examples**
🟢 Thomas doubted until he met the risen Jesus (Jn 20:24–29). Mother Teresa experienced decades of spiritual darkness while serving faithfully.
🟢 "I believe; help my unbelief!" (Mk 9:24) is a prayer Jesus answered.
