# Q115 · Are actions good or evil because God says so, or does God say so because they are good or evil?

**Short answer**
Neither option alone. This is the old "Euthyphro dilemma". Catholics answer: goodness is not an arbitrary command of God, nor a standard above God. Goodness flows from God's own nature, and His commands express who He is.

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 If good were whatever God commands, He could command cruelty and it would become good. That would make morality arbitrary.
- 🟢 If God commanded things because they are good by some outside standard, that standard would be above God.
- ✅ Third way: God *is* Goodness itself. He cannot command evil, not because something limits Him, but because He cannot contradict Himself (CCC 385, 2086).

**What the Church teaches**
✅ The moral law is rooted in God's wisdom and is "written" in our nature, which He created (CCC 1950–1954).

**Why it matters**
God's commandments are not random rules but a description of how humans flourish.
