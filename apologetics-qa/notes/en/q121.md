# Q121 · What kind of physical intimacy is permitted before marriage?

**Short answer**
Signs of affection that are respectful and appropriate to the relationship are good. Acts that arouse sexual passion, which by nature lead toward the full gift of marriage, belong to marriage.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Engaged couples are called to live chastity in continence, reserving for marriage the expressions of affection that belong to married love (CCC 2350).
- ✅ Chastity integrates sexuality into the whole person, and it grows with practice and grace (CCC 2337, 2342).

**Practical guidance**
- 🟢 Ask: does this show respect for the other person, or does it treat them as a means to pleasure?
- 🟢 Avoid situations that make temptation likely; set limits together before, not in the moment.
- 🟢 Prayer, confession and good friends help.

**Why it matters**
Chastity is not a "no" to love; it protects love so that it can become a full "yes" in marriage.
