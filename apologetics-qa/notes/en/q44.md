# Q44 · Why don't Catholics accept the five solas?

**Short answer**
Catholics agree with much of what each *sola* affirms, but not with what it denies. The problem is the word "alone".

**The five, briefly**
- **Sola scriptura (Scripture alone):** ✅ Catholics revere Scripture, but receive it with Tradition and the Church (CCC 80–85). See Q38.
- **Sola fide (faith alone):** ✅ We are saved through faith, but a faith that works through love (Gal 5:6; Jas 2:24). See Q41.
- **Sola gratia (grace alone):** 🟢 Catholics fully agree that salvation is by grace; they add that grace truly enables us to cooperate (CCC 2001–2002).
- **Solus Christus (Christ alone):** 🟢 Christ is the one mediator (1 Tim 2:5); Mary and the saints share in His mediation by prayer, not alongside it (CCC 970).
- **Soli Deo gloria (glory to God alone):** 🟢 Agreed; honoring saints honors the God who made them holy.

**Why it matters**
Catholics and Protestants share more than they often think. Dialogue goes better when we start from agreement.

**For class**
Pick one *sola*. What does a Catholic agree with in it, and where exactly is the difference?
