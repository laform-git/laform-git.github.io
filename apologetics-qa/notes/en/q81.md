# Q81 · Why can't women be ordained priests in the Catholic Church?

**Short answer**
Because Jesus chose only men as apostles, and the Church holds she has no authority to change what Christ established. This is not a judgment about women's worth or ability.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ "Only a baptized man validly receives sacred ordination" (CCC 1577, canon 1024).
- ✅ Saint John Paul II declared that "the Church has no authority whatsoever to confer priestly ordination on women", a judgment "to be definitively held" (*Ordinatio Sacerdotalis*, 1994).
- ✅ Women and men have equal dignity (CCC 2334).

**Reasons given**
- 🟢 Jesus broke many customs about women (speaking with the Samaritan woman, women disciples), so His choice of only men was not just cultural pressure.
- 🟢 The priest acts *in persona Christi*, in the person of Christ the Bridegroom, toward the Church His bride.
- 🟢 The holiest human person in the Church, Mary, was not a priest.

**Why it matters**
Greatness in the Church is holiness, not office. Women have shaped the Church as saints, Doctors of the Church, founders and leaders.
