# Q157 · Why did Satan and the other fallen angels reject God if they were not tempted like Adam and Eve?

**Short answer**
Angels are pure spirits with great intelligence and real freedom. Some, through pride, freely chose themselves over God. No outside tempter was needed; the root of their sin was within their own will.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Satan and the other demons were created naturally good by God, but became evil by their own doing (CCC 391, Fourth Lateran Council).
- ✅ Their sin was a radical, irrevocable rejection of God (CCC 392–393).
- ✅ "It is the irrevocable character of their choice, and not a defect in the infinite divine mercy, that makes the angels' sin unforgivable" (CCC 393).

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 Pride: wanting to be like God without God (compare Gen 3:5; Isa 14:13–14 is often applied to Satan).
- 🟢 Angels see clearly and decide all at once, without the gradual weakness of humans, so their choice is final.

**Why it matters**
Pride is the root sin. Humility, like Mary's, is its cure.
