# Q57 · Where is the Sacrament of Baptism in the Bible?

**Short answer**
Jesus commands it: "Go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit" (Mt 28:19). Baptism is the door to Christian life.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Baptism frees from sin, gives new birth as children of God, and makes us members of Christ and the Church (CCC 1213).
- ✅ It is necessary for salvation for those who have heard the Gospel and can ask for it (CCC 1257).
- ✅ It marks the soul permanently and is never repeated (CCC 1272).

**In Scripture**
- 🟢 "Unless one is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God" (Jn 3:5).
- 🟢 "Repent and be baptized… for the forgiveness of your sins" (Acts 2:38).
- 🟢 "Baptism now saves you" (1 Pet 3:21).
- 🟢 We are "buried with Him by baptism" (Rom 6:3–4).

**Why it matters**
Baptism is not just a symbol; it truly changes who we are.
