# Q27 · Why did Christ have to suffer and die on the Cross?

**Short answer**
God did not need blood to be appeased. The Cross shows how far love goes: Christ entered the full weight of sin and death, which are the real effects of evil, and turned them into the place of our healing.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Jesus' death was part of God's plan, foretold in Scripture, yet freely accepted by Christ out of love (CCC 599–600, 609).
- ✅ His obedience unto death repairs our disobedience (CCC 615; Rom 5:19).
- ✅ "Greater love has no man than this, that a man lay down his life for his friends" (Jn 15:13).

**Ways to understand it**
- 🟢 Love proves itself by sacrifice.
- 🟢 Christ shows sin for what it is: it killed God made man.
- 🟡 Theologians use several models together: sacrifice, ransom, satisfaction, victory over death. No single model says everything.

**Why it matters**
In every suffering, a Christian can look at the Cross and know God has been there first.
