# Q126 · "My body, my choice": a woman has the right to control her body; just as no one can be forced to donate a kidney, a pregnant woman cannot be forced to keep the fetus alive.

**Short answer**
Bodily autonomy is real and important, but the unborn child has a body of their own, with their own DNA, heart and life. And abortion is not just refusing to help; it is ending a life that already depends on the mother by nature.

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 This argument (from Judith Jarvis Thomson's "violinist" analogy, 1971) compares pregnancy to being kidnapped and attached to a stranger. But in most pregnancies the child is not a stranger: parents have special duties to their own children.
- 🟢 Refusing to donate a kidney means letting a person die of their own illness. Abortion directly ends a life.
- 🟢 Parents are already required by law to feed and care for their born children, even when it is a burden.

**What the Church teaches**
✅ Freedom does not include a right to take the life of an innocent person (CCC 2273). The Church also insists on support for mothers in difficulty.
