# Q89 · Why do Catholics make the sign of the cross?

**Short answer**
The sign of the cross is a short creed with the body: it professes the Trinity ("in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit") and the Cross by which we are saved. It recalls our baptism.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The Christian begins the day, prayers and activities with the sign of the cross (CCC 2157).
- ✅ At baptism we are marked with the cross, the sign of Christ's grace (CCC 1235).

**History**
🟢 Around 200 AD, Tertullian wrote that Christians traced the cross on their foreheads at every step, coming and going, dressing, eating and lighting lamps.

**Why it matters**
In a few seconds it reminds us whose we are and what saved us.
