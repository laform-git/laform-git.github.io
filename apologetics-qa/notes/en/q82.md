# Q82 · Where is the Sacrament of Anointing of the Sick in the Bible?

**Short answer**
"Is any among you sick? Let him call for the elders of the Church, and let them pray over him, anointing him with oil in the name of the Lord… and if he has committed sins, he will be forgiven" (Jas 5:14–15).

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Anointing gives strength, peace and courage in illness or old age, unites the sick person to Christ's Passion, forgives sins, and can restore health if God wills (CCC 1520–1523).
- ✅ It is not only for the dying; anyone seriously ill or elderly and weak may receive it, and it can be repeated (CCC 1514–1515).

**In Scripture**
🟢 The apostles "anointed with oil many that were sick and healed them" (Mk 6:13).

**Why it matters**
No one has to face serious illness alone. Christ comes close through His Church.
