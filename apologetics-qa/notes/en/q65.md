# Q65 · Where is the Sacrament of the Eucharist (Communion) in the Bible?

**Short answer**
At the Last Supper Jesus took bread and said, "This is my body", and took the cup and said, "This is my blood of the covenant", then commanded, "Do this in remembrance of me" (Lk 22:19–20).

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Christ instituted the Eucharist at the Last Supper as the memorial of His death and Resurrection (CCC 1323, 1337).
- ✅ The Eucharist is "the source and summit of the Christian life" (CCC 1324).

**In Scripture**
- 🟢 The four accounts: Mt 26:26–28; Mk 14:22–24; Lk 22:19–20; 1 Cor 11:23–26.
- 🟢 Paul warns that whoever eats unworthily "will be guilty of profaning the body and blood of the Lord" (1 Cor 11:27).
- 🟢 Foreshadowed in the Passover lamb (Ex 12), the manna (Ex 16) and Melchizedek's bread and wine (Gen 14:18).

**Why it matters**
The Eucharist is not just a meal of remembrance; it is Jesus Himself, given to us.
