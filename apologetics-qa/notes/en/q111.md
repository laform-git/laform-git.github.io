# Q111 · Does objective morality exist, or is all morality relative?

**Short answer**
Objective morality exists. Some things are right or wrong for everyone, regardless of feelings or culture, because they are rooted in human nature and in God.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The natural law is "written and engraved in the soul of each and every man", knowable by reason (CCC 1954).
- ✅ It is universal and unchanging in its principles (CCC 1956, 1958).
- ✅ "The work of the law is written on their hearts" (Rom 2:15).

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 Everyone, including relativists, protests when treated unjustly, which shows they believe in a real standard.
- 🟢 If morality were only cultural, reformers who opposed slavery or racism could never be right against their cultures.
- 🟢 Cultures differ in customs but share core rules: don't murder the innocent, honor parents, keep promises.

**Why it matters**
Objective morality makes human rights possible and makes moral growth meaningful.
