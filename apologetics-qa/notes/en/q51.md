# Q51 · Where is the Perpetual Virginity of Mary in the Bible? Did Mary have other children besides Jesus?

**Short answer**
The Church teaches that Mary remained a virgin before, during and after Jesus' birth. The "brothers and sisters" of Jesus in the Gospels are relatives, a common meaning of the word in that culture.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Mary is "ever-virgin" (CCC 499–501).
- ✅ The "brothers" named in Mk 6:3, James and Joses, are sons of another Mary (Mt 27:56; CCC 500).

**Reasons**
- 🟢 Hebrew and Aramaic had no word for "cousin"; "brother" covered relatives (Abram calls his nephew Lot "brother", Gen 13:8).
- 🟢 On the Cross, Jesus entrusts His mother to John (Jn 19:26–27); this would be strange if she had other sons.
- 🟢 Early Christians, and even Luther, Calvin and Zwingli, held Mary's perpetual virginity.

**Why it matters**
Mary's whole life was given to God. Her virginity is a sign of total belonging to Him.
