# Q53 · How is Mary our Mother, as well as being the Mother of Jesus?

**Short answer**
Jesus gave her to us from the Cross. United to Christ, we become members of His Body, so the mother of the Head is mother of the members.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Mary is "Mother of the Church" and our mother in the order of grace (CCC 963, 968–970).
- ✅ Her motherhood continues: she intercedes for us and cares for Christ's brothers and sisters (CCC 969).

**In Scripture**
- 🟢 "Woman, behold your son… Behold your mother" (Jn 19:26–27). John stands for every beloved disciple.
- 🟢 The woman of Revelation 12 has "the rest of her offspring… who keep the commandments of God and bear testimony to Jesus" (Rev 12:17).

**Why it matters**
A Christian is never an orphan. Mary loves us as she loved her Son, and leads us to Him.
