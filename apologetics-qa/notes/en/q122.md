# Q122 · What kind of physical intimacy is permitted within marriage?

**Short answer**
Within marriage, sexual love is good and holy. The Church's guiding principle is that each act of intercourse must remain a true marital act: loving, mutual, respectful, and open to life. Affection that leads to and completes in natural intercourse is part of married love.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The acts by which spouses unite are "noble and honorable" and a source of joy (CCC 2362).
- ✅ Each marital act must keep both meanings: union and openness to life (CCC 2366, 2369).
- ✅ Spouses must respect each other's dignity; love never uses or forces the other (CCC 2363).

**Moral theologians' guidance**
🟢 Acts of affection and arousal are good when they lead to natural intercourse; acts that replace it and end outside it are not in keeping with the marital act. Couples with specific questions should speak with a trusted priest.

**Why it matters**
Married sexuality is not just tolerated; it is a gift that images God's faithful, life-giving love.
