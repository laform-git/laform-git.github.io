# Q40 · Why are Protestant and Catholic Bibles different?

**Short answer**
Catholic Bibles have 73 books; most Protestant Bibles have 66. The difference is seven Old Testament books (and parts of two others) that the early Church used but that Protestants later set aside.

**The books**
✅ Tobit, Judith, 1 and 2 Maccabees, Wisdom, Sirach and Baruch, plus parts of Esther and Daniel, called "deuterocanonical" (CCC 120).

**History**
- 🟢 These books were in the Septuagint, the Greek Old Testament the apostles usually quoted.
- 🟢 Church councils at Rome (382), Hippo (393) and Carthage (397) listed them as Scripture. Trent (1546) confirmed the same list.
- 🟢 Martin Luther moved them to an appendix, following the later Jewish canon; most Protestant Bibles eventually dropped them.

**Why it matters**
The New Testament books are the same in both. The question shows again why the Church's authority matters: someone had to decide what belongs in the Bible.
