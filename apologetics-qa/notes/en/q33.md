# Q33 · How do we know that Christ wanted the Church to be headed by a Pope?

**Short answer**
Jesus renamed Simon "Peter" (Rock), built the Church on him, gave him the keys of the Kingdom and told him to strengthen the others and feed His sheep. The bishop of Rome continues Peter's role.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Christ made Peter the visible foundation of His Church and chief shepherd (CCC 552–553, 881).
- ✅ The Pope, as Peter's successor, is "the perpetual and visible source and foundation of unity" (CCC 882).

**In Scripture**
- 🟢 Mt 16:18–19: "You are Peter… I will give you the keys." In Isaiah 22:22, the keys mark the king's chief steward, an office with successors.
- 🟢 Lk 22:31–32: "Strengthen your brethren." Jn 21:15–17: "Feed my sheep."
- 🟢 Peter speaks first and leads in Acts 1–2 and 15.

**In history**
🟢 Early writers like Clement of Rome (c. 96) and Irenaeus (c. 180) show the Church of Rome's special authority.

**Why it matters**
A family needs a father; a flock needs a shepherd. The papacy keeps the Church one across centuries and continents.
