# Q135 · I'm personally against abortion, but I can't impose my beliefs on others.

**Short answer**
If you oppose abortion because it ends a human life, that is not a private belief like a favorite food; it concerns another person's life. We would not say "I'm personally against slavery, but I won't impose that on others."

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 Ask why you are personally against it. If the answer is "because it kills a child", the same reason applies to everyone.
- 🟢 Protecting life is a matter of human rights, knowable by reason, not a religious ritual.
- 🟢 Opposing abortion also means helping mothers in practical ways, not only in words.

**What the Church teaches**
✅ Catholics in public life have a grave duty to oppose laws that attack human life (*Evangelium Vitae* 73). Everyone is called to defend life in the ways open to them.
