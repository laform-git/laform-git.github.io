# Q96 · How do I dialogue with Jehovah's Witnesses?

**Short answer**
Be friendly, know your faith, and focus on the central question: who is Jesus? Jehovah's Witnesses deny the Trinity and the divinity of Christ, teaching that Jesus is God's first creation.

**Who they are**
🟢 Founded in the 1870s from Charles Taze Russell's Bible Student movement; led today by the Governing Body of the Watch Tower Society. They use their own translation, the *New World Translation*.

**Key points to discuss**
- ✅ **Jesus is God:** "the Word was God" (Jn 1:1); Thomas: "My Lord and my God" (Jn 20:28); "before Abraham was, I AM" (Jn 8:58).
- ✅ **The Holy Spirit is a Person:** He speaks, teaches and can be grieved (Acts 13:2; Jn 14:26; Eph 4:30).
- ✅ **The soul and hell:** they deny both; see Lk 16:19–31; Mt 25:46.
- 🟢 **Authority:** ask why a group founded in the 19th century should be trusted over 2,000 years of Christian witness.

**Tips**
🟢 Keep Bible verses ready, compare translations, and invite them back.
