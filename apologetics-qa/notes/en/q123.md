# Q123 · Where in the Bible does Jesus ever forbid abortion?

**Short answer**
Jesus never mentions abortion by name, just as He never mentions many specific crimes. He reaffirmed "You shall not kill" (Mt 19:18) and showed special love for children. Scripture shows life in the womb as a real human person known by God.

**In Scripture**
- 🟢 "Before I formed you in the womb I knew you" (Jer 1:5).
- 🟢 "You knit me together in my mother's womb" (Ps 139:13).
- 🟢 John the Baptist, unborn, "leaped in her womb" at Mary's greeting (Lk 1:41–44).
- 🟢 The Greek word *brephos* is used for both an unborn child (Lk 1:41) and a newborn (Lk 2:12).

**Early Christians**
🟢 The *Didache* (late 1st century): "You shall not murder a child by abortion."

**What the Church teaches**
✅ Human life must be respected and protected absolutely from the moment of conception (CCC 2270–2271).
