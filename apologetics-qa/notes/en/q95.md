# Q95 · What do Scientologists believe?

**Short answer**
Scientology was founded by science-fiction writer L. Ron Hubbard in the 1950s. It teaches that each person is an immortal spiritual being (a "thetan") burdened by past traumas, which can be cleared through paid counseling sessions called "auditing".

**Key ideas**
- 🟢 Based on Hubbard's book *Dianetics* (1950); the Church of Scientology was founded in 1954.
- 🟢 Auditing uses a device called an E-meter. Members progress through levels toward becoming "Clear" and "Operating Thetan".
- 🟡 Advanced teachings are kept secret from new members; former members and journalists have published them, and the group disputes some accounts.
- 🟡 The organization has faced many lawsuits and criticism over its practices and costs.

**Main differences with Christianity**
✅ No personal Creator God at the center, no need for a Savior, and salvation is earned through costly techniques, not received as a free gift of grace (CCC 1996).
