# Q90 · Do Catholics worship graven images? Exodus 20 says "You shall not make for yourself a graven image."

**Short answer**
No. The commandment forbids making images to worship as gods. God Himself ordered sacred images, such as the cherubim on the Ark. Catholics use statues and icons the way families use photos: to remember and honor, never to adore.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Idolatry is forbidden; worship belongs to God alone (CCC 2112–2114).
- ✅ Since God became visible in Jesus, Christians may make images of Christ and the saints (CCC 2131).
- ✅ "The honor rendered to an image passes to its prototype"; veneration of images is not adoration (CCC 2132, Council of Nicaea II, 787).

**In Scripture**
🟢 God commands cherubim of gold for the Ark (Ex 25:18–20) and a bronze serpent (Num 21:8–9); Solomon's Temple was full of carved images (1 Kgs 6:23–35).

**Why it matters**
Beauty and images help us pray. The danger is idolatry of things like money, success or ourselves.

**For class**
Do you keep a photo of someone you love? Do you worship the photo? What does it do for you?
