# Q140 · What's wrong with masturbation if it doesn't hurt anyone?

**Short answer**
Sexuality is meant to be a gift to another person in love and in openness to life. Masturbation turns it inward toward oneself, and it can become a habit that weakens self-mastery. The Church also recognizes that guilt may be lessened by habit, anxiety or immaturity.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Masturbation is "an intrinsically and gravely disordered action" because sexual pleasure is sought outside the relationship of mutual self-giving (CCC 2352).
- ✅ "To form an equitable judgment about the subjects' moral responsibility… one must take into account affective immaturity, force of acquired habit, conditions of anxiety or other psychological or social factors that lessen, if not even reduce to a minimum, moral culpability" (CCC 2352).

**Hope**
🟢 Growth in chastity is a gradual process (CCC 2343). Confession, prayer, healthy friendships, exercise and avoiding triggers help.
