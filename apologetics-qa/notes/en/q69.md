# Q69 · Where is the Sacrament of Matrimony in the Bible?

**Short answer**
God created marriage "in the beginning" (Gen 2:24), and Jesus raised it to a sacrament, a sign of His own love for the Church (Eph 5:31–32).

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Marriage between a baptized man and woman is a sacrament (CCC 1601).
- ✅ Its properties are unity and indissolubility, and it is ordered to the good of the spouses and to children (CCC 1643–1654).

**In Scripture**
- 🟢 "What God has joined together, let no man put asunder" (Mt 19:4–6).
- 🟢 Jesus' first sign is at a wedding in Cana (Jn 2:1–11).
- 🟢 Paul calls marriage "a great mystery… I mean in reference to Christ and the Church" (Eph 5:32). In the Latin Bible, "mystery" was translated *sacramentum*.

**Why it matters**
Christian spouses are a living image of Christ's faithful love for His people.
