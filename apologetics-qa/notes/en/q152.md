# Q152 · Why do we need to be persistent in prayer? Why doesn't God answer my prayers?

**Short answer**
God always hears, and always answers, but not always with "yes" or "now". Sometimes the answer is "wait", or "I have something better". Persistence deepens our trust and purifies what we ask for.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Jesus taught "always to pray and not lose heart" (Lk 18:1–8; parable of the persistent widow).
- ✅ When prayer seems unheard, we should ask whether we pray as God's children, seeking His will (CCC 2735–2737).
- ✅ "Do not be troubled if you do not immediately receive from God what you ask… for He wants to do something even greater for you" (Evagrius, quoted in CCC 2737).

**Examples**
🟢 Saint Monica prayed about 17 years for her son Augustine, who became a great saint.
🟢 Jesus prayed "let this cup pass", and the Father's answer was the Resurrection.
