# Q124 · Why is the Church against abortion? Why does it try to impose this belief on non-Catholics?

**Short answer**
Because abortion ends the life of an innocent human being, and protecting innocent life is a matter of justice for everyone, not a private religious rule. Science shows a new human life begins at conception; reason, not only faith, concludes it should be protected.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Direct abortion is gravely contrary to the moral law (CCC 2271).
- ✅ The right to life is a basic right that civil law must recognize; it does not depend on parents or the state (CCC 2273).
- ✅ The Church also calls for real support for mothers and families (CCC 2274; *Evangelium Vitae* 99).

**Reasoning**
🟢 Laws against killing the innocent are not "religious impositions"; they are the foundation of every just society. Many non-religious people also oppose abortion on these grounds.

**Why it matters**
Being pro-life means caring for both mother and child, before and after birth.
