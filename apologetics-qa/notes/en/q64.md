# Q64 · Where else in the Bible does Jesus speak about the Eucharist, and why are most Christians unaware of it?

**Short answer**
The longest teaching is the Bread of Life discourse in John 6, where Jesus says, "Unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink His blood, you have no life in you" (Jn 6:53). Many read it only symbolically, so its force is missed.

**Key points in John 6**
- 🟢 Jesus repeats the teaching more strongly when people object; in verses 54–58 John switches to a Greek verb meaning "chew" or "gnaw" (*trōgō*).
- 🟢 Many disciples leave: "This is a hard saying" (Jn 6:60, 66). Jesus does not call them back to explain it as a metaphor.
- 🟢 Peter answers: "Lord, to whom shall we go? You have the words of eternal life" (Jn 6:68).

**Other places**
🟢 The road to Emmaus: they knew Him "in the breaking of the bread" (Lk 24:30–35); the early Church "devoted themselves… to the breaking of the bread" (Acts 2:42); and 1 Cor 10:16; 11:23–29.

**What the Church teaches**
✅ The Church reads John 6 as Jesus' promise of the Eucharist (CCC 1336, 1384).
