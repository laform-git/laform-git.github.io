# Q23 · Why did God permit polygamy, divorce, slavery and revenge in the Old Testament?

**Short answer**
God tolerated some customs for a time "because of the hardness of your hearts" (Mt 19:8), while slowly leading His people toward the full moral truth revealed in Christ. Permitting is not approving.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Polygamy and divorce were tolerated, but "from the beginning it was not so"; Jesus restores marriage to its original unity (Mt 19:3–9; CCC 1610, 1614).
- ✅ The Mosaic law limited these practices: "an eye for an eye" capped revenge instead of encouraging it.
- ✅ The Church teaches that enslaving persons offends human dignity (CCC 2414).

**Why gradual?**
- 🟢 A good teacher starts where students are. God formed a people over many centuries, not in one day.
- 🟢 Old Testament law already protected slaves and foreigners in ways unusual for its time (Ex 21; Dt 15; Dt 23:15–16).

**Why it matters**
The direction of Scripture is toward greater dignity and love, reaching its peak in the Gospel.
