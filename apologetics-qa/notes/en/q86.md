# Q86 · Why do Catholics call priests "Father" when Matthew 23:9 says "Call no man your father"?

**Short answer**
Jesus was warning against pride and against putting any human in God's place, not banning a word. The same New Testament uses "father" for earthly and spiritual fathers many times.

**In Scripture**
- 🟢 Paul: "I became your father in Christ Jesus through the Gospel" (1 Cor 4:15).
- 🟢 Stephen addresses the Jewish leaders as "brethren and fathers" (Acts 7:2).
- 🟢 "Honor your father and mother" (Mt 15:4) and "our father Abraham" (Rom 4:12).
- 🟢 In the same passage Jesus also says "call no man teacher" (Mt 23:10), yet Christians rightly call people teachers.

**What it means**
✅ Priests are spiritual fathers: they give new life in baptism, feed with the Eucharist and care for their parish family.

**Why it matters**
Reading one verse alone can turn a warning about pride into a rule Jesus never meant.
