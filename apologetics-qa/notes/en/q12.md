# Q12 · Who or what is God? What are God's attributes?

**Short answer**
God is the One who simply is: infinite, eternal, unchanging, all-knowing, all-powerful and perfectly good. He is not one being among others but the source of all being.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ God is "I AM": He does not receive existence; He is existence itself (Ex 3:14; CCC 212–213).
- ✅ God is one, almighty, eternal, infinite, the Truth and Love itself (CCC 200–202, 214–221, 268–271).
- ✅ Our words about God are true but limited; He is always greater than what we can say (CCC 39–43).

**Main attributes**
- 🟢 Simple: He has no parts.
- 🟢 Eternal: no beginning, end or succession of moments.
- 🟢 Immutable: He does not change, because He lacks nothing.
- 🟢 Omnipotent, omniscient, omnipresent.
- ✅ Above all, God is love (1 Jn 4:8).

**Why it matters**
A God who could be measured would not be God. Knowing what He is helps us avoid making Him in our own image.
