# Q98 · What was the Filioque controversy, and what caused the Great Schism of 1054?

**Short answer**
The Great Schism split the Catholic West and the Orthodox East. Its causes were theological (the *Filioque*), about authority (the role of the Pope), and cultural and political, building over centuries.

**The Filioque**
- 🟢 The Nicene Creed (381) says the Holy Spirit "proceeds from the Father". In the West, "and the Son" (*Filioque*) was added, first in Spain (Toledo, 589), and later accepted in the Roman liturgy (around 1014).
- ✅ Catholic teaching: the Spirit proceeds from the Father *through* and *with* the Son, as from one principle; this does not deny the Father as source (CCC 246–248).
- 🟡 The East objected both to the theology and to changing a common creed without a council.

**1054**
🟢 Papal legate Cardinal Humbert and Patriarch Michael Cerularius excommunicated each other. The split hardened after the Crusaders sacked Constantinople in 1204.

**Today**
🟢 In 1965 Pope Paul VI and Patriarch Athenagoras lifted the mutual excommunications. Dialogue continues.
