# Q110 · Is it ever okay to steal?

**Short answer**
Stealing, taking what belongs to another against their reasonable will, is wrong. But in extreme need, when a person's life depends on it, taking what is necessary is not stealing, because the goods of the earth are meant for everyone.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ The seventh commandment forbids unjustly taking or keeping another's goods (CCC 2401, 2408–2409).
- ✅ "There is no theft if consent can be presumed or if refusal is contrary to reason and the universal destination of goods. This is the case in obvious and urgent necessity when the only way to provide for immediate, essential needs (food, shelter, clothing) is to put at one's disposal and use the property of others" (CCC 2408).
- ✅ What was stolen must be returned or repaid (CCC 2412).

**Examples of hidden theft**
🟢 Wasting time paid for at work, cheating on taxes, copying software or schoolwork, not paying debts (CCC 2409).

**Why it matters**
Private property is good, but it serves people. Christians share generously so that no one is driven to such need.
