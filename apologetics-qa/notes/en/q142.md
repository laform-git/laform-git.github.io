# Q142 · Why is the Church against in vitro fertilization? Shouldn't couples be able to use technology against infertility?

**Short answer**
The Church feels deeply with couples suffering infertility and supports medicine that helps the body conceive naturally. IVF is not accepted because it replaces the marital act with a lab procedure and usually involves creating, freezing and discarding embryos.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Techniques that separate conception from the marital act are morally unacceptable (CCC 2376–2377; *Donum Vitae*; *Dignitas Personae* 12–16).
- ✅ A child is a gift, not something owed; there is no "right to a child" (CCC 2378).
- ✅ Research to reduce infertility is encouraged (CCC 2375).

**Why IVF raises concerns**
- 🟢 Many more embryos are typically created than are transferred; most never come to birth.
- 🟢 Embryos are often screened, frozen or discarded.

**Important**
✅ Children conceived through IVF are loved by God with the same dignity as every child. The Church's teaching concerns the method, never the worth of the person.
