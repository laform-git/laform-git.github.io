# Q146 · Does the Church ever permit capital punishment (the death penalty)?

**Short answer**
Today the Church teaches that the death penalty is inadmissible, because modern societies can protect people without killing the guilty, and every person keeps their dignity and the possibility of conversion.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Revised CCC 2267 (2018): "the death penalty is inadmissible because it is an attack on the inviolability and dignity of the person", and the Church works for its abolition worldwide.
- ✅ Saint John Paul II already taught that cases where execution is absolutely necessary are "very rare, if not practically nonexistent" (*Evangelium Vitae* 56, 1995).

**History**
🟡 For centuries the Church allowed that legitimate authority could use the death penalty in grave cases. How the 2018 change develops that teaching is discussed by theologians.

**Why it matters**
Even the worst criminal is a person made in God's image; justice should seek protection and, if possible, conversion.
