# Q113 · If people disagree about morality, shouldn't we tolerate each other's opinions and not impose our morality on others?

**Short answer**
Christians should respect every person and their freedom of conscience. But tolerance does not mean every view is equally true, and every law "imposes" some morality. The question is which morality protects human dignity.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Every person must follow a well-formed conscience, and no one may be forced to act against it in religious matters (CCC 1782, 2106).
- ✅ Respect for persons does not require approving every act (CCC 1793).
- ✅ Civil law should protect basic rights such as life, based on natural law that all can know by reason (CCC 2273).

**Reasoning**
- 🟢 Laws against theft, racism or child abuse "impose morality" too, and rightly.
- 🟢 Real tolerance means bearing with people we disagree with, which presumes we do disagree.

**Why it matters**
Christians can propose their moral views in public debate like anyone else, with reasons and charity, not force.
