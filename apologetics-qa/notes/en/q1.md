# Q1 · What is truth? Can I know anything with certainty?

**Short answer**
Truth is the agreement between what the mind holds and what actually is. Yes, some things can be known with certainty, and every doubt already relies on them.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Truth is "the conformity of the mind with reality", the classic definition of Aquinas.
- ✅ Some first principles are self-evident: something exists, and a thing cannot be and not be at the same time in the same respect. They cannot be proved, because every proof uses them.
- ✅ God is Truth itself, and human reason can reach truth, including the truth that God exists (CCC 2465–2466; CCC 36).

**Objection**
"Nothing can be known for certain." 🟢 That sentence claims to be certain, so it refutes itself. A chain of proofs cannot go back forever; it needs a first truth that is seen, not proved.

**Why it matters**
If no truth can be known, no faith, science or justice is possible. Every serious conversation starts here.

**For class**
Name one thing you are certain of. How do you know it without a proof?
