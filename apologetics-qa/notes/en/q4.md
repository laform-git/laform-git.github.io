# Q4 · What is faith, and why is it hard to believe in God?

**Short answer**
Faith is trusting God and accepting what He has revealed because He reveals it. It is hard because God is not seen, because we are wounded by sin, and because faith asks us to change our lives.

**What the Church teaches**
- ✅ Faith is both a gift of God and a free human act; it is "a personal adherence of man to God" (CCC 150, 153–155).
- ✅ Faith is certain, more certain than human knowledge, because it rests on God's word, though its truths can seem obscure to reason (CCC 157).
- ✅ Faith can be lost, so it must be nourished by prayer, Scripture and the sacraments (CCC 162).

**Why it is hard**
- 🟢 God is spirit and is not seen with the eyes.
- 🟢 Suffering and evil seem to argue against Him.
- 🟢 Belief has consequences: it asks for conversion, and the will often resists before the mind does.

**Why it matters**
Faith is not believing without reasons; it is trusting a Person for good reasons when we cannot see everything.

**For class**
