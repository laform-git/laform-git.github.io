# Substances — 01 Alcohol, Risks and Dependence
*Alcohol is legal, social and everywhere, which is exactly why its harms are underestimated. It is one of the leading preventable causes of death and cancer, it damages sleep and mood, and about one in ten adults develops a use disorder.*

> **Educational, not medical advice.** Stopping heavy daily drinking suddenly can cause dangerous withdrawal (seizures, delirium tremens). Anyone drinking heavily should stop with medical help. SAMHSA Helpline: 1-800-662-4357.

## Core Insight

Alcohol is a toxin the body treats as an emergency to clear. Small amounts feel relaxing because alcohol boosts GABA (the brain's brake) and blocks glutamate (the accelerator). The brain then pushes back, which is why the next morning brings anxiety, poor sleep and low mood, and why tolerance and dependence build.

## How Big the Problem Is

- ✅ **WHO (2023):** "no level of alcohol consumption is safe for our health" with respect to cancer risk.
- ✅ **U.S. Surgeon General Advisory (January 2025):** alcohol is the third leading preventable cause of cancer in the U.S., after tobacco and obesity, linked to at least seven cancers (breast, colorectal, esophagus, liver, mouth, throat, voice box), contributing to about 100,000 cancer cases and 20,000 cancer deaths a year. Most Americans don't know this.
- 🟢 **CDC:** excessive drinking causes about 178,000 deaths a year in the U.S. (2020–2021 estimate).
- 🟢 Roughly one in ten U.S. adults has an alcohol use disorder in a given year (NSDUH surveys).

## What Alcohol Does to the Body

| System | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Brain** | Impaired judgment and coordination; blackouts; long-term shrinkage and memory problems with heavy use |
| **Sleep** | Faster sleep onset but suppressed REM and broken second half of the night |
| **Mood** | Rebound anxiety; heavy use roughly doubles depression risk; many alcohol-related depressions lift after 2–4 weeks sober |
| **Liver** | Fatty liver → alcoholic hepatitis → cirrhosis |
| **Heart** | High blood pressure, irregular heartbeat (atrial fibrillation) |
| **Hormones** | Lower testosterone in men; disrupted cycles in women |
| **Pregnancy** | Fetal alcohol spectrum disorders; no safe amount in pregnancy |

🟡 Older studies suggesting that moderate drinking protects the heart are now widely questioned; many "non-drinkers" in those studies were former heavy drinkers or sick people, which made moderate drinkers look healthier.

## Who Is at Risk (from Pihl)

- ✅ **Family history:** in Pihl's studies, sons from families with alcoholism across generations (and anxiety-sensitive people) showed a stronger reward response to alcohol, felt better for longer, and had their response to a stressful event dampened by drinking, which makes alcohol more reinforcing for them.
- 🟢 **Personality:** anxious people drink to relieve tension; sensation-seekers drink for the thrill. Prevention matched to personality (the SURPS program) reduced teen drinking more than generic programs.
- 🟢 **Early start:** drinking before age 15 is linked to much higher later dependence.

## Danger Signs of a Problem

- Needing more to feel the same effect (tolerance)
- Shakiness, sweating, anxiety or poor sleep when not drinking (withdrawal)
- Drinking more or longer than intended; failed attempts to cut down
- Drinking to cope with stress, grief or sleep
- Continuing despite harm to work, health or relationships

## Treatment That Works

- ✅ **Medications:** naltrexone and acamprosate reduce relapse; disulfiram for some. All are underused.
- ✅ **Therapy:** cognitive behavioral therapy, motivational interviewing.
- 🟢 **Mutual help:** a 2020 Cochrane review found Alcoholics Anonymous and twelve-step facilitation as effective as or better than other treatments for staying abstinent.
- ✅ **Medical detox** for heavy daily drinkers, because withdrawal can be life-threatening.

## Key Corrections

- ❌ "A glass of wine is good for the heart." ✅ Not supported as a reason to drink; cancer risk starts at low levels.
- ❌ "Alcohol helps me sleep." ✅ It shortens time to fall asleep but damages sleep quality and REM.
- ❌ "It's legal, so it's safe." ✅ Legal status reflects history and culture, not safety.

## Practical Takeaway

Less is better and none is best for health. For anyone who suspects a problem: talk to a doctor, and never stop heavy daily drinking abruptly without medical help.
