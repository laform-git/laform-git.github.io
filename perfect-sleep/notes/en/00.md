## Often-missed factors

- **Nocturnal reflux.** Silent reflux fragments sleep without fully waking the sleeper, so having no heartburn does not settle it. First moves cost nothing and stack with other benefits: sleep on the left side, raise the head of the bed 6–8 inches (blocks under the legs, never extra pillows), keep 3 hours from last bite to bed. If fragmentation persists, ask the physician about nocturnal acid exposure. [[PERFECT SLEEP — 15 GERD and Sleep]].
- **Bedroom sound.** A bed partner's apnea is an external arousal source not addressed by anything else in this series. Snore → silence → sudden loud resumption is intermittent noise, and intermittent noise is far more arousing than steady noise at the same volume, because the brain cannot habituate to it. Test continuous white noise or a fan (it works by raising the floor, not by being louder); consider mattress motion isolation for the movement at the end of each event. The partner's apnea being properly treated is the largest lever — and that is for their health first. [[PERFECT SLEEP — 14 Sleep-Disordered Breathing]] and [[PERFECT SLEEP — 17 External Environment]].
- **The two measurements most often skipped.** Whether wakings come from polyuria (too much urine made) or capacity (too little room) — settled by a 2–3 night bladder diary, not by inference. And return-to-sleep latency after each waking, which matters more than the raw count. [[PERFECT SLEEP — 19 Measuring It]].

## How to use this series

Read 01, 02 and 03 first. They are the frame. Everything after is a specific factor acting on that frame.

Where evidence is thin, the note says so. Where a popular claim does not survive scrutiny, the note says that too.

## WHAT TO HOLD ONTO

- Sleep is not one problem expressing itself in four places — low energy, low testosterone, food craving, lost exercise. It is one problem, and it runs in reverse when the nights are restored.
- The counterintuitive detail is the point of these notes.
- Measure before adding interventions. The skipped measurements usually decide which tap to work on.
