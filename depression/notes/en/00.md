## Evidence labels

🟢 Strong (replicated, large studies or meta-analyses) · 🟡 Moderate (consistent but limited, or mostly observational) · 🟠 Preliminary (early, small or animal) · 🔴 Contested (experts disagree, or the popular claim outruns the data)

## The central idea

Depression is not one disease or one chemical shortage. Several systems — stress hormones, immunity, brain energy, plasticity, sleep and the clock, hormones, thinking patterns and the social world — can push the brain into a depressed state, and that state pushes back on them. **Arrows run both ways.** Different people have different weights, which is why no single treatment works for everyone.
