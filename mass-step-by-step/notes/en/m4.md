# The Mass, Return Home — 4 — Sent Out

## The final prayer

**What happens:** Everyone stands. The priest says one last prayer and everyone answers **"Amen."** There may be a few announcements about parish life.

**Why:** Listen to the announcements. That parish is your family now: the soup kitchen, the Bible study, the youth group, the funeral lunch. There's a place for you in it.

## The blessing

**What happens:** The priest raises his hand: "May almighty God bless you, the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit." Everyone makes the Sign of the Cross and answers: **"Amen."**

**Why:** The Mass ends the way it began: with the Sign of the Cross. You came in under that sign, and you go out under it, blessed and protected.

## "Go forth, the Mass is ended"

**What happens:** The priest or deacon says: "Go forth, the Mass is ended," or "Go and announce the Gospel of the Lord." Everyone answers: **"Thanks be to God."**

**Why:** The word *Mass* comes from these words in Latin: ***Ite, missa est.*** It means, roughly: *Go, you are sent.*

The Mass doesn't really end. It sends you. You came in carrying your week. You go out carrying Christ, into your kitchen, your office, your car, your family. Whoever you meet this week should meet a little bit of him in you.

> 🟢 The name "Mass" comes from the Latin dismissal *missa*, "sending" (Catechism 1332).

## Before you leave

**What happens:** The priest and servers walk out, usually with a final hymn. Many people stay a moment longer.

**Why:** Don't rush to the parking lot. Stay for the last verse. Kneel for a minute and say thank you. On your way out, genuflect toward the tabernacle again, and make the Sign of the Cross with holy water at the door. They are the same gestures you came in with. But you are not the same.

## Where to go from here

You don't have to do everything at once. Here are three small steps:

**1. Come back next Sunday.** Every Sunday is a gift, and the Church asks Catholics to be there for it. One Mass at a time.

> ✅ Church teaching: the Sunday obligation (Catechism 2180–2183).

**2. Go to Confession.** If it's been years, just say so: *"Father, it's been a long time. I'm not sure how to do this."* He will help you through it, step by step. You'll walk out lighter than you've felt in years.

**3. Pick one moment.** Next time, choose one part of this guide (the three crosses, the Creed, "Lord, I am not worthy") and pray it with your whole heart. Next week, choose another.

**Want to go deeper?** The full Mass series in this library (*Mass-0* to *Mass-16*) walks through every moment of the Mass at retreat depth: the history, the Scripture, the saints, and guided meditations for each step.
