Connect Claude to Your Apps

Claude knows the world. Connectors let it know your world: your email, calendar, notes and files.

In this lesson
  • What a connector is, and the three ways Claude reaches your stuff
  • How to turn a connector on and control what it can do
  • How to ask so Claude reads the right things and acts safely
The big idea

Without connectors, Claude only sees what you type or attach. With them, it can look things up in your own apps and, when you allow it, do things there: draft an email, add a calendar event, update a Notion page.

🔌 Connector

A secure link to an online app (Gmail, Calendar, Notion, Dropbox…). Works from phone, web and computer. Best for text: emails, events, pages.

📁 Folder

A folder on your computer, added in the desktop app. Any file type. Only when you send from that computer.

🌐 Browser

For websites with no connector. Claude clicks and fills forms in your browser or its own.

Rule of thumb: connector first, folder for files, browser as the last resort.

Reading vs acting. Reading (search my email) is low-risk. Acting (send, delete, book) changes the real world, so Claude shows you what it will do first. Read the draft before you approve.

Step by step
1Open Settings → Connectors in Claude.
2Pick an app (start with Calendar or Gmail) and choose Connect.
3Sign in to that app and approve what Claude may see or do.
4In a chat, use the + / tools menu to switch connectors on or off for that chat.
5Ask a small read-only question first, then build up to actions.

You can disconnect any app at any time from the same Settings page.

Recipes to copy and paste
Inbox triage (read only)
Search my Gmail from the last 3 days. List anything that needs a reply from me, oldest first, with one line each on what they want. Do not send anything.
Email → calendar
Find the email from Dr. Ruiz about my appointment. Create a calendar event with the date, time and address from that email. Show me the event before you save it.
Week ahead
Look at my calendar for next week. Tell me which days are overloaded, and suggest two blocks of 90 minutes for focused work.
Notes from files
In Dropbox, find the latest file with "invoice" in the name. Summarize it in five bullet points and save the summary as a new page in my Notion "Finance" page.
Mistakes to avoid

✕ "Check my email"

Too vague. Say which emails: who, which days, what you need from them.

✕ Approving without reading

An approved email is sent for real. Read every draft and every event before you say yes.

✕ Turning everything on

More connectors in a chat means more noise. Turn on only the ones that chat needs.

✕ Expecting files from a connector

Most connectors move text. For PDFs and photos, use a folder from the desktop app.

Practice (10 minutes)
  1. Connect your Calendar. Ask: "What is on my calendar tomorrow?"
  2. Ask Claude to create one test event 2 days from now. Read it before approving. Then ask it to delete it.
  3. Connect Gmail. Ask for a 5-line summary of your unread email from today, with nothing sent.
Check yourself
You want Claude to save a PDF into a folder. Connector, folder or browser?

A folder, from the desktop app. Connectors usually move text only.

Claude drafted a reply to your landlord. What do you do before approving?

Read it all: names, dates, amounts, tone. Once approved, it is sent for real.

Your bank has no connector. Can Claude still help?

Yes, through the browser, carefully. Never give Claude your passwords in the chat; sign in yourself when the browser asks.

REMEMBER
Connector first. Read before you approve. Small question first, then bigger tasks.
