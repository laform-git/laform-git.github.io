CLAUDE CLASS · CLAUDE-10 · ADVANCED

Make Claude Yours

Stop repeating yourself. Teach Claude once, in the right place, and let it teach you back.

In this lesson
  • The four places where Claude keeps "how you like things"
  • How to turn a job you just did into a reusable skill
  • How to use Claude as your personal teacher
The big idea

Every time you explain the same thing twice, you are doing Claude's homework. Put each kind of knowledge in its place:

PlaceWhat goes thereExample
🧠 MemoryFacts about you, used in every chat"I write in Mexican Spanish. I run a small bakery."
📁 Project instructionsRules for one area of your life or work"In Business: use our invoice format, prices in pesos."
🛠️ SkillA step-by-step recipe for a job you repeat"Weekly sales report: these sources, this layout, save here."
🧊 ArtifactA tool or page you keep and reuseA price calculator, a checklist, a tracker you fill in

Quick test: is it about me? Memory. About this area? Project. A process? Skill. A thing to use? Artifact.

Step by step

Turn a job into a skill

1Do the job once in a chat, and correct it until the result is exactly right.
2Say: "Turn what we just did into a skill called Weekly Report."
3Claude shows a card with the skill. Read the steps, then Save.
4Next time, just ask for the job ("make the weekly report") and Claude follows the saved steps.

Check and edit what Claude knows

1Ask: "What do you remember about me?" Correct anything wrong.
2Memory and skills can be reviewed and turned off in Settings.
Recipes to copy and paste
Make Claude your teacher
Teach me how [topic] works, step by step, as if I am new to it. After each step, ask me one question to check I understood before you continue.
Learn by doing
I want to learn to [task] myself. Don't do it for me. Give me the first step, wait for my answer, then tell me what I did well and what to fix.
Find your gaps
Here is how I currently use Claude: [describe]. What are the three features I am not using that would save me the most time? Explain each in two lines with one example.
Save a preference
Remember that I prefer short answers with a list of steps, and that I write in Mexican Spanish.
Mistakes to avoid

✕ Rules in every message

If you type the same rule twice, it belongs in Memory or the project instructions.

✕ A skill from a bad run

Only save a skill after the job came out right. A skill copies mistakes too.

✕ Never checking Memory

Old facts stay until you fix them. Ask "what do you remember about me?" now and then.

✕ Asking for answers only

If you want to learn, ask Claude to quiz you and to let you try first.

Practice (10 minutes)
  1. Ask "What do you remember about me?" Fix one thing that is wrong or missing.
  2. Pick one job you did with Claude this week. Turn it into a skill and save it.
  3. Use the "Make Claude your teacher" recipe on one Claude feature you don't understand yet.
Check yourself
You always want prices in pesos for your business chats. Where does that go?

In that project's instructions. It is a rule for one area, not a fact about you everywhere.

When is the right moment to save a skill?

After the job came out exactly right, so the skill copies a good result.

How do you make Claude teach instead of just answer?

Ask it to go step by step, to quiz you after each step, and to let you try first.

REMEMBER
Me → Memory. Area → Project. Process → Skill. Tool → Artifact. And let Claude quiz you.
