Artifacts: What Claude Makes, and Which One to Use

Claude does not only answer. It makes real things you keep: documents, tables, slides, flyers, charts, short videos and more.

In this lesson
  • The nine kinds of things Claude can make for you
  • How to pick the right one in five seconds
  • How to share it, and how to change it yourself
  • How artifacts are different from Notion
The big idea

An artifact is something Claude makes that you keep. It opens next to the chat, it gets its own page, and you can come back to it, change it and share it. A plain answer in the chat is read once. An artifact is a thing you use again.

The nine kinds
KindUse it forExample
📄 DocsWriting to keep: letters, plans, reports. Edit together; download as Word or PDF.Elle’s price list as a document
📊 SheetsTables with formulas: budgets, trackers, lists. Downloads as Excel.Orders per week
🎞️ SlidesPresentations to show on a screen or download.A family-trip slideshow
🎨 DesignPosters, flyers, social posts, web screens.A weekend-sale flyer
🧭 WhiteboardIdeas as boxes and arrows that you move around together.Planning a party
📈 Data (Dashboard)Charts and numbers that update.Bakery sales by month
🎬 MotionShort animated videos. Export to MP4.A 20-second product promo
🎨 Design SystemYour brand’s colors, fonts and logo, reused in everything else.Elle Bakehouse brand kit
🧩 Custom pageSmall tools and interactive pages.The Marian Antiphons page with A−/A+ and scroll

You do not have to name the kind. Ask for what you want, and Claude picks. Naming it ("make it a Sheet") just makes sure.

How to choose
1Want to read, change or share writing? → Docs
2Numbers to add up? → Sheets
3Something to show people? → Slides
4Something to look at or print? → Design

Ideas to arrange → Whiteboard. Numbers to watch over time → Data. A little film → Motion. A tool you click → Custom page.

Artifacts vs. Notion

Notion is a separate app. Claude can read and write your Notion pages through a connector, but those pages live in Notion. A Doc is Claude’s own document: you and Claude write it together, and it lives in Claude. If you ask for "a doc", you get a Doc artifact. If you want it in Notion, say "in Notion".

Private until you share

Every artifact starts private: only you can see it. When you want someone else to see it, open the artifact, use Share, and send the link. You decide who sees it, and you can stop sharing later.

Changing it

✋ Do it yourself

Docs, Sheets and Slides open in an editor. Tap a word, a cell or a title and type, just like in Word or Excel.

💬 Ask Claude

Say what to change in the chat: "make the title bigger", "add a column for tips". Claude updates the same artifact.

Recipes to copy and paste
A letter to keep
Write a short thank-you letter to our customers for this year. Make it a Doc so I can edit it.
A table that adds up
Make a Sheet of this week’s orders: date, name, item, price, and a total at the bottom.
Slides for a talk
Make 6 slides about our family trip to Oaxaca: one per day, one big photo idea each, few words.
A flyer
Design a flyer for a weekend sale at Elle Bakehouse: 20% off conchas, Saturday and Sunday, warm colors.
A brand kit once
Make a Design System for Elle Bakehouse with our colors, two fonts and the logo, so every flyer and page matches.
Mistakes to avoid

✕ Asking for a file you do not need

"A Word file" gives you a file. "A doc" gives you something you can edit and share, and still download as Word.

✕ Rebuilding instead of changing

To fix an artifact, ask Claude to change it. Do not start a new one each time.

✕ Sharing private things

Check what is inside before you send the link. Anyone with the link may see it.

✕ Mixing it up with Notion

An artifact lives in Claude. Notion pages live in Notion. Say which one you want.

Practice (10 minutes)
  1. Ask Claude: "Make a Sheet of my monthly bills with a total."
  2. Change one number yourself, right in the Sheet.
  3. Ask Claude: "Add a column for the due date." See it update.

Where this is going

Claude is moving from writing answers to making finished things: documents, decks, designs and videos you open, change and share, all from one conversation. The habit to build now: say what you are making and who it is for, keep it as an artifact, and change it instead of starting over.
