CLAUDE CLASS · CLAUDE-09

Claude Can Do More Than You Think

12 features most people never touch, in plain words.

The map: everything connects to Claude
← swipe to see the whole map →

Claude sits in the center. Every box is one extra power that plugs into it. You do not need all of them: pick the ones that fit your day. Below, the same boxes in three groups.

1 · In every chat2 · Works for you3 · For builders
1 · In every chat
📁

Projects

A folder for one topic. Every chat inside shares the same files and instructions, so you never explain twice.

Try: make a project called "Taxes" and drop in last year's papers.
🧊

Artifacts

Claude builds a real thing you can keep: a page, a chart, a small app or a document. It opens beside the chat and you can share the link.

Try: "Make me a weekly meal plan I can tick off."
🧠

Memory

Claude remembers useful facts about you from past chats: your job, your style, what you are working on. You can see it, edit it or turn it off in Settings.

Try: "Remember that I write in Mexican Spanish."
💭

Adaptive Thinking

Claude decides how hard to think. A quick question gets a quick answer; a hard problem gets more time to reason first.

Try: nothing to switch on. For hard problems, say "take your time and think this through."
🛠️

Skills

Saved recipes for jobs you repeat. Claude follows the same steps every time, like a checklist it never forgets.

Try: "Turn how we just made this report into a skill."
✏️

Design

Claude makes visuals you can look at and edit: posters, slides, app screens, flyers. You change them by talking.

Try: "Design a flyer for my bakery's weekend sale."
2 · Claude works for you
📅

Scheduled Tasks

Give one instruction and Claude runs it on its own, later or every day, even while you are away.

Try: "Every Monday at 8, send me this week's orders."
💻

Your computer

When your chat is linked to your computer through the Claude desktop app, Claude can open, sort, rename and edit files in folders you allow, and use apps on that computer.

Try: "Rename all the photos in this folder by date."
🌐

The web

To look things up, Claude searches and reads web pages right in the chat. No browser needed.

To do things on a website (click, fill in forms, sign in), Claude uses a browser, in one of two ways:

① Your browser: your own Chrome, with the Claude in Chrome extension and your own logins.
② Claude's browser: the browser built into the Claude desktop app, with its own logins.

Try: "Find three flights to Cancún under $300 and list them."
3 · For builders
⌨️

Claude Code

Claude writes and fixes software for you, working right inside the project's files.

Try: "Build me a simple website for my business."
📄

CLAUDE.md

A note you leave in a project folder that Claude reads first, every time: the rules, the names, how you like things done.

Like: a project's instructions, but for Claude Code.
⚡

Prompt Caching

For people who build apps on Claude: long instructions sent again and again are remembered for a while, so answers come faster and cost less.

Who needs it: developers building on the Claude API. You do not need to do anything in the app.
Where to start
1Make one Project for the thing you do most.
2Ask Claude for an Artifact you will keep.
3Hand one repeat job to a Scheduled Task.

Each one takes a few minutes to set up and saves time every day after.
