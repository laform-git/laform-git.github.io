# JW-04 A Bible Changed to Fit

> **Error 4 of 10.** When the Bible contradicted their doctrine, they changed the Bible.

## What they teach

The **New World Translation** (1950–1961) is the most accurate Bible in English. It "restores" God's name, Jehovah, to the New Testament, and Witnesses are taught to read and quote it alone.

**Where it came from.** The NWT was produced by a committee the Society refused to name, so no one could check whether its members knew Greek or Hebrew. Its changes follow one pattern: wherever the text points to Jesus being God, the translation bends.

## Why it is an error

- **The same word is translated two ways.** When the Greek word for worship is about Jehovah, the NWT says "worship." When it is about Jesus, it says "do obeisance" (Heb 1:6). Nothing changes but the doctrine.
- **Words are added that aren't there.** In Colossians 1:16–17 the NWT adds the word "other" four times, so that Jesus made "all *other* things." The word is not in the Greek.
- **"A god" appears only for Jesus.** In John 1 the word *God* appears several times without the article, and the NWT translates it "God" every time but once: in John 1:1, for the Word, it becomes "a god." That leaves two gods, a big one and a small one, which Isaiah 43:10 forbids.
- **God's name is inserted where no manuscript has it.** The NWT puts "Jehovah" into the New Testament more than 200 times, yet not one of the thousands of Greek New Testament manuscripts contains it. The Apostles wrote *Lord*, and applied Old Testament "LORD" texts directly to Jesus (Rom 10:9–13; Phil 2:10–11).

### The New World Translation
*"The Bible translation published by the Watch Tower Society for Jehovah's Witnesses, first issued between 1950 and 1961."*

Because the same Greek is rendered one way when it refers to God and another when it refers to Jesus, it is the main point of contention in any Catholic conversation with Witnesses. See [[JW-00 Cheat Sheet — Jehovah's Witnesses|Jehovah's Witnesses]].

### The Bible They Changed — Verse by Verse

#### JOHN 1:1 — "THE WORD WAS A GOD"
- **Claim:** The Greek has no article before "God," so it means "a god."
- **Response:** The same article-less theos appears in Jn 1:6, 1:12, 1:13 and 1:18, and the NWT translates it "God" every time. It becomes "a god" only for Jesus. And a second "god" beside Jehovah is exactly what Isaiah 43:10 forbids.
- **Ask:** "Is Jesus a true god or a false god? If true, you have two gods. If false, why does John call him one?"

#### COLOSSIANS 1:16–17 — "ALL OTHER THINGS"
- **Claim:** Jesus made all other things after being made himself.
- **Response:** "Other" is not in the Greek. The NWT adds it four times. The real text says he made all things and is before all things, which puts him outside creation.
- **Ask:** "Can you show me 'other' in the Greek interlinear?"

#### JOHN 8:58 — "I HAVE BEEN"
- **Claim:** Jesus only said he existed before Abraham.
- **Response:** The Greek is ego eimi, "I am," the words of God's name in the Greek Old Testament (Ex 3:14). The crowd picked up stones (Jn 8:59), the penalty for blasphemy. Nobody was stoned for claiming to be old.

#### HEBREWS 1:6 — "DO OBEISANCE"
- **Claim:** Angels bow to Jesus but do not worship him.
- **Response:** The Greek proskyneo is rendered "worship" by the NWT when the object is Jehovah. It changes only for Jesus. In Revelation 22:8–9 an angel refuses this very act and says "Worship God."

#### HEBREWS 1:8 — "GOD IS YOUR THRONE"
- **Claim:** God is the source of Jesus' throne.
- **Response:** The Father says this "to the Son," quoting Psalm 45:6. The natural reading, followed by nearly every translation, is "Your throne, O God." The Father calls the Son God.

#### TITUS 2:13 & 2 PETER 1:1 — "THE GREAT GOD AND OF OUR SAVIOR"
- **Claim:** God and Jesus are two persons in these verses.
- **Response:** One article governs both titles, which names one person. The NWT translates the identical construction in 2 Peter 1:11 and 2:20 ("our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ") as one person. Applied consistently, Jesus is "our God and Savior."

#### LUKE 23:43 — "TRULY I TELL YOU TODAY"
- **Claim:** The comma belongs after "today," so the thief enters Paradise later.
- **Response:** Jesus says "Truly I say to you" dozens of times and never once attaches "today" to it anywhere else. The comma is moved to protect soul sleep.

#### MATTHEW 25:46 — "EVERLASTING CUTTING-OFF"
- **Claim:** The wicked are annihilated, not punished.
- **Response:** Kolasis means punishment. "Eternal punishment" stands beside "eternal life" with the same word, aionios. If the life lasts forever, so does the punishment.

#### "JEHOVAH" INSERTED INTO THE NEW TESTAMENT
- **Claim:** The NWT restores God's name where copyists removed it.
- **Response:** The NWT inserts "Jehovah" into the New Testament more than 200 times, yet no surviving Greek New Testament manuscript contains the divine name. The apostles wrote Kyrios, "Lord," and applied Old Testament YHWH texts directly to Jesus (Rom 10:9–13 quoting Joel 2:32; Phil 2:10–11 quoting Is 45:23). "Jehovah" itself is a medieval blend of YHWH with the vowels of Adonai.
