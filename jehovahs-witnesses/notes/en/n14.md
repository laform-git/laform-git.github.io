# JW-14 The Strangest Claims

> Many Witnesses have never heard of these. They are worth knowing because they show how the organization's "truth" has shifted. (Failed end-time dates: see [[JW-10 Failed Prophecies]].)

## The Strangest Claims

### THE GREAT PYRAMID
- **Claim:** Russell used measurements of the Great Pyramid of Giza to confirm his prophetic dates.
- **Response:** Scripture forbids seeking God's will through such means (Dt 18:10–12), and Jesus says no one knows the day or hour (Mt 24:36). A pyramid monument still stands at Russell's burial site in Pittsburgh.

### BETH SARIM — A HOUSE FOR ABRAHAM
- **Claim:** Rutherford taught that Abraham, Isaac, Jacob and other patriarchs would return around 1925, and in 1929–30 the Society built a mansion in San Diego for them.
- **Response:** The patriarchs never came. Rutherford lived in the house until his death, and it was quietly sold in 1948. Jesus already told us where Abraham is: alive to God (Mt 22:32; Lk 16:22).

### "MIRACLE WHEAT"
- **Claim:** Around 1911 the Watch Tower promoted a seed wheat sold at a high price as miraculously productive.
- **Response:** Government tests found it ordinary. When a newspaper mocked it, Russell sued for libel and lost (1913). God's gifts are not sold as merchandise (Acts 8:20).

### ORGAN TRANSPLANTS AS CANNIBALISM
- **Claim:** The Watchtower (15 November 1967) called organ transplants cannibalism.
- **Response:** The ruling was reversed in 1980, leaving earlier members who refused transplants to bear the cost. God's commandments do not flip on a 13-year cycle. The Church teaches organ donation can be an act of charity (CCC 2296).

### VACCINATION
- **Claim:** The Society's Golden Age magazine (1920s–1931) called vaccination a violation of God's covenant.
- **Response:** The teaching was later dropped without acknowledgment. A body that claims to be God's only channel has repeatedly issued medical rulings it had to abandon.

### BIRTHDAYS ARE EVIL
- **Claim:** Birthdays are banned partly because the only two birthdays in Scripture (Pharaoh, Gen 40; Herod, Mt 14) include an execution.
- **Response:** An execution happened at the party; the Bible never condemns the birthday. By the same reasoning, meals would be banned because Judas betrayed Jesus at one.
