# JW-08 Two Classes of Christians

> **Error 8 of 10.** They tell millions of believers that heaven is closed to them and that the Lord's Supper isn't for them.

## What they teach

Only **144,000** "anointed" Christians go to heaven to rule with Christ. Everyone else, the "great crowd," will live forever on a paradise earth and is not born again. Once a year, at the **Memorial**, bread and wine are passed around the hall, and almost everyone passes them on without eating or drinking, because the meal is only for the anointed.

**Where it came from.** In 1935 Rutherford announced that the "great multitude" of Revelation 7 was an earthly class. The heavenly number was said to be nearly full, so new converts were told their hope was on earth.

## Why it is an error

- **It splits the one flock in two.** Jesus spoke of one flock and one shepherd. Scripture knows no second-class Christians.
- **It closes heaven to the people it claims to save.** Jesus says no one can see the kingdom of God unless he is born again. If the great crowd is not born again, then by Jesus' own words they won't see it.
- **It reads the numbers wrong.** The 144,000 are 12,000 from each of the twelve tribes of Israel (Rev 7:4–8). Read literally, every one would be a Jewish man (Rev 14:4). The number is a symbol of completeness, 12 × 12 × 1,000, not a ceiling.
- **It turns the Eucharist into a meal you're supposed to refuse.** Christ gave his Body and Blood so that we would eat and live.

## Response

### Scripture ✅

- *"After this I looked, and behold, a great multitude which no man could number … standing before the throne and before the Lamb"* (Rev 7:9). This comes right after the 144,000, and the multitude is in heaven, before the throne.
- *"Unless one is born anew, he cannot see the kingdom of God"* (Jn 3:3).
- *"There shall be one flock, one shepherd"* (Jn 10:16).
- *"There is neither Jew nor Greek … for you are all one in Christ Jesus"* (Gal 3:28).
- *"Unless you eat the flesh of the Son of man and drink his blood, you have no life in you"* (Jn 6:53).
- *"Do this in remembrance of me"* (Lk 22:19). The command is to *do this*, not to watch others do it.

### Catechism ✅

- CCC 1023–1029: heaven is the face-to-face vision of God, promised to all who die in his grace and friendship.
- CCC 1324: the Eucharist is the source and summit of the Christian life.
- CCC 1374: in the Eucharist Christ is truly, really and substantially present, Body, Blood, soul and divinity.
- CCC 1384: the Lord addresses an urgent invitation to us to receive him.

### Councils and Encyclicals ✅

- **Council of Trent, Session XIII (1551):** Christ is truly, really and substantially present in the Eucharist.
- **John Paul II,** ***Ecclesia de Eucharistia*** (2003): the Church draws her life from the Eucharist, which is offered to every faithful believer.

### Saints 🟢

- **St. Ignatius of Antioch** (c. 110) warned against those who stay away from the Eucharist because they don't confess it to be the flesh of our Savior (*To the Smyrnaeans* 7).
- **St. Justin Martyr** (c. 155) described Sunday worship for the pagans: all the baptized receive the food made Eucharist, which they believe is the flesh and blood of the incarnate Jesus (*First Apology* 66). Every believer received, not a handful.
- **St. Thomas Aquinas** wrote the hymns still sung at Corpus Christi, *Lauda Sion* and *Adoro te devote*, in praise of the Bread given for all.

## From the full reference

### Death and the Afterlife
**JW teaching:** Only 144,000 go to heaven; the rest live forever on a paradise earth.

**Catholic teaching:** 🟢 Heaven is the face-to-face vision of God (CCC 1023). The 144,000 is the symbolic fullness of the redeemed, not a cap (CCC 1138).

### Two Classes of Christians
- **Response:** Jesus offers the same promise to all who believe (Jn 3:16; Gal 3:28).

### The Memorial — Only the Anointed Partake
- **Response:** A rite built to be declined is the reverse of the Eucharist, the source and summit of Christian life (CCC 1324).
