# JW-09 The Church Fell Away

> **Error 9 of 10.** To make room for themselves, they have to say Christ lost his Church for 1,800 years.

## What they teach

Soon after the Apostles died, Christianity fell into apostasy. The Trinity, the immortal soul, hell, the cross and Christmas were pagan corruptions. True worship was lost until Russell's Bible students restored it in the 1870s.

**Where it came from.** Every new sect needs this story. If the Church never fell, there's no need for a restorer, so the founder must explain why God waited 18 centuries for him. Russell told the story; Rutherford made it official.

## Why it is an error

- **It makes Jesus a failure.** Christ promised that the powers of death would never prevail against his Church and that he would be with it always. A total apostasy means his promise failed.
- **It has no witnesses.** If the true faith survived somewhere, who kept it? No group held JW doctrine between the Apostles and the 1870s. It wasn't hidden; it didn't exist.
- **It condemns the martyrs.** The Christians who died in the arena confessed the faith the Watch Tower calls apostate.
- **The Church's sins don't cancel Christ's promise.** There have been sinners in the Church from the start, and Judas was one of the Twelve. Christ promised a Church that would endure, not one without sinners.

## Response

### Scripture ✅

- *"On this rock I will build my church, and the powers of death shall not prevail against it"* (Mt 16:18).
- *"Lo, I am with you always, to the close of the age"* (Mt 28:20). Always, not until the year 100 and then again from 1879.
- The Father will give *"another Counselor, to be with you for ever"* (Jn 14:16).
- *"To him be glory in the church and in Christ Jesus to all generations, for ever and ever"* (Eph 3:21). All generations.
- The Church is *"the pillar and bulwark of the truth"* (1 Tim 3:15). A pillar that collapsed for 1,800 years isn't a pillar.

### Catechism ✅

- CCC 811–812: the Church is one, holy, catholic and apostolic, marks that come from Christ and can be recognized through history.
- CCC 857–862: the Church is apostolic because she is built on the Apostles and is taught and governed by their successors until Christ returns.
- CCC 869: the Church is indefectible, held fast by Christ's promise.

### Councils ✅

- **Vatican II,** ***Lumen Gentium*** **§8:** the one Church of Christ, which we confess in the Creed as one, holy, catholic and apostolic, subsists in the Catholic Church governed by the successor of Peter and the bishops in communion with him.

### Doctors and Saints 🟢

- **St. Irenaeus** (c. 180) named the bishops of Rome one after another, from Peter to his own day, to show that the Apostles' teaching was handed on without a break (*Against Heresies* III.3).
- **St. Vincent of Lérins** (434) gave the test of true doctrine: hold what has been believed everywhere, always, and by all (*Commonitorium* 2). JW doctrine fails all three: not everywhere, not always, and not by anyone before 1870.
- **St. Augustine** answered the sect of his day, the Donatists, who said the Church had fallen everywhere except their corner of Africa: the whole world judges securely.
