> Where the movement came from, and the one hinge every error turns on.

## Origins — Who Jehovah's Witnesses Are
Founded in the early 1870s by Charles Taze Russell (1852–1916), a Pittsburgh businessman with no formal theological training. Russell rejected the Trinity, hell, and the divinity of Christ, and launched Zion's Watch Tower and Herald of Christ's Presence in 1879. After his death Joseph Franklin Rutherford took control of the Watch Tower Society and in 1931 renamed the movement Jehovah's Witnesses. Headquarters: Warwick, New York. About 8.7 million members worldwide (2019). Since 2012 the Governing Body in Warwick claims to be the sole channel through which God teaches his people.
