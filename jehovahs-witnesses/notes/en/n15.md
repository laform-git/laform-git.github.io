# JW-15 The Church's Official Response

> How the Church has judged the Witnesses: their baptism, their Christology, and the pastoral answer.

## The Church's Official Response
✅ **JW baptism is invalid.** The Archdiocese of San Francisco's canonical analysis (1964, followed universally since) found no Trinitarian faith behind the words: the divinity of Christ and the personhood of the Spirit are explicitly denied, so the God invoked is not the Triune God. Converts are baptized absolutely, not conditionally.

✅ **Nicaea (325) already condemned JW Christology.** Arianism taught that the Son is the first and greatest creature. Every Catholic who recites the Creed professes its refutation.

🟢 **Sects or New Religious Movements: A Pastoral Challenge** (May 1986), issued jointly by the Pontifical Council for Culture and three Vatican Secretariats from reports of 75 bishops' conferences, names Jehovah's Witnesses among movements of grave pastoral concern. It identifies weak catechesis and lukewarm community life as the openings they exploit, and prescribes renewed formation rather than mere polemics.

🟢 The bishops of Alta and Baja California, in a joint **Pastoral Exhortation Concerning Proselytism**, name Jehovah's Witnesses alongside Mormons and others as groups that reject the historical Christian churches.

🟢 **Dominus Iesus** (CDF, 2000, Cardinal Ratzinger, approved by John Paul II) §16–17 distinguishes real but wounded Christian communions from bodies whose denial of the Trinity and Christ's divinity places them outside Christian communion altogether. JW falls in the second group.

🟢 **John Paul II**, addressing the Mexican bishops (12 May 1990), named deficient catechesis, lukewarm witness, and weak community as what the sects exploit. His answer: the visible holiness of Catholic life.

🕯 **Reflect:** The Church names weak catechesis as the open door. How strong is my own formation?
