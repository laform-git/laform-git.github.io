# JW-13 Their Rules — Shunning, Holidays, Politics, Blood

> The rules that shape a Witness's daily life, and what Scripture actually says about each. (Governing Body: [[JW-03 The Organization in Place of the Church]]; Two Classes and the Memorial: [[JW-08 Two Classes of Christians]]; Armageddon: [[JW-05 Salvation Through the Organization]].)

## Their Own Teachings

### SHUNNING DISFELLOWSHIPPED MEMBERS
- **Claim:** Members who leave must be avoided, even by their own families.
- **Response:** Church discipline in Scripture aims at repentance and return (2 Cor 2:6–8; Gal 6:1). Jesus ate with sinners (Lk 15:2). Cutting off a parent or child to enforce loyalty to an organization is not Christian charity.

### NO BIRTHDAYS, CHRISTMAS, OR EASTER
- **Claim:** These are pagan in origin.
- **Response:** Christmas and Easter celebrate the Incarnation and the Resurrection, the heart of the Gospel. The angels themselves celebrated the birth of Christ (Lk 2:13–14). Origin arguments prove nothing: the days of the week carry pagan names too.

### NO VOTING, MILITARY, OR FLAG SALUTE
- **Claim:** Christians must stay out of all politics.
- **Response:** Christians owe honor to legitimate authority (Rom 13:1–7; 1 Pet 2:17) and share in the common good (CCC 2238–2240). Conscientious objection is respected, but total withdrawal is not a biblical command.

### NO BLOOD TRANSFUSIONS
- **Claim:** "Abstain from blood" (Acts 15:29) forbids transfusions.
- **Response:** The command concerns eating blood (Lev 17:10–14), given so Jewish and Gentile believers could share meals. A transfusion is not eating, and the law serves life (Mk 3:4). The Society introduced the ban in 1945 and now permits certain blood fractions, so the line is organizational, not biblical.
