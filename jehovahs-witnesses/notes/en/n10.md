# JW-10 Failed Prophecies

> **Error 10 of 10.** As a belief, this one does the least damage. As evidence, it does the most: by the Bible's own test, the organization has not spoken for God.

## What they teach

Jehovah's organization is God's prophet for the last days. Christ returned invisibly in **1914**, and **Armageddon** is very near. Members are urged to stay faithful because the end will come within their lifetime.

**Where it came from.** The organization has set or strongly pointed to dates again and again:

- **1874:** Russell taught that Christ's invisible presence began that year.
- **1914:** Russell expected the end of the present world. When it didn't come, the date was reinterpreted as Christ's invisible enthronement.
- **1925:** Rutherford's campaign *Millions Now Living Will Never Die* (1920) pointed to 1925 for the return of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. The Society even built a house in San Diego, Beth Sarim, for them.
- **1975:** Society publications from 1966 on pointed to 1975 as the end of 6,000 years of human history. Members sold homes and postponed careers. In 1980 the Watchtower admitted many had been disappointed.

## Why it is an error

- **The Bible gives a test, and they fail it.** A prophet whose word does not come true has not spoken for the Lord.
- **Jesus forbade date-setting.** No one knows the day or the hour, not even the angels.
- **Moving the goalposts is not new light.** When a prediction fails and is quietly redefined as "invisible," no prediction can ever be proved wrong. That is not prophecy.
- **Real people paid the price.** Families sold homes, skipped education and delayed medical care because the end was supposedly near.

## Response

### Scripture ✅

- *"When a prophet speaks in the name of the LORD, if the word does not come to pass or come true, that is a word which the LORD has not spoken"* (Dt 18:22).
- *"Of that day and hour no one knows, not even the angels of heaven, nor the Son, but the Father only"* (Mt 24:36).
- *"It is not for you to know times or seasons which the Father has fixed by his own authority"* (Acts 1:7).
- *"If any one says to you, 'Lo, here is the Christ!' or 'There he is!' do not believe it"* (Mt 24:23). And *"If they say to you, 'Lo, he is in the inner rooms,' do not believe it"* (Mt 24:26). An invisible, secret coming is exactly what Jesus warned against.
- His real coming will be like lightning from east to west (Mt 24:27), seen by every eye (Rev 1:7).

### Catechism ✅

- CCC 673: since the Ascension Christ's coming has been imminent, but its timing belongs to the Father alone.
- CCC 675–676: before Christ comes, the Church will face a final trial and deceptions, including false hopes of a kingdom realized within history.

### Doctors 🟢

- **St. Augustine**, *City of God* XVIII.53: those who try to calculate the years remaining to the world should relax their fingers and stop counting, because the Lord said it is not for us to know.
- **St. Thomas Aquinas**: the time of the end is hidden so that we stay watchful every day.

### Saints 🟢

- The **saints** prepared for the end by living each day as though it were their last, not by calculating the year. **St. Charles Borromeo**, asked what he would do if he knew the world was ending in an hour, is said to have answered that he would keep doing what he was doing.

## One line at the door

> *"Deuteronomy 18:22 says a prophet whose word doesn't come true has not spoken for God. The organization pointed to 1914, 1925 and 1975. How should we judge that?"*

Say it gently. Many Witnesses at the door have never been shown the old publications.
