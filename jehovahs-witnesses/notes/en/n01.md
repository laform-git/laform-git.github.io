# JW-01 They Deny That Jesus Is God

> **Error 1 of 10, the worst.** Get this one wrong and nothing else they say can save anyone.

## What they teach

Jehovah alone is God. Jesus is Michael the Archangel, the first thing God ever made. On earth he was a perfect man, and after he rose he became Michael again. He is great and honored, but he is a creature.

It sounds respectful toward Jesus, but it takes away everything that makes him our Savior.

**Where it came from.** This is not new. Around the year 318 a priest in Alexandria named **Arius** taught that "there was a time when the Son was not." It spread through the Empire in catchy songs sung in the markets. In 325 the bishops met at **Nicaea**, many still scarred from the persecutions they had just survived, and answered him with the words we pray every Sunday: *"true God from true God, begotten, not made."* In 1870s Pittsburgh, **Charles Taze Russell** revived the same heresy under a new name. The Church answered it 1,700 years ago, and the Witness at your door has never been told.

## Why it is an error

- **It changes who God is.** This is not a disagreement about a detail. They worship a God with no eternal Son.
- **It destroys salvation.** Sin against the infinite God needs a payment of infinite worth, and a creature's death cannot pay it. If Jesus is not God, the Cross saves no one.
- **It turns worship of Jesus into idolatry.** Scripture commands everyone, the angels included, to worship the Son. If he were a creature, that command would order idolatry.
- **It voids their baptism.** Their baptism invokes a God who is not the Trinity, so it is not Christian baptism.

## Response

### Scripture ✅

- *"In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God"* (Jn 1:1). John does not hedge.
- Thomas, who had refused to believe, touched the wounds and fell to his knees: *"My Lord and my God!"* (Jn 20:28). Jesus did not correct him. When John bowed before an angel, the angel stopped him at once: *"Worship God"* (Rev 22:9). Jesus didn't stop Thomas, because Jesus is not an angel.
- *"Before Abraham was, I am"* (Jn 8:58). These are the words God spoke to Moses from the burning bush (Ex 3:14). The crowd picked up stones. Nobody stones a man for saying he's old; they stoned him because he claimed to be God.
- *"To what angel did God ever say, 'Thou art my Son'?"* (Heb 1:5). To none. *"Let all God's angels worship him"* (Heb 1:6).
- The Father himself speaks to the Son: *"Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever"* (Heb 1:8). The Father does not flatter a creature.
- *"In him the whole fulness of deity dwells bodily"* (Col 2:9).

### Catechism ✅

- CCC 234: the Trinity is the central mystery of Christian faith and life.
- CCC 253: one God in three Persons, not three gods.
- CCC 464–465: Jesus is true God and true man. Nicaea condemned Arius and confessed the Son consubstantial with the Father.
- CCC 328–336: Michael is a created angel, a servant and messenger of God.

### Councils and Encyclicals ✅

- **Nicaea (325):** *"God from God, Light from Light, true God from true God, begotten, not made, consubstantial with the Father."*
- **Pius XII,** ***Sempiternus Rex*** (1951): Christ is one divine Person, true God and true man.
- **CDF,** ***Dominus Iesus*** §16–17 (2000): bodies that deny the Trinity and Christ's divinity stand outside Christian communion.

### Doctors 🟢

- **St. Athanasius** stood almost alone against the Arian tide. He was exiled five times and condemned by bishops who had given in, and people said it was *Athanasius against the world*. He never gave up one word, because he saw what was at stake: only God can save. *"For He was made man that we might be made God"* (*On the Incarnation* 54).
- **St. Thomas Aquinas** (*ST* III q.1 a.2): only a Savior who is God could make satisfaction of infinite worth for sin.
- **St. Augustine**, *On the Trinity*: Father, Son and Spirit are one God, equal in eternity and glory.

### Saints 🟢

- Around 110, **St. Ignatius of Antioch**, a disciple of St. John, was taken to Rome to be thrown to the lions. On the way he wrote to the Ephesians of *"Jesus Christ, our God,"* and called him *"God existing in flesh."* That was more than 200 years before Nicaea. The Church did not invent Christ's divinity in 325; she defended what her martyrs had already died for.

## One line at the door

> *"Thomas fell on his knees and called Jesus 'my Lord and my God.' An angel would have stopped him. Jesus didn't. Why not?"*

Then stop talking and let the question do its work.

🕯 **Reflect:** Athanasius gave up his home five times rather than give up one word about who Christ is. What have I given up for it?

## From the full reference

### God
**JW teaching:** Jehovah alone is God, one single person. The Trinity is a pagan invention of later councils.

**Catholic teaching:** ✅ One God in three Persons, one divine substance, coequal and coeternal (CCC 253–256). The New Testament baptizes in the single "name" of Father, Son, and Holy Spirit (Mt 28:19) and blesses in all three (2 Cor 13:13). Nicaea (325) did not invent this; it defended the apostolic faith against Arius, who taught exactly what JWs teach now.

🕯 **Reflect:** Do I know why the Church confesses three Persons and one God, or do I just recite it?

### Jesus Christ
**JW teaching:** Jesus is Michael the Archangel, God's first creation, through whom everything else was made. He is "a god," not God.

🕯 **Reflect:** If the Son were a creature, who died on the Cross, and could that death redeem the world?

### JESUS IS MICHAEL THE ARCHANGEL
- **Claim:** Jesus and Michael are the same being.
- **Response:** "To which of the angels did God ever say, 'You are my Son'?" (Heb 1:5, 13). None. Michael "did not dare" rebuke the devil on his own authority (Jude 9); Jesus rebukes him directly (Mt 4:10).
