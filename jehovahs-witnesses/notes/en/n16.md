# JW-16 At the Door — 22 Things You Will Hear

## Door Responses — What You Will Hear, What to Say
Twenty-two lines Witnesses commonly use at the door, with a short answer and one question to leave them with. Stay courteous: most Witnesses are sincere and sent out by people they trust. The goal is not to win the doorstep but to leave one question they cannot answer from the Watchtower.

### 1. "WE'RE SHARING A MESSAGE ABOUT GOD'S KINGDOM."
- **Claim:** God's Kingdom is a coming government that began ruling invisibly in 1914.
- **Response:** The Kingdom arrived in the person of Christ (Mk 1:15; Lk 17:21) and is already present in mystery in his Church (CCC 541, 567, 763). 1914 is a Watchtower date, not a biblical one.
- **Ask:** "Who is the King of that Kingdom, and is he God?"

### 2. "DID YOU KNOW GOD HAS A PERSONAL NAME, JEHOVAH?"
- **Claim:** True worshippers use the name Jehovah.
- **Response:** Catholics honor the divine name; it is revealed in Exodus 3:14 (CCC 203–213). Jesus taught us to call God "Our Father" (Mt 6:9), and the apostles applied YHWH texts to Jesus as "Lord" (Rom 10:9–13).
- **Ask:** "Why did the apostles, writing under inspiration, never once write 'Jehovah' in the New Testament?"

### 3. "DON'T YOU THINK WE'RE LIVING IN THE LAST DAYS?"
- **Claim:** World events show Armageddon is near.
- **Response:** Christians have lived in the "last days" since Pentecost (Acts 2:17; Heb 1:2). Jesus forbids date-setting (Mt 24:36; Acts 1:7). The Society has already set dates that failed.
- **Ask:** "If 1914, 1925 and 1975 were wrong, why should I trust the next date?"

### 4. "WOULD YOU LIKE A FREE BIBLE STUDY?"
- **Claim:** A structured study will show you what the Bible really teaches.
- **Response:** The "study" follows a Watchtower book, not the Bible itself. Offer instead to read a Gospel together, chapter by chapter, in a translation you both accept.
- **Ask:** "Would you read the Gospel of John with me, with no other publication open?"

### 5. "THE WORD 'TRINITY' ISN'T IN THE BIBLE."
- **Claim:** A doctrine without the word is not biblical.
- **Response:** "Theocracy" and "Governing Body" are not in the Bible either. The reality is there: one God (Dt 6:4), the Father is God (Jn 6:27), the Son is God (Jn 1:1; 20:28), the Spirit is God (Acts 5:3–4), baptism in one name of three (Mt 28:19). ✅ CCC 253–256
- **Ask:** "Is a teaching false because a word is missing, or only if the reality is missing?"

### 6. "JESUS NEVER SAID 'I AM GOD.'"
- **Claim:** Jesus never claimed divinity.
- **Response:** He claimed God's name (Jn 8:58), God's power to forgive sins (Mk 2:5–7), God's worship (Jn 5:23), and oneness with the Father (Jn 10:30). His enemies understood him and tried to stone him "because you, being a man, make yourself God" (Jn 10:33).
- **Ask:** "If he was not claiming to be God, why did they want to kill him for blasphemy?"

### 7. "JESUS PRAYED TO THE FATHER. CAN GOD PRAY TO HIMSELF?"
- **Claim:** Prayer proves Jesus is less than God.
- **Response:** The Son prays as man, in the nature he assumed (Phil 2:7). The Son praying to the Father is two distinct Persons, not God talking to himself. That is exactly what the Trinity teaches. 🟢 CCC 470, 2599–2606
- **Ask:** "Do you know that the Trinity teaches the Father and Son are distinct Persons, not the same one?"

### 8. "JESUS IS THE SON OF GOD, NOT GOD THE SON."
- **Claim:** "Son of God" means created by God.
- **Response:** A son shares his father's nature: the son of a man is a man. The Jews understood "Son of God" as "making himself equal with God" (Jn 5:18).
- **Ask:** "What nature does a son have, if not his father's?"

### 9. "THE HOLY SPIRIT IS GOD'S ACTIVE FORCE."
- **Claim:** The Spirit is impersonal energy.
- **Response:** A force cannot be grieved (Eph 4:30), cannot speak (Acts 13:2), cannot decide (Acts 15:28), cannot be lied to (Acts 5:3–4). ✅ CCC 687–688
- **Ask:** "Can you lie to electricity?"

### 10. "CHRISTMAS AND EASTER COME FROM PAGAN FESTIVALS."
- **Claim:** Celebrating them is idolatry.
- **Response:** The Church celebrates the Incarnation and the Resurrection. The angels celebrated the birth (Lk 2:13–14); the Resurrection is the center of the Gospel (1 Cor 15:14). A pagan date does not make the event pagan.
- **Ask:** "Is it wrong to rejoice that God's Son was born and rose from the dead?"

### 11. "THE CROSS IS A PAGAN SYMBOL. JESUS DIED ON A STAKE."
- **Claim:** Stauros means an upright pole only.
- **Response:** Thomas speaks of the print of the nails, plural, in his hands (Jn 20:25), fitting a crossbeam. The earliest Christian writers (Letter of Barnabas, Justin Martyr) describe a cross. Russell's own early Watchtower covers carried a cross and crown.
- **Ask:** "Why did the Watchtower itself use the cross until the 1930s?"

### 12. "THE DEAD ARE CONSCIOUS OF NOTHING" (ECCLESIASTES 9:5).
- **Claim:** There is no soul that survives death.
- **Response:** Ecclesiastes speaks "under the sun," of earthly life. Jesus shows Moses and Elijah alive at the Transfiguration (Mt 17:3), the souls under the altar crying out (Rev 6:9–10), and Paul wanting to depart and be with Christ (Phil 1:23). 🟢 CCC 366, 1022
- **Ask:** "If the dead are unconscious, how were Moses and Elijah talking with Jesus?"

### 13. "A LOVING GOD WOULD NEVER TORMENT PEOPLE IN HELL."
- **Claim:** Hell is a cruel invention.
- **Response:** Jesus speaks of hell more than anyone in Scripture (Mt 10:28; 25:46; Mk 9:43–48). Hell is the self-chosen, definitive rejection of God, respected by a God who will not force love (CCC 1033). ✅
- **Ask:** "Who taught more about hell than Jesus did?"

### 14. "ONLY 144,000 GO TO HEAVEN."
- **Claim:** The rest of the faithful live on earth.
- **Response:** Right after the 144,000, John sees "a great multitude which no man could number" standing before the throne in heaven (Rev 7:9). The 144,000 are drawn from the twelve tribes of Israel (Rev 7:4–8), and are they all Jewish men, as Revelation 14:4 would also require?
- **Ask:** "Where is the great multitude standing in Revelation 7:9?"

### 15. "CATHOLICS WORSHIP MARY AND STATUES."
- **Claim:** Catholic devotion is idolatry.
- **Response:** Worship (latria) is for God alone. Mary and the saints receive honor, as God commanded images for the Ark and Temple (Ex 25:18; 1 Kgs 6:23). ✅ CCC 971, 2131–2132
- **Ask:** "Was God commanding idolatry when he ordered the cherubim made for the Ark?"

### 16. "YOUR CHURCH HAS A HISTORY OF CORRUPTION AND VIOLENCE."
- **Claim:** A sinful history proves apostasy.
- **Response:** Sinners in the Church do not cancel Christ's promise (Mt 16:18); Judas was an apostle. The Church confesses her members' sins (Tertio Millennio Adveniente §33). The Society has its own record of failed prophecy and reversed rulings.
- **Ask:** "Did Christ promise a Church without sinners, or a Church the gates of hell would not overcome?"

### 17. "ONLY THOSE IN JEHOVAH'S ORGANIZATION WILL SURVIVE ARMAGEDDON."
- **Claim:** Salvation requires membership in the Watch Tower.
- **Response:** Christ is the one mediator (1 Tim 2:5) and the one judge (Jn 5:22). An organization founded in the 1870s cannot be the ark of salvation for the eighteen centuries before it.
- **Ask:** "Where was Jehovah's organization in the year 1000?"

### 18. "WE'RE THE ONLY ONES WHO PREACH DOOR TO DOOR, LIKE THE APOSTLES."
- **Claim:** House-to-house preaching marks the true Church.
- **Response:** Acts 20:20 describes Paul teaching in homes of believers, not a sales route. The Church has sent missionaries to every nation for twenty centuries. Zeal is not proof of truth (Rom 10:2).
- **Ask:** "Does zeal prove a message is true, or only that the messenger believes it?"

### 19. "READ THIS WATCHTOWER ARTICLE; IT EXPLAINS THE VERSE."
- **Claim:** Society literature gives the correct interpretation.
- **Response:** Politely decline the literature and stay with the text. The question is what the Bible says, not what a magazine says about it.
- **Ask:** "Can we answer this from the Bible alone, the way you say you want to?"

### 20. "WHY DO YOU CALL PRIESTS 'FATHER'? JESUS FORBADE IT" (MATTHEW 23:9).
- **Claim:** Calling anyone "Father" is disobedience.
- **Response:** Jesus rebukes the Pharisees' pride of title, not the word. Paul calls himself a father to his converts (1 Cor 4:15); Stephen calls the Sanhedrin "fathers" (Acts 7:2). Witnesses call their own fathers "father."
- **Ask:** "Did Paul disobey Jesus when he said 'I became your father in Christ'?"

### 21. "TRUE CHRISTIANS ARE NO PART OF THE WORLD."
- **Claim:** Voting, military service and national loyalty are forbidden.
- **Response:** Christians are in the world but not of it (Jn 17:15–16). Paul honors authority (Rom 13:1–7) and used his Roman citizenship (Acts 22:25). ✅ CCC 2238–2240
- **Ask:** "Was Paul compromising with the world when he appealed to Caesar?"

### 22. "MAY WE COME BACK NEXT WEEK?"
- **Claim:** Return visits build a relationship toward a study.
- **Response:** Yes, on your terms: one passage, the Bible only, and one agreed question, such as John 1:1 or Colossians 1:16 in the Greek interlinear. Pray for them before they return. Their conversion is God's work; your part is truth with charity (1 Pet 3:15–16).
- **Ask:** "Next week, will you bring the Greek of John 1:1 and show me where it says 'a god'?"

🕯 **Reflect:** The Witness at my door has rehearsed his answers. Have I rehearsed mine, and have I prayed for him by name?
