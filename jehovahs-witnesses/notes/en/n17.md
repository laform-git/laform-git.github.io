# JW-17 Quick Reference — JW vs. Catholic

> The seven core differences at a glance.

## Quick Reference — JW vs. Catholic

### GOD
- **Claim:** Jehovah alone is God; the Trinity is pagan.
- **Response:** One God in three Persons, the apostolic faith defended at Nicaea. ✅ CCC 253–256; Mt 28:19

### JESUS CHRIST
- **Claim:** Jesus is Michael the Archangel, God's first creation.
- **Response:** Eternally begotten, not made, consubstantial with the Father; angels worship him. ✅ Nicene Creed; Jn 1:1; 8:58; Heb 1:6

### HOLY SPIRIT
- **Claim:** An impersonal force.
- **Response:** He teaches, grieves, intercedes, and can be lied to, as only a Person can. ✅ CCC 687; Acts 5:3–4

### THE BIBLE
- **Claim:** The NWT is the accurate translation.
- **Response:** It translates the same Greek differently whenever it would show Jesus is God. 🟢 Jn 1:1; Col 1:16; Heb 1:6

### SALVATION
- **Claim:** A perfect man's ransom, plus loyalty to the organization.
- **Response:** The eternal Son's sacrifice, received by grace through faith working in love. 🟢 CCC 615, 1987

### AFTERLIFE
- **Claim:** Soul sleep, no hell, 144,000 in heaven.
- **Response:** Immediate judgment; heaven, purgatory, and hell are real; 144,000 is symbolic. 🟢 CCC 1020–1037

### THE CHURCH
- **Claim:** Christianity apostatized; the Watch Tower restored it.
- **Response:** The gates of hell never prevailed; the Watch Tower's own dates failed. ✅ Mt 16:18; Dt 18:22
