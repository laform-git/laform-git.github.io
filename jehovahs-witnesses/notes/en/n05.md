# JW-05 Salvation Through the Organization

> **Error 5 of 10.** They put an organization where Christ alone belongs: between the soul and God.

## What they teach

Soon God will destroy the wicked at **Armageddon**. Only those inside "Jehovah's organization" will survive. To stay inside, a Witness must accept what the Governing Body teaches, preach door to door, attend meetings and obey the rules. Anyone who leaves or is expelled is **shunned**, even by his own family.

**Where it came from.** Rutherford taught in the 1920s and 1930s that the Society was God's visible organization, a kind of ark. Shunning grew into a formal rule in the 1950s and was tightened in 1981.

## Why it is an error

- **It replaces the one Mediator.** Scripture names one mediator between God and men, Christ Jesus. An organization founded in 1879 can't stand in his place.
- **It turns grace into a workload.** Salvation is a gift received by faith working through love, not a record of hours and placements.
- **It can't explain 18 centuries.** If salvation requires the organization, everyone who lived before 1879 was lost, including the martyrs.
- **Shunning breaks what Christ came to heal.** Discipline in Scripture aims at repentance and return. Cutting off a parent or a child to enforce loyalty to an organization is not Christian charity.

## Response

### Scripture ✅

- *"There is one God, and there is one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus"* (1 Tim 2:5).
- *"There is salvation in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven … by which we must be saved"* (Acts 4:12). The name is Jesus, not Watch Tower.
- *"By grace you have been saved through faith; and this is not your own doing, it is the gift of God"* (Eph 2:8).
- *"I am the way, and the truth, and the life"* (Jn 14:6).
- On discipline: *"So you should rather turn to forgive and comfort him"* (2 Cor 2:7). The father in the parable runs to the son who left (Lk 15:20).

### Catechism ✅

- CCC 846: all salvation comes from Christ the Head through his Body, the Church he founded. That is the Church built on the Apostles, not one built in the 1870s.
- CCC 1987–1996: justification is the work of God's grace, given freely in Christ.
- CCC 2010: no one can merit the first grace. It is pure gift.

### Councils and Encyclicals ✅

- **Council of Trent, Session VI (1547),** ***Decree on Justification:*** we are justified freely by God's grace, and nothing before justification, whether faith or works, merits the grace of justification itself.
- **Pius XII,** ***Mystici Corporis*** (1943): the Church is the Mystical Body of Christ. Christ is its Head and the source of every grace, not a governing board.

### Doctors 🟢

- **St. Augustine** fought the Pelagians, who thought we could earn heaven by effort. His answer still stands: when God crowns our merits, he crowns his own gifts.
- **St. Thomas Aquinas** (*ST* I-II q.112 a.1): grace comes from God alone, because only God can make us share in his own nature.

### Saints 🟢

- **St. Thèrèse of Lisieux**, Doctor of the Church, offered herself to God with empty hands, trusting in his merciful love and not in her own works. A Witness counts hours. Thèrèse counted on mercy.

## From the full reference

### The Cross and Salvation
**JW teaching:** Jesus died on a single stake. His death was the ransom of a perfect human life. Salvation depends on remaining in the organization.

**Catholic teaching:** 🟢 Christ's death has infinite redemptive value because it is the obedience of the eternal Son made flesh (CCC 615). Salvation is by grace through faith working in love, given in the sacraments Christ instituted (CCC 1987–2029), not by loyalty to a publishing society.

🕯 **Reflect:** Does the weight of my sin need a creature to pay for it, or the Creator?

### Armageddon
- **Claim:** Soon God will destroy everyone outside the organization.
- **Response:** No organization holds the key to judgment; Christ does (Jn 5:22). The Church hopes for the salvation of all and never limits God's mercy to a membership list (1 Tim 2:4; CCC 847).
