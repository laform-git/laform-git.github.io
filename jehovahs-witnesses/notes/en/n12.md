# JW-12 Verses They Misread

> Seven verses Witnesses use to make Jesus a creature, and what each one actually says. (Jesus as Michael the Archangel: see [[JW-01 They Deny That Jesus Is God]].)

## Verses They Misread

### COLOSSIANS 1:15 — "FIRSTBORN OF ALL CREATION"
- **Claim:** Jesus was the first thing God created.
- **Response:** Prototokos means rank and inheritance. David, the youngest son, is called firstborn (Ps 89:27); so is Ephraim, the younger brother (Jer 31:9). Greek has a word for "first created," protoktistos, and Paul does not use it.

### REVELATION 3:14 — "THE BEGINNING OF GOD'S CREATION"
- **Claim:** Jesus was God's first creation.
- **Response:** Arche means source or ruler as well as beginning. God is called the arche in Revelation 21:6, and Jesus takes the same title in Revelation 22:13. God was not created.

### PROVERBS 8:22 — WISDOM "CREATED"
- **Claim:** Jesus is Wisdom, and Wisdom was created.
- **Response:** Proverbs 8 is poetry personifying wisdom as a woman. If wisdom were created, God once had none. Christ is "the wisdom of God" (1 Cor 1:24), eternal because God is never without it.

### JOHN 14:28 — "THE FATHER IS GREATER THAN I"
- **Claim:** Jesus admits he is less than God.
- **Response:** Greater in position, not nature. The Son took "the form of a servant" (Phil 2:6–7) and speaks from within it. No angel ever needs to say "God is greater than I"; only an equal does.

### MARK 13:32 — THE SON DOES NOT KNOW THE HOUR
- **Claim:** God knows everything, so Jesus is not God.
- **Response:** Jesus speaks by his human knowledge, which grew (Lk 2:52). What he said he did not know, he was not sent to reveal (CCC 472–474). Peter says, "Lord, you know all things" (Jn 21:17), and Jesus does not correct him.

### JOHN 17:3 — "THE ONLY TRUE GOD"
- **Claim:** Only the Father is true God.
- **Response:** Jude 4 calls Jesus "our only Master and Lord"; by the same logic the Father would not be Lord. 1 John 5:20 says of Jesus, "He is the true God and eternal life."

### 1 CORINTHIANS 8:6 — "ONE GOD THE FATHER, ONE LORD JESUS CHRIST"
- **Claim:** The Father is God; Jesus is only Lord.
- **Response:** Paul splits the Shema, "The LORD our God, the LORD is one" (Dt 6:4), and puts Jesus inside it. Thomas calls Jesus both "my Lord and my God" (Jn 20:28), and Jesus accepts it.
