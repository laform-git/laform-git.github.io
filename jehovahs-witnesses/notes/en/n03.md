# JW-03 The Organization in Place of the Church

> **Error 3 of 10.** This is the error that holds all the others in place. A Witness cannot question any teaching, because the organization tells him what the Bible means.

## What they teach

God has one channel on earth: the **Governing Body**, a small group of men at headquarters in Warwick, New York. Since 2012 they have said that they alone are "the faithful and discreet slave" of Matthew 24:45. Their interpretation of the Bible is to be accepted as God's direction. When they change a teaching, it is called "new light."

**Where it came from.** Russell started the Watch Tower in 1879. After his death, Rutherford took control and centralized all authority in the Society. The idea that the Society speaks for God grew step by step until 2012, when the Governing Body named itself the one faithful slave.

## Why it is an error

- **Its authority has no source.** Christ gave authority to the Apostles, and they handed it to the men they ordained. The Governing Body has no line back to anyone Christ sent. It started with a businessman in the 1870s.
- **It sits above the Word instead of serving it.** It changes its own teaching, then calls the change light from God. God's truth doesn't reverse itself (Mal 3:6; Heb 13:8).
- **"Bible alone" became "organization alone."** Reject the Church, and somebody still has to say what the Bible means. For a Witness, that somebody is a board in Warwick.
- **Its rulings can cost lives.** The 1945 ban on blood transfusions is enforced by the organization, not by Scripture. The command in Acts 15:29 is about eating blood, and the Society now allows some blood fractions. The line is drawn by men, and members have died keeping it.

## Response

### Scripture ✅

- *"You are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church, and the powers of death shall not prevail against it"* (Mt 16:18). Christ built one Church on the Apostles, not a publishing society.
- *"He who hears you hears me"* (Lk 10:16). Christ speaks through those he sends.
- *"As the Father has sent me, even so I send you"* (Jn 20:21).
- The Apostles chose Matthias to take Judas' office (Acts 1:20–26). The office continued after the man.
- *"What you have heard from me … entrust to faithful men who will be able to teach others also"* (2 Tim 2:2). That is four generations in one verse: Paul, Timothy, faithful men, others.
- The Church is *"the pillar and bulwark of the truth"* (1 Tim 3:15).

### Catechism ✅

- CCC 77: the Apostles left bishops as their successors, handing on their own teaching authority.
- CCC 85: the task of authentically interpreting the Word of God belongs to the living teaching office of the Church.
- CCC 861–862: the bishops are the successors of the Apostles by divine institution.

### Councils and Encyclicals ✅

- **Vatican II,** ***Dei Verbum*** **§7:** the Apostles left bishops as their successors to keep the Gospel whole and alive.
- ***Dei Verbum*** **§10:** the Church's teaching office is not above the Word of God but serves it, teaching only what has been handed on. That is the exact opposite of "new light."
- **Vatican II,** ***Lumen Gentium*** **§20:** the bishops succeed the Apostles by divine institution as shepherds of the Church.

### Doctors and Saints 🟢

- **St. Clement of Rome** (c. 96), writing while St. John was still alive, explained that the Apostles appointed bishops and arranged that other approved men should succeed them (*1 Clement* 42–44).
- **St. Irenaeus** (c. 180), Doctor of the Church, answered the sects of his day by listing the bishops of Rome one by one from Peter. His point was simple: the true teaching is found where the line from the Apostles is unbroken (*Against Heresies* III.3).
- **St. Ignatius of Antioch** (c. 110) told the churches to follow their bishop as Jesus Christ followed the Father.

## One line at the door

> *"Our bishops trace back to the Apostles, one by one. Who gave the Governing Body its authority, and who gave it to them?"*

🕯 **Reflect:** The Church does not invent her teaching; she guards what she received. Do I trust her because she is old, or because Christ promised to stay with her?
