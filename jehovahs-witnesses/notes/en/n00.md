# JW-00 Cheat Sheet — Jehovah's Witnesses

> The 10 errors of Jehovah's Witnesses, worst first. For each one: what they believe, the truth, your response, the verse to open, and the question to leave with them. Read it before you answer the door.

Evidence: ✅ Dogma · 🟢 Common doctrine · 🟡 Theological opinion · 🔴 Devotional

---

## 1. They deny that Jesus is God

- **They believe:** Jesus is Michael the Archangel, God's first creation.
- **The truth:** Jesus is God the Son, equal to the Father. ✅
- **Response:** "Thomas called Jesus 'my Lord and my God,' and Jesus accepted it."
- **Verse:** John 20:28
- **Ask:** "If Jesus is an angel, why didn't he correct Thomas?"

## 2. They deny that the Holy Spirit is God

- **They believe:** The Holy Spirit is God's active force, like electricity.
- **The truth:** The Spirit is the third divine Person. ✅
- **Response:** "Peter says lying to the Spirit is lying to God."
- **Verse:** Acts 5:3–4
- **Ask:** "Can you lie to a force?"

## 3. They put the organization in place of the Church

- **They believe:** The Governing Body is God's only channel on earth.
- **The truth:** Christ gave authority to the Apostles and their successors, the bishops. ✅
- **Response:** "Our bishops trace back to the Apostles. Your Governing Body traces back to 1870s Pittsburgh."
- **Verse:** Matthew 16:18
- **Ask:** "Who gave the Governing Body its authority?"

## 4. They changed the Bible to fit

- **They believe:** The New World Translation is the most accurate Bible.
- **The truth:** It translates the same Greek one way for Jehovah and another way for Jesus. 🟢
- **Response:** "Your Bible says 'worship' for Jehovah and 'obeisance' for Jesus. It's the same Greek word."
- **Verse:** Hebrews 1:6
- **Ask:** "Why translate one word two ways?"

## 5. They make salvation depend on the organization

- **They believe:** Only those loyal to Jehovah's organization will survive Armageddon.
- **The truth:** Salvation comes through Christ, the one mediator, by grace. ✅
- **Response:** "The Bible names one mediator, and it isn't an organization."
- **Verse:** 1 Timothy 2:5
- **Ask:** "Where was Jehovah's organization in the year 1000?"

## 6. They deny the immortal soul

- **They believe:** At death a person simply stops existing.
- **The truth:** The soul is immortal and faces judgment at death. ✅
- **Response:** "Jesus said men can kill the body but cannot kill the soul."
- **Verse:** Matthew 10:28
- **Ask:** "If the soul dies with the body, what can't men kill?"

## 7. They deny hell

- **They believe:** The wicked are wiped out, not punished.
- **The truth:** Hell is real and eternal. ✅
- **Response:** "Jesus warned about hell more than anyone else in the Bible."
- **Verse:** Matthew 25:46
- **Ask:** "If the life is eternal, why isn't the punishment?"

## 8. They split Christians into two classes

- **They believe:** Only 144,000 go to heaven or may take the bread and wine. Everyone else lives on earth.
- **The truth:** Every believer is called to be born again, to the Eucharist, and to heaven. 🟢
- **Response:** "Right after the 144,000, John sees a crowd no one could count, standing in heaven."
- **Verse:** Revelation 7:9; John 6:53
- **Ask:** "Jesus said that without his flesh and blood you have no life. Why do you refuse them?"

## 9. They say the Church fell away

- **They believe:** True Christianity disappeared after the Apostles until Russell restored it.
- **The truth:** Christ promised his Church would never fail. ✅
- **Response:** "Jesus said the gates of hell would never prevail. Was he wrong for 1,800 years?"
- **Verse:** Matthew 16:18; 28:20
- **Ask:** "Who kept the faith between the Apostles and 1879?"

## 10. Their prophecies have failed

- **They believe:** Their organization is God's prophet for the last days.
- **The truth:** They pointed to 1914, 1925 and 1975, and the promised end never came. ✅
- **Response:** "The Bible gives a test for a true prophet."
- **Verse:** Deuteronomy 18:22
- **Ask:** "What does that verse say about a prophet whose word fails?"

---

## At every door

Stay friendly. Use one verse. Ask one question. Take no literature: "Let's use only the Bible." When they leave, pray for them by name.
