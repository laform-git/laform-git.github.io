# JW-07 No Hell

> **Error 7 of 10.** If there is no hell, nothing you choose ultimately matters.

## What they teach

There is no hell of punishment. A loving God would never torment anyone forever. The wicked are simply destroyed and cease to exist. "Hell" in the Bible just means the grave.

**Where it came from.** Russell rejected hell as a young man. He later said the doctrine had nearly driven him from faith altogether. Rather than accept what Jesus taught, he built a system without it.

## Why it is an error

- **Jesus taught it more than anyone.** The strongest warnings about hell in the whole Bible come from the lips of Christ himself.
- **"Eternal" can't mean two things in one sentence.** Jesus puts "eternal punishment" right beside "eternal life," using the same word. If the life lasts forever, so does the punishment.
- **It misunderstands love.** God does not force anyone to love him. Hell is the final, freely chosen rejection of God, and God respects that choice.
- **It takes away the weight of our choices.** If the worst that can happen is to stop existing, then sin has no final stake.

## Response

### Scripture ✅

- *"They will go away into eternal punishment, but the righteous into eternal life"* (Mt 25:46).
- *"Depart from me, you cursed, into the eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels"* (Mt 25:41).
- *"It is better for you to enter life maimed than with two hands to go to hell, to the unquenchable fire"* (Mk 9:43). The fire is unquenchable.
- *"The smoke of their torment goes up for ever and ever; and they have no rest, day or night"* (Rev 14:11). Annihilated people do not lack rest.
- *"They shall suffer the punishment of eternal destruction and exclusion from the presence of the Lord"* (2 Thess 1:9).

### Catechism ✅

- CCC 1033: hell is the state of definitive self-exclusion from communion with God, chosen by dying in mortal sin without repentance.
- CCC 1035: the Church affirms the existence and eternity of hell. Its chief punishment is eternal separation from God.
- CCC 1037: God predestines no one to hell. It takes a willful turning away from God.

### Councils and Popes ✅

- **Fourth Lateran Council (1215):** the wicked will receive perpetual punishment with the devil; the good, everlasting glory with Christ.
- **Benedict XII,** ***Benedictus Deus*** (1336): the souls of those who die in actual mortal sin go down into hell immediately after death.

### Doctors 🟢

- **St. Augustine**, *City of God* XXI: since Christ used the same word for eternal punishment and eternal life, to say one ends is to say the other ends too.
- **St. Thomas Aquinas**: God's justice and mercy are not opposed. Mercy is offered to all, but it can be refused.

### Saints 🔴

- In 1917 the three children of **Fátima** were shown a vision of hell, and afterward little **St. Jacinta** offered every sacrifice she could for sinners. Private revelation adds nothing to the faith, but her response shows what hell meant to a child who believed it.

## One line at the door

> *"In Matthew 25:46 Jesus says 'eternal punishment' and 'eternal life' in the same sentence. If the life lasts forever, why doesn't the punishment?"*

🕯 **Reflect:** Hell is real because freedom is real. Do I take my choices as seriously as God does?
