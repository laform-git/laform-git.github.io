# JW-06 No Immortal Soul

> **Error 6 of 10.** They teach that when you die, you are gone. Nothing of you remains until God decides to recreate you.

## What they teach

Man does not *have* a soul; he *is* a soul, a living body. At death he simply stops existing, like a candle blown out. *"The dead know nothing"* (Eccl 9:5). At the resurrection, God recreates the person from his memory.

**Where it came from.** Russell took this from the Adventist preacher **George Storrs**, who taught "conditional immortality": the soul is not immortal by nature. It is an old idea the Church had already rejected.

## Why it is an error

- **It misreads Ecclesiastes.** That book describes life "under the sun," what a man sees from earth without revelation. It is not a teaching on what happens to the soul.
- **A recreated copy is not you.** If you stop existing and God makes a new person with your memories, that new person is a copy. The one who died is still gone. Their teaching can't even keep the resurrection.
- **Jesus taught the opposite.** He said men can kill the body but not the soul. That sentence is impossible if the soul dies with the body.

## Response

### Scripture ✅

- *"Do not fear those who kill the body but cannot kill the soul"* (Mt 10:28). This is the key verse. Men can end the body, but not the soul.
- *"Today you will be with me in Paradise"* (Lk 23:43). The thief was promised life that very day.
- *"My desire is to depart and be with Christ, for that is far better"* (Phil 1:23). If death meant nothing, it would not be "far better."
- *"We would rather be away from the body and at home with the Lord"* (2 Cor 5:8).
- Moses and Elijah, long dead, spoke with Jesus at the Transfiguration (Mt 17:3).
- The souls of the martyrs cry out under the altar, *"O Sovereign Lord, holy and true, how long?"* (Rev 6:9–10).
- *"Lord Jesus, receive my spirit"* (Acts 7:59). Stephen didn't expect to go out like a candle.

### Catechism ✅

- CCC 362–365: man is a unity of body and soul. The soul is the spiritual principle in him.
- CCC 366: every soul is created immediately by God, is immortal, and does not perish when it separates from the body at death.
- CCC 1021–1022: at death each soul receives its eternal reward at the particular judgment.

### Councils and Popes ✅

- **Fifth Lateran Council (1513):** solemnly condemned the claim that the human soul is mortal.
- **Benedict XII,** ***Benedictus Deus*** (1336): the souls of the just, once purified, see God face to face right after death, before the resurrection of the body.

### Doctors 🟢

- **St. Thomas Aquinas** (*ST* I q.75 a.6): the human soul is spiritual and does not depend on matter for its existence, so it cannot be destroyed when the body dies.
- **St. Augustine**, *Confessions*: "You have made us for yourself, and our heart is restless until it rests in you." A soul made for God is not made to go out like a lamp.

### Saints 🟢

- The **Church has always prayed for the dead** and asked the saints to pray for us, as the inscriptions in the Roman catacombs show. You don't pray for people who no longer exist.

## One line at the door

> *"Jesus said men can kill the body but cannot kill the soul. If the soul dies with the body, what is it that men can't kill?"*

🕯 **Reflect:** My soul will stand before God the moment I die. Am I living as if that's true?
