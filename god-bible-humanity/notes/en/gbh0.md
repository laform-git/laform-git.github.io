## What the course is

A five-hour course in which Bishop Barron reads the Bible through the Catholic tradition. He asks three linked questions: who is God, what is the biblical story, and how are we rightly related to God. He draws from both Testaments and from Aquinas, Augustine, Irenaeus and the mystics. The course ends with the Sermon on the Mount and the transforming power of the Cross and Resurrection.

## Three rules Barron sets in lecture 1

1. **The Bible is a library, not one book.** Read each genre by its own rules: poetry, ancient history, biography, letters, apocalyptic.
2. **Not everything in the Bible is what the Bible teaches.** Follow the great themes, patterns and trajectory, not isolated verses.
3. **Read within the tradition.** Scripture is understood inside the Church, as Shakespeare is understood inside a tradition of commentators and actors.
