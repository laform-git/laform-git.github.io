## The Insight

You asked for a patched roof. The architect is adding a wing. Lewis's warning: once surrender genuinely begins, God will not stop at making you merely good enough — the target was never "decent by comparison to others." It was perfection, full stop, which means the process necessarily goes further and feels more invasive than anyone bargains for at the outset, like a house renovation that turns out to be knocking down load-bearing walls nobody agreed to lose.

The warning is aimed at one exact moment: when the process stops feeling manageable, which is precisely when most people assume something's gone wrong and try to negotiate back down to the smaller original request. Discomfort at that stage is not a sign the plan failed. It's evidence the renovation is actually underway.
